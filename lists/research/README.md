# Awesome Research [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Scholarly search, AI-assisted discovery, citation mapping, evidence synthesis, reference management, legal access, repositories, reproducibility, and research integrity.

Part of [akashic](../../README.md), the Ego Hygiene knowledge collection.

Free search does not necessarily mean free full text. Verify AI-generated synthesis against the underlying papers, methods, corrections, and retractions—especially for consequential decisions.

## Scholarly Search and Metadata

- [BASE](https://www.base-search.net/) - Searches academic repositories and web resources through a free Bielefeld University service with open-access filters.
- [CORE](https://core.ac.uk/) - Aggregates open-access papers and full text from repositories and journals through a free nonprofit service.
- [Crossref Metadata Search](https://search.crossref.org/) - Searches open DOI records and bibliographic metadata supplied by scholarly publishers and repositories.
- [Dimensions](https://app.dimensions.ai/discover/publication) - Connects publications, datasets, grants, trials, patents, policy documents, and researchers; advanced analytics are paid.
- [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/) - Searches articles, theses, books, court opinions, citations, authors, and scholarly profiles across disciplines for free.
- [OpenAIRE Explore](https://explore.openaire.eu/) - Connects publications, datasets, software, projects, organizations, funders, and communities through open European infrastructure.
- [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org/) - Catalogs works, authors, institutions, venues, funders, topics, and citations through a free interface, API, and snapshots.
- [ScienceOpen](https://www.scienceopen.com/) - Supports free publication search, citation exploration, collections, author profiles, and post-publication discussion.
- [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/) - Provides free AI-assisted search with recommendations, citation context, summaries, influential-citation signals, and alerts.
- [The Lens](https://www.lens.org/) - Connects scholarly works, patents, citations, biological sequences, institutions, and technology landscapes through a freemium service.

## Field-Specific Sources and Registries

- [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/) - Provides a free United States government registry and results database for clinical studies conducted worldwide.
- [dblp computer science bibliography](https://dblp.org/) - Curates open bibliographic records for computer-science journals, conferences, authors, and venues.
- [ERIC](https://eric.ed.gov/) - Indexes education research, reports, journal articles, and policy literature through the United States Department of Education.
- [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/) - Searches life-science articles, preprints, grants, patents, guidelines, citations, and available full text for free.
- [NASA Astrophysics Data System](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/) - Provides free literature and citation-network discovery for astronomy, physics, planetary science, and related fields.
- [Patterns](https://www.cell.com/patterns/home) - Open-access Cell Press journal publishing data-science research, resources, perspectives, and cross-disciplinary applications.
- [PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/) - Maintains a free philosophy bibliography, repository, reading lists, author pages, and subject classifications.
- [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) - Searches biomedical and life-science citations, abstracts, and linked full text through the National Library of Medicine.
- [SciELO](https://www.scielo.org/) - Publishes and indexes open-access research with an emphasis on Latin America, Iberia, and the Global South.

## AI-Assisted Discovery and Understanding

- [Consensus](https://consensus.app/) - Answers natural-language questions from scholarly literature with linked evidence, study snapshots, and freemium review tools.
- [Edison Playground](https://platform.edisonscientific.com/) - Provides literature, precedent-search, and analysis agents with a limited free allowance.
- [Elicit](https://elicit.com/) - Finds papers, builds evidence tables, extracts study details, screens literature, and produces cited reports through a freemium workspace.
- [Explainpaper](https://www.explainpaper.com/) - Explains highlighted passages in uploaded papers and supports contextual follow-up questions through a freemium service.
- [Keenious](https://keenious.com/) - Recommends academic works from text or questions and helps explain literature across languages and disciplines.
- [Scholarcy](https://www.scholarcy.com/) - Converts papers into structured summaries and supports flashcards, literature matrices, notes, and bibliography exports.
- [Scite](https://scite.ai/) - Uses paid Smart Citations to show whether later papers support, contrast, or merely mention a result; a trial is available.
- [SciSpace](https://scispace.com/) - Combines literature search, PDF questions, extraction tables, review assistance, citations, and drafting through a freemium workspace.
- [Undermind](https://www.undermind.ai/) - Follows citation trails, analyzes full text, builds reports, and monitors topics through a freemium agentic search service.

## Citation Graphs and Research Mapping

- [bibliometrix](https://www.bibliometrix.org/) - Provides an open-source R package and Biblioshiny interface for bibliometric analysis and science mapping.
- [Connected Papers](https://www.connectedpapers.com/) - Generates freemium visual similarity graphs around a seed paper to surface prior and derivative work.
- [Litmaps](https://www.litmaps.com/) - Builds interactive citation maps and can alert users when relevant papers enter a mapped area.
- [Open Knowledge Maps](https://openknowledgemaps.org/) - Creates free, open-source visual topic maps from scholarly search results to expose clusters and themes.
- [Open Research Knowledge Graph](https://orkg.org/) - Represents research contributions as structured, open comparisons rather than relying only on narrative papers.
- [ResearchRabbit](https://www.researchrabbit.ai/) - Supports iterative paper and author discovery, collections, collaboration, citation exploration, and integrity signals.
- [VOSviewer](https://www.vosviewer.com/) - Creates free bibliometric networks of publications, authors, institutions, terms, citations, and co-authorship.

## Systematic Reviews and Evidence Synthesis

- [ASReview LAB](https://asreview.nl/) - Uses open-source active learning to prioritize records during screening while preserving researcher decisions.
- [CADIMA](https://www.cadima.info/) - Supports collaborative systematic reviews and evidence maps across disciplines through a free web tool.
- [Covidence](https://www.covidence.org/) - Manages deduplication, screening, extraction, risk-of-bias assessment, and review teams through a paid service with a trial.
- [EPPI-Reviewer](https://eppi.ioe.ac.uk/cms/er4/) - Supports screening, coding, text mining, evidence maps, reviews, and meta-analysis through a paid platform with a trial.
- [Nested Knowledge](https://about.nested-knowledge.com/) - Builds freemium living reviews with screening, extraction, qualitative synthesis, meta-analysis, and interactive evidence views.
- [PROSPERO](https://www.crd.york.ac.uk/prospero/) - Registers systematic-review protocols prospectively for free to expose ongoing work and reduce unintended duplication.
- [Rayyan](https://www.rayyan.ai/) - Supports freemium deduplication, blinded screening, collaboration, exclusion reasons, and AI-assisted relevance ranking.
- [RevMan Web](https://www.cochrane.org/products-and-services/review-writing-software) - Produces intervention reviews, meta-analysis, forest plots, and evidence tables; access is paid or free for eligible users.
- [revtools](https://revtools.net/) - Provides an open-source R package for importing, deduplicating, visualizing, and interactively screening bibliographic records.

## Reference Management

- [Better BibTeX for Zotero](https://retorque.re/zotero-better-bibtex/) - Adds stable citation keys, automatic exports, and advanced BibTeX and BibLaTeX workflows for free.
- [EndNote](https://endnote.com/) - Manages large libraries, full text, collaboration, and manuscript citations through a paid desktop and web suite with a trial.
- [JabRef](https://www.jabref.org/) - Provides a free, open-source, cross-platform BibTeX and BibLaTeX manager for LaTeX-centered workflows.
- [Mendeley Reference Manager](https://www.mendeley.com/reference-management/reference-manager) - Organizes and annotates literature, creates citations, syncs libraries, and includes discovery through a free proprietary service.
- [Paperpile](https://paperpile.com/) - Integrates paid reference management with Google Docs, Drive, browsers, and collaborative writing; a trial is available.
- [ReadCube Papers](https://www.papersapp.com/) - Combines discovery, annotation, recommendations, citation formatting, and shared libraries through a paid service with a trial.
- [Zotero](https://www.zotero.org/) - Captures sources, stores and annotates PDFs, manages shared libraries, and generates citations through free open-source software.
- [ZoteroBib](https://zbib.org/) - Creates bibliographies for free without requiring an installation or account.

## Legal Full-Text and Open-Access Tools

- [CORE Discovery](https://core.ac.uk/services/discovery) - Locates accessible paper copies through a free browser and repository plugin, including some records without DOIs.
- [Directory of Open Access Books](https://www.doabooks.org/) - Indexes peer-reviewed scholarly books published under open-access licenses through a free directory.
- [Directory of Open Access Journals](https://doaj.org/) - Curates peer-reviewed open-access journals and articles that meet published transparency criteria.
- [EndNote Click](https://click.endnote.com/) - Searches open-access sources and available institutional subscriptions for full text through a free extension.
- [LibKey Nomad](https://libkeynomad.com/) - Surfaces open copies and library-entitled full text through a free extension; subscription access depends on the user's institution.
- [OAPEN Library](https://library.oapen.org/) - Hosts quality-controlled open-access academic books and chapters in a free repository.
- [PubMed Central](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) - Archives full-text biomedical and life-science literature through the National Library of Medicine.
- [Unpaywall](https://unpaywall.org/) - Locates legal open-access article versions by DOI through a free browser extension and open database.

## Preprints and General Repositories

- [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/) - Hosts free preprints in physics, mathematics, computer science, statistics, economics, and related fields.
- [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/) - Hosts screened biology preprints that have not necessarily undergone peer review.
- [ChemRxiv](https://chemrxiv.org/) - Hosts chemistry and adjacent-field preprints with support from international chemical societies.
- [EarthArXiv](https://eartharxiv.org/) - Hosts community preprints in Earth, environmental, planetary, and related sciences.
- [HAL](https://hal.science/) - Preserves papers, theses, reports, preprints, and institutional collections in a French national repository.
- [medRxiv](https://www.medrxiv.org/) - Hosts screened health-science preprints with version history and prominent non-peer-review warnings.
- [PsyArXiv](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv) - Hosts community-run psychology preprints on Open Science Framework infrastructure.
- [SocArXiv](https://osf.io/preprints/socarxiv) - Hosts community-run social-science preprints on Open Science Framework infrastructure.
- [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/) - Assigns DOIs to publications, software, datasets, posters, and other outputs through a free CERN-operated repository.

## Dataset Discovery and Repositories

- [Berlin Open Data](https://daten.berlin.de/) - Official portal for discovering and reusing open datasets published by Berlin's government and public institutions.
- [DataCite Commons](https://commons.datacite.org/) - Explores the open persistent-identifier graph connecting datasets, software, publications, people, organizations, and repositories.
- [DataONE Search](https://search.dataone.org/data) - Provides free federated discovery for Earth, environmental, ecological, and related research data.
- [datos.gob.mx](https://www.datos.gob.mx/) - Official Mexican open-data portal for discovering, downloading, and reusing datasets from federal public institutions.
- [Dryad](https://datadryad.org/stash) - Publishes curated reusable data with persistent identifiers; discovery is free while deposits may cost.
- [Figshare](https://figshare.com/) - Publishes datasets, figures, posters, code, presentations, and other outputs with DOIs through a freemium repository.
- [Google Dataset Search](https://datasetsearch.research.google.com/) - Searches dataset metadata across government, academic, nonprofit, and commercial repositories for free.
- [Harvard Dataverse](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/) - Publishes, cites, preserves, versions, and discovers research data through a free general repository.
- [ICPSR](https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/) - Archives social and behavioral data, documentation, teaching datasets, and restricted-data services with mixed access.
- [opendata.swiss](https://opendata.swiss/en) - Central Swiss government catalog for open datasets from federal, cantonal, municipal, and other public organizations.
- [OpenML](https://www.openml.org/) - Open platform for sharing datasets, machine-learning tasks, runs, models, benchmarks, metadata, and reproducible experiments.
- [re3data](https://www.re3data.org/) - Describes research-data repositories, policies, licenses, certifications, identifiers, and disciplinary coverage through an open registry.

## Protocols, Notebooks, and Reproducibility

- [Binder](https://mybinder.org/) - Launches temporary reproducible computing environments from public version-controlled repositories through a free service.
- [eLabFTW](https://www.elabftw.net/) - Provides a self-hostable electronic lab notebook with experiments, templates, inventory, timestamps, and team controls; managed hosting is paid.
- [ESMira](https://esmira.kl.ac.at/) - Free decentralized open-source platform for anonymous experience-sampling, ambulatory-assessment, and ecological-momentary-assessment studies.
- [Open Science Framework](https://osf.io/) - Organizes projects, files, collaborators, registrations, preregistrations, and preserved outputs through free open-source infrastructure.
- [Project Jupyter](https://jupyter.org/) - Combines code, prose, equations, data, and visualizations in free open-source notebooks and computational environments.
- [protocols.io](https://www.protocols.io/) - Creates, versions, discusses, and publicly shares stepwise laboratory and computational protocols through a freemium platform.
- [Quarto](https://quarto.org/) - Publishes reproducible articles, reports, websites, books, and presentations from notebooks and Markdown through open-source software.
- [ReproZip](https://www.reprozip.org/) - Captures computational dependencies and environments so analyses can be packaged and reproduced elsewhere.
- [Software Heritage](https://www.softwareheritage.org/) - Archives public source code and supplies persistent SWHIDs for preservation and citation through open infrastructure.

## Writing and Collaboration

- [Authorea](https://www.authorea.com/) - Supports collaborative scholarly authoring and preprints with data, code, citations, and versioned research artifacts.
- [Curvenote](https://curvenote.com/) - Produces collaborative computational manuscripts, scientific sites, notebooks, and interactive publications through open and commercial tools.
- [Manubot](https://manubot.org/) - Uses Git, Markdown, continuous integration, and automated citations for transparent collaborative manuscripts.
- [Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com/) - Provides a freemium collaborative LaTeX editor with templates, comments, history, previews, and submission integrations.
- [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/) - Supports profiles, publication discovery, collaboration, discussion, metrics, and author-mediated requests through a free commercial network.
- [Typst](https://typst.app/) - Combines an open-source scientific typesetting compiler with freemium collaborative hosting, templates, previews, and bibliography support.

## Identity, Integrity, Peer Review, and Alerts

- [JournalTOCs](https://www.journaltocs.ac.uk/) - Provides free individual table-of-contents alerts and RSS feeds across journals, with premium institutional services.
- [OpenCitations](https://opencitations.net/) - Publishes reusable CC0 citation data, bibliographic metadata, APIs, indexes, and software.
- [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/) - Provides an open-source platform for submissions, peer review, publishing, discussion, and open reports.
- [ORCID](https://orcid.org/) - Supplies free persistent researcher identifiers and portable records connecting people with affiliations, funding, works, and reviews.
- [PREreview](https://prereview.org/) - Supports requesting, writing, publishing, and discussing constructive preprint reviews through a free open-source community platform.
- [PubPeer](https://pubpeer.com/) - Hosts free nonprofit post-publication discussion and alerts readers when viewed papers have comments.
- [R Discovery](https://discovery.researcher.life/) - Combines freemium paper search, personalized feeds, reading lists, summaries, and new-publication alerts.
- [Research Organization Registry](https://ror.org/) - Supplies open CC0 identifiers and metadata for research and funding organizations through search, API, and data dumps.
- [Retraction Watch Database](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/retraction-watch/) - Provides free, regularly updated retraction metadata through Crossref's REST API and downloadable CSV data.
- [Think. Check. Submit.](https://thinkchecksubmit.org/) - Provides free checklists for assessing whether journals, book publishers, and other venues appear trustworthy.
