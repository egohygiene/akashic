# Awesome Spirituality, Religion, and Occult Knowledge [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A pluralistic, research-grounded guide to religious traditions, spiritual practice, sacred texts, material history, esotericism, community, care, and critical inquiry.

Part of [akashic](../../README.md), the Ego Hygiene knowledge collection.

<!-- site-advisory: Educational and comparative—not an endorsement of any doctrine, supernatural claim, organization, teacher, or treatment. This collection approaches religion, spirituality, and esoteric traditions through academic scholarship, primary texts, material history, community self-representation, and lived practice. Compare translations and interpretations, distinguish devotional claims from empirical evidence, and follow community-specific rules for sacred, secret, gender-restricted, seasonal, or otherwise sensitive knowledge.

## Academic Study and Religious Literacy

- [American Academy of Religion](https://aarweb.org/) - Maintains scholarly standards, teaching resources, grants, meetings, and an international research network; some benefits require membership.
- [Association of Religion Data Archives](https://www.thearda.com/) - Provides free surveys, maps, profiles, membership statistics, and downloadable datasets for quantitative research.
- [Bible Odyssey](https://bibleodyssey.scholasticahq.com/) - Publishes public-facing historical and literary Bible scholarship under the Society of Biblical Literature.
- [Database of Religious History](https://religiondatabase.org/) - Encodes expert-reviewed claims about religious groups, practices, and institutions alongside citations and qualitative notes; JavaScript is required.
- [Encyclopedia of Shinto](https://d-museum.kokugakuin.ac.jp/eos/) - Offers Kokugakuin University's English-language reference to Shinto history, concepts, institutions, rituals, and material culture.
- [Encyclopædia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/) - Publishes scholarly articles on Iranian history, languages, and cultures, including Zoroastrianism, Manichaeism, and related traditions.
- [International Association for the History of Religions](https://www.iahrweb.org/) - Coordinates global associations and congresses for historical, social, and comparative study of religion.
- [Oxford Research Encyclopedia of Religion](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/62249) - Collects peer-reviewed overview essays across traditions and methods; access is a mixture of open and subscription content.
- [Pew Research Center: Religion](https://www.pewresearch.org/topic/religion/) - Provides surveys, datasets, and demographic analysis of religion worldwide, with a focus on social patterns rather than theology.
- [Society of Biblical Literature](https://www.sbl-site.org/) - Supports critical study of biblical and related literature through publications, conferences, teaching resources, and professional networks.
- [UCLA Encyclopedia of Egyptology](https://uee.ucla.edu/) - Offers free peer-reviewed articles on ancient Egyptian history, religion, archaeology, texts, and material culture.

## Primary Texts and Tradition-Specific Libraries

Translations, commentaries, and textual boundaries reflect editorial or community choices. Check each item's provenance, edition, and license before reuse. See also the collection's existing entries for the [Internet Sacred Text Archive](../awesome-abundance/books-knowledge-and-archives/README.md#open-access-books-and-monographs) and [Perseus Digital Library](../awesome-abundance/books-knowledge-and-archives/README.md#open-access-books-and-monographs).

- [84000: Translating the Words of the Buddha](https://84000.co/) - Provides free translations of the Tibetan Buddhist canon with introductions, glossaries, and scholarly reading tools.
- [Altafsir.com](https://www.altafsir.com/) - Collects Qur'anic commentaries from multiple periods and schools through the Royal Aal al-Bayt Institute; translation coverage varies.
- [Bahá'í Reference Library](https://www.bahai.org/library/) - Presents authoritative Bahá'í writings and official translations in browsable and downloadable formats.
- [Buddhist Digital Resource Center](https://www.bdrc.io/) - Preserves millions of pages of Buddhist texts in Tibetan, Sanskrit, Pali, Chinese, and other languages; item rights vary.
- [CBETA Online](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/) - Searches and displays the Chinese Buddhist canon and related collections; much of the interface is in Chinese.
- [Chinese Text Project](https://ctext.org/) - Connects premodern Chinese works, scanned editions, dictionaries, and textual-analysis tools; independently verify its machine-generated translations.
- [GRETIL](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil.html) - Preserves electronic texts in Sanskrit and other Indian languages across Hindu, Buddhist, and Jain traditions; the legacy project is migrating to TextGrid.
- [Guru Granth Sahib Project](https://project.gurugranthsahib.io/) - Builds a collaborative SikhRI translation, transcreation, and commentary on the Guru Granth Sahib; the work remains in progress.
- [JAINpedia](https://jainpedia.org/) - Combines digitized Jain manuscript folios with contextual articles, timelines, and teaching material from the Institute of Jainology and partner collections.
- [Muktabodha Digital Library](https://muktabodha.org/digital-library/) - Preserves Sanskrit manuscripts and searchable e-texts, especially Śaiva, Śākta, Tantric, and Vedic works; formats and rights vary.
- [New Testament Virtual Manuscript Room](https://ntvmr.uni-muenster.de/) - Provides images, metadata, transcriptions, and comparison tools for Greek New Testament manuscripts; accounts enable saved work and annotations.
- [Quran.com](https://quran.com/) - Provides multilingual translations, recitations, tafsir, and study tools through the nonprofit Quran Foundation; it is a tradition-hosted resource.
- [Quranic Arabic Corpus](https://corpus.quran.com/) - Supplies word-by-word morphology, syntax trees, grammar, and an ontology for Qur'anic Arabic.
- [Sefaria](https://www.sefaria.org/) - Connects Jewish texts in Hebrew, Aramaic, and translation with commentaries and cross-references; licenses differ between editions.
- [SuttaCentral](https://suttacentral.net/) - Presents early Buddhist texts, translations, parallels, and terminology across Pali, Chinese, Sanskrit, and Tibetan collections.

## Historical Archives, Manuscripts, and Material Culture

Digital availability does not imply unrestricted reuse. Follow holding-institution rights statements, culturally sensitive access conditions, and manuscript-specific permissions.

- [British Library Digitised Manuscripts and Archives](https://www.bl.uk/collection/digitised-manuscripts-archives) - Surfaces manuscripts and archives from many regions and traditions; some services remain affected by the library's cyberattack recovery.
- [Cambridge Digital Library](https://cudl.lib.cam.ac.uk/) - Provides free access to manuscripts and rare books across Jewish, Islamic, Christian, Buddhist, Hindu, and Jain collections; item rights vary.
- [Codex Sinaiticus](https://www.codexsinaiticus.org/en/) - Reunites high-resolution images and transcriptions of the fourth-century biblical manuscript held by four partner institutions.
- [Coptic SCRIPTORIUM](https://copticscriptorium.org/) - Combines annotated Coptic corpora, dictionaries, linked data, and computational research tools.
- [Cuneiform Digital Library Initiative](https://cdli.earth/) - Aggregates images, transliterations, and metadata for cuneiform artifacts, including temple, ritual, mythological, and administrative records.
- [Digital Bodleian](https://digital.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/) - Opens Oxford manuscripts and rare books across alchemy, astrology, Hebrew, Arabic, Sanskrit, religion, and history.
- [Digital Scriptorium](https://digital-scriptorium.org/) - Serves as a union catalog for premodern manuscripts held in North American collections, with links to participating repositories.
- [Digital Vatican Library](https://digi.vatlib.it/) - Provides IIIF-enabled access to manuscripts, incunabula, archival materials, and other Vatican Library holdings; publication-quality reuse may require authorization.
- [Electronic Text Corpus of Sumerian Literature](https://etcsl.orinst.ox.ac.uk/) - Preserves transliterations and English translations of nearly 400 Sumerian literary compositions; the completed legacy corpus has not been updated since 2006.
- [Endangered Archives Programme](https://eap.bl.uk/) - Digitizes vulnerable archives worldwide while keeping originals in their countries of origin; access and reuse conditions vary by project.
- [Fihrist](https://www.fihrist.org.uk/) - Searches descriptions of Islamic manuscripts held by United Kingdom institutions; many records link outward rather than supplying full scans.
- [Heilbrunn Timeline of Art History](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/timeline-of-art-history) - Connects expert-authored essays and global chronologies to Metropolitan Museum objects, including extensive religious art and ritual material.
- [HMML Reading Room](https://www.vhmml.org/readingRoom) - Catalogs and displays endangered manuscript traditions across Africa, Europe, the Middle East, and South Asia; image access requires a free account.
- [International Dunhuang Programme](https://idp.bl.uk/) - Connects Silk Road manuscripts and artifacts dispersed among international collections, including Buddhist, Manichaean, Christian, and Zoroastrian materials.
- [Leon Levy Dead Sea Scrolls Digital Library](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive) - Provides searchable high-resolution and multispectral images of the Dead Sea Scrolls through the Israel Antiquities Authority.
- [Mesoweb](https://www.mesoweb.com/) - Publishes Mesoamerican archaeology reports, codex studies, journals, and epigraphic resources in English and Spanish; it is not an authority for living communities.
- [Open Context](https://opencontext.org/) - Publishes open archaeological datasets, maps, images, and field records with transparent project-level licenses.
- [OPenn](https://openn.library.upenn.edu/) - Publishes complete high-resolution manuscript images and machine-readable descriptions under public-domain or free-cultural licenses.
- [Papyri.info](https://papyri.info/) - Integrates editions, metadata, images, bibliography, and collaborative editing for ancient papyri; translations and images are unevenly available.
- [Princeton Geniza Project](https://geniza.princeton.edu/) - Searches transcriptions and descriptions of Cairo Geniza documents in Judaeo-Arabic, Hebrew, Arabic, and related languages.
- [Princeton Manuscripts of the Islamic World](https://digital-collections.princeton.edu/collections/islamicmss) - Provides digitized Arabic, Persian, and Ottoman Turkish manuscripts across religious, scientific, literary, and occult subjects.
- [Qatar Digital Library](https://www.qdl.qa/en) - Opens Gulf history and Arabic scientific manuscript collections created with the British Library; read colonial provenance and reuse terms critically.
- [Source Library](https://sourcelibrary.org/about) - Pairs historical scans with searchable machine translations and provenance links; use translations for discovery and verify them against scans and critical editions.
- [Syriaca.org](https://syriaca.org/) - Publishes open linked data on Syriac places, people, saints, authors, manuscripts, and bibliography.
- [UNESCO Memory of the World](https://www.unesco.org/en/memory-world) - Maintains a global documentary-heritage register and preservation resources; entries do not necessarily include complete digital objects.
- [Warburg Iconographic Database](https://iconographic.warburg.sas.ac.uk/home) - Searches images concerning mythology, religion, astrology, science, ritual, and cultural transmission; noncommercial reuse terms apply.

## Indigenous and Community-Governed Knowledge

“Indigenous spirituality” is not a single category. These resources are not permission to reproduce sacred, secret, gender-restricted, seasonal, or community-controlled knowledge; nation- and community-specific protocols supersede open-web availability.

- [African Online Digital Library](https://aodl.org/) - Connects oral histories and archives concerning African religious pluralism, West African Islam, Sufi communities, and regional cultural history.
- [AIATSIS Ethical Research](https://aiatsis.gov.au/research/ethical-research) - Provides guidance for meaningful engagement, reciprocity, and Indigenous-led research from Australia's national Indigenous research institute.
- [Digital Pasifik](https://digitalpasifik.org/) - Aggregates Pacific cultural heritage from many institutions while exposing source provenance; legacy metadata may contain colonial or outdated language.
- [First Nations Principles of OCAP](https://fnigc.ca/ocap-training/) - Explains First Nations ownership, control, access, and possession of data through a Canada-specific, community-governed framework.
- [Local Contexts Traditional Knowledge Labels](https://localcontexts.org/labels/traditional-knowledge-labels/) - Enables communities to communicate attribution, permission, sacred, secret, gender, seasonal, and other protocols for cultural knowledge.
- [Mukurtu CMS](https://mukurtu.org/) - Provides a free community-driven digital-heritage platform with granular cultural protocols and Traditional Knowledge Labels; access rules are set locally.
- [Native Knowledge 360°](https://americanindian.si.edu/nk360) - Offers Native-centered educational resources and histories from the Smithsonian National Museum of the American Indian.
- [PARADISEC](https://www.paradisec.org.au/) - Preserves endangered-language, oral-history, music, and ritual recordings from the Pacific and surrounding regions; community access restrictions must be honored.
- [Protocols for Native American Archival Materials](https://www2.archivists.org/standards/protocols-for-native-american-archival-materials) - Guides culturally responsive care, description, access, and repatriation while requiring interpretation with each sovereign community.
- [Sustainable Heritage Network](https://sustainableheritagenetwork.org/about) - Trains tribal archives, libraries, and museums in ethical digitization and preservation without disclosing community knowledge.
- [Te Ara: Traditional Māori Religion](https://teara.govt.nz/en/traditional-maori-religion-nga-karakia-a-te-maori) - Provides an attributed introduction to Māori religious concepts through New Zealand's national encyclopedia; it does not replace iwi or hapū authorities.

## Regional Traditions and Living Heritage

These sources illuminate particular regions and communities without flattening them into single belief systems. Museum and colonial records should be read alongside living-community interpretation and provenance research.

- [African and Diasporic Religious Studies Association](https://adrsa.org/) - Connects scholars and practitioners researching Africana religions; public programming is unevenly archived and intentionally includes practitioner perspectives.
- [African Association for the Study of Religions](https://www.a-asr.org/) - Supports Africa-centered scholarship on Indigenous religions, Islam, Christianity, diaspora traditions, and new movements.
- [African Cosmos: Stellar Arts](https://africa.si.edu/exhibitions/african-cosmos-stellar-arts) - Explores cosmology, divination, ancestors, and sacred arts across selected African traditions through Smithsonian museum interpretation.
- [Association for the Study of Religion in Southern Africa](https://asrsa.org/) - Coordinates independent, non-confessional academic study of religion in Southern Africa.
- [Bishop Museum Ethnology Database](https://data.bishopmuseum.org/ethnologydb/index.php) - Searches Hawaiian and Pacific cultural objects; only part of the collection is illustrated and collecting histories require scrutiny.
- [Códice Maya de México](https://www.codicemayademexico.inah.gob.mx/) - Presents INAH research on a pre-Hispanic Maya Venus divinatory almanac, its cosmology, materials, provenance, and authentication; the site is primarily Spanish.
- [Digital Florentine Codex](https://florentinecodex.getty.edu/en/) - Opens a searchable Nahuatl–Spanish manuscript and contextual essays on sixteenth-century Indigenous Mexico while acknowledging its colonial co-production.
- [Digital Library of the Caribbean](https://dloc.com/) - Provides a multilingual, shared-governance library of Caribbean archives; rights, digitization depth, and historical terminology vary by collection.
- [FAMSI Mesoamerican Codices](https://www.famsi.org/mayawriting/codices/) - Offers LACMA-stewarded facsimiles and scholarly introductions to Maya, Mexica, and Mixtec codices; consult modern Indigenous interpretation separately.
- [Hemispheric Institute Digital Video Library](https://hemisphericinstitute.org/en/hidvl.html) - Preserves performance practices across the Americas, including ritual, Indigenous, Afro-diasporic, and decolonial work; videos remain copyrighted.
- [Japanese Journal of Religious Studies](https://nirc.nanzan-u.ac.jp/journal/6) - Publishes open-access peer-reviewed research on Japanese religions through Nanzan University.
- [KOSANBA](https://haitianstudies.ucsb.edu/index.php/kosanba/about) - Supports community-engaged scholarly study of Haitian Vodou and culture through a specialized UCSB-based association.
- [Matariki for Teachers](https://www.tepapa.govt.nz/learn/for-educators/teaching-resources/matariki-for-teachers/getting-started/how-use-resource) - Shares Te Papa curriculum resources on Matariki knowledge, remembrance, ceremony, and place-based learning credited to Māori knowledge holders.
- [Papakilo Database](https://www.papakilodatabase.com/) - Federates Hawaiian genealogy, newspapers, oral histories, and ethnological records through the Office of Hawaiian Affairs; mixed provenance and rights require care.
- [SAT Daizōkyō Text Database](https://21dzk.l.u-tokyo.ac.jp/SAT/) - Provides a University of Tokyo research interface for the Taishō Buddhist canon and related text-critical tools, with limited English guidance.
- [Ulukau: The Hawaiian Electronic Library](https://ulukau.org/) - Opens Hawaiian-language newspapers, dictionaries, books, music, and cultural reference works; historical sources may reflect missionary or colonial framing.

## Mysticism, Contemplative Practice, and Meditation

- [Belur Math and Ramakrishna Mission](https://belurmath.org/) - Offers Vedanta teachings, media, and a worldwide branch directory through an official monastic network; retreat and local-program costs vary.
- [Center for Action and Contemplation](https://cac.org/) - Shares Christian contemplative teaching through free daily meditations and podcasts alongside paid courses and programs.
- [Contemplative Outreach](https://www.contemplativeoutreach.org/) - Provides Centering Prayer instruction, groups, and a free app within a Christian framework; retreats and some programs are paid.
- [Insight Meditation Society](https://www.dharma.org/) - Runs residential Theravāda and Insight meditation retreats with published fees and financial-aid options.
- [Institute for Jewish Spirituality](https://www.jewishspirituality.org/) - Teaches Jewish meditation, text study, and embodied practice through free introductory resources plus paid courses and retreats.
- [Plum Village](https://plumvillage.org/) - Offers extensive free teachings and paid retreats through a global engaged-Buddhist community, with some scholarship support.
- [Sikh Research Institute](https://sikhri.org/) - Publishes Sikh articles, podcasts, videos, and courses; much is free while structured programs may charge fees.
- [Spirit Rock Meditation Center](https://www.spiritrock.org/) - Offers Insight meditation retreats and classes on fee or donation models, with scholarships and accessibility information.
- [The Inayatiyya](https://inayatiyya.org/) - Shares Sufi teaching, a center directory, and paid courses or retreats from within a specific lineage.
- [UCLA Mindful](https://www.uclahealth.org/uclamindful) - Provides university-based guided meditations and a free app, supplemented by paid classes and training.
- [World Community for Christian Meditation](https://wccm.org/meditate/) - Provides Christian meditation instruction and local or online groups through free materials plus member-supported and paid programs.

## Western Esotericism and Occult History

Resources in this section study esoteric movements, texts, and practices historically and critically. Historical availability is not evidence that a supernatural, predictive, or medical claim is true.

- [Bibliotheca Philosophica Hermetica Collection](https://www.embassyofthefreemind.com/collection) - Preserves Hermeticism, alchemy, Rosicrucianism, mysticism, and Christian Kabbalah through the Embassy of the Free Mind; digital and onsite access vary.
- [Cambridge Society for Psychical Research Collection](https://www.lib.cam.ac.uk/collections/departments/archives-modern-and-medieval-manuscripts-and-university-archives-0) - Preserves correspondence, investigation records, and books documenting Spiritualism and psychical research without validating the recorded claims.
- [Chymistry of Isaac Newton](https://newton.dlib.indiana.edu/) - Supplies manuscript scans, scholarly transcriptions, and contextual editions of Newton's alchemical writings.
- [Cornell Witchcraft Collection](https://digital.library.cornell.edu/collections/witchcraft) - Digitizes historical books concerning European witchcraft beliefs, persecution, trials, and demonology.
- [Corpus of Coptic Magical Formularies](https://www.coptic-magic.phil.uni-wuerzburg.de/) - Documents Coptic-language magical texts through a university project; treat formulas as historical evidence rather than instructions.
- [DACALBO](https://dacalbo.hpdst.gr/) - Opens a scholarly digital archive of Byzantine and post-Byzantine Greek alchemical manuscripts.
- [Drew University Spiritualism Pamphlets](https://digitalcollections.drew.edu/SpecialCollections/19thCenturyPamphlets/Spiritualism/) - Digitizes nineteenth-century primary sources both advocating and contesting Spiritualism.
- [Harry Price Library of Magical Literature](https://www.london.ac.uk/about/services/senate-house-library/collections/printed-special-collections/harry-price-library-magical-literature) - Preserves books and archives on stage magic, witchcraft, Spiritualism, psychical research, and extraordinary claims.
- [International Association for the Preservation of Spiritualist and Occult Periodicals](https://iapsop.com/) - Makes historical pages on Spiritualism, Theosophy, New Thought, and occult movements searchable; it is independently curated and not an endorsement.
- [International Playing-Card Society](https://i-p-c-s.org/) - Documents playing-card and tarot history through a nonprofit specialist society, journal, pattern sheets, and historical essays.
- [Koestler Parapsychology Library](https://ppls.ed.ac.uk/psychology/research/facilities/philosophy-and-psychology-library/koestler-parapsychology-library) - Supports historical and psychological research into psychical claims, anomalous experiences, skepticism, and “pseudo-psi” at the University of Edinburgh.
- [Ktiv](https://www.nli.org.il/en/discover/manuscripts/hebrew-manuscripts) - Aggregates catalog records and digitized Hebrew manuscripts from collections worldwide; image access and reuse rights vary by holder.
- [Making and Knowing Project](https://www.makingandknowing.org/) - Reconstructs early modern artisanal and natural knowledge around a Columbia University digital critical edition.
- [Museum of Witchcraft and Magic Collections](https://museumofwitchcraftandmagic.co.uk/collections/) - Provides object and library records covering British magic, folk practice, modern Paganism, and their material histories.
- [Nag Hammadi Archive](https://ccdl.claremont.edu/digital/collection/nha) - Preserves scholarly photographs and documentation concerning the Coptic codices through Claremont Colleges Digital Library.
- [Salem Witch Trials Documentary Archive](https://salem.lib.virginia.edu/home.html) - Connects transcribed court records, maps, images, and contextual material through the University of Virginia.
- [Science History Institute Alchemy Collection](https://digital.sciencehistory.org/focus/alchemy) - Brings together digitized alchemical manuscripts, rare books, images, and material objects.
- [Survey of Scottish Witchcraft](https://witches.hca.ed.ac.uk/home/) - Documents people accused of witchcraft in early modern Scotland using a University of Edinburgh research database.
- [The Zohar at the National Library of Israel](https://www.nli.org.il/en/discover/judaism/jewish-bookshelf/zohar) - Curates manuscripts, historic editions, commentaries, studies, and recordings around the Zohar.
- [TheDefix](https://www.thedefix.uni-hamburg.de/) - Catalogs ancient curse tablets with original texts, physical descriptions, translations, and bibliography through the University of Hamburg.
- [Trismegistos](https://www.trismegistos.org/) - Connects metadata for papyri, inscriptions, manuscripts, people, places, and ancient magical texts across specialist databases.
- [Visconti Tarot at Yale](https://beinecke.library.yale.edu/collections/highlights/visconti-tarot) - Presents a fifteenth-century painted card group with context separating its early game use from later divination.
- [Warburg Digital Library](https://wdl.warburg.sas.ac.uk/home) - Organizes digitized scholarship and primary sources into collections including Astrology and Astronomy and Magic and Science.

## Mythology, Folklore, and Vernacular Religion

Folklore archives preserve living and historical cultural expression; item visibility does not erase community ownership, context, or restrictions.

- [American Folklore Society](https://americanfolkloresociety.org/) - Connects professional folklorists, publications, conferences, public programs, and ethics guidance.
- [Dúchas](https://www.duchas.ie/en) - Opens manuscripts, field recordings, photographs, and indexes from Ireland's National Folklore Collection.
- [Folklore Society](https://www.folklore-society.com/) - Supports historical and contemporary folklore research through a longstanding scholarly society and journal.
- [Library of Congress American Folklife Center](https://www.loc.gov/research-centers/american-folklife-center/about-this-research-center/) - Preserves ethnographic recordings, field collections, community documentation, and cultural traditions from the United States and beyond.
- [Oral Tradition](https://oraltradition.org/) - Publishes open-access interdisciplinary scholarship on oral traditions across languages, cultures, and historical periods.
- [Smithsonian Center for Folklife and Cultural Heritage](https://folklife.si.edu/) - Works with communities on living cultural heritage, archives, education, language sustainability, and public interpretation.

## Academic Centers, Journals, and Research Networks

- [Amsterdam Hermetica](https://hermetica.uva.nl/) - Supports critical historical and empirical study of esotericism at the University of Amsterdam, spanning antiquity through the present.
- [AAR Religion and Disability Studies Unit](https://papers.aarweb.org/group/15622) - Provides an academic forum for disability, crip theory, lived religion, theology, access, and disabled religious experience.
- [Aries](https://brill.com/view/journals/arie/arie-overview.xml) - Publishes peer-reviewed research on Western esotericism; subscription and open-access availability varies by article.
- [Correspondences](https://correspondencesjournal.com/) - Offers open-access, peer-reviewed, non-advocative scholarship on esotericism.
- [Critical Research on Religion](https://journals.sagepub.com/home/crr) - Applies critical theory, feminism, postcolonialism, queer theory, and ideology critique to religion; most articles are paywalled.
- [Culture and Cosmos](https://www.cultureandcosmos.org/) - Publishes peer-reviewed open-access work on astrology, cultural astronomy, and their historical settings.
- [Collaborative on Faith and Disability](https://faithanddisability.org/) - Connects university-affiliated research, disability advocacy, inclusive practice, and an interfaith institute on theology and disability.
- [European Society for the Study of Western Esotericism](https://www.esswe.org/) - Connects scholars through conferences, regional networks, prizes, publications, and doctoral support.
- [Gnosis: Journal of Gnostic Studies](https://brill.com/view/journals/gnos/gnos-overview.xml) - Covers Gnostic movements, texts, reception, and adjacent late-antique traditions through peer-reviewed scholarship.
- [Magic, Ritual, and Witchcraft](https://www.pennpress.org/journals/journal/magic-ritual-and-witchcraft/) - Examines magical beliefs and practices globally through reviewed historical and interdisciplinary research.
- [MOSAIC: Mapping Occult Sciences Across Islamicate Cultures](https://www.mosaicsynergy.net/) - Maps occult-science texts and exchanges across Greek, Syriac, Arabic, Persian, and Turkish intellectual traditions through an ERC-funded project.
- [Nova Religio](https://online.ucpress.edu/nr) - Studies emergent, alternative, and minority religions through peer-reviewed religious-studies research.
- [International Society for the Study of New Religions](https://www.issnr.org/about/) - Promotes interdisciplinary, non-confessional research on new religiosity through a scholarly professional network.
- [Journal of Feminist Studies in Religion](https://www.fsrinc.org/about-jfsr/) - Publishes interreligious scholarship accountable to feminist movements and academic religious studies; current articles generally require subscription access.
- [LGBTQ Religious Archives Network](https://lgbtqreligiousarchives.org/) - Documents LGBTQ+ religious movements and justice work through profiles, oral histories, exhibitions, and an archival catalog with collection-specific access.
- [New Religiosity and the Digital Study of Eudaimonia](https://www.newreligiosity.org/) - Develops an open dataset on post-1970 religious movements through an ongoing university partnership; outputs remain incomplete.
- [Religion and Gender](https://www.openhumanitiespress.org/journals/titles/religion-and-gender/) - Publishes open-access scholarship connecting religion with feminist, queer, sexuality, masculinity, and diversity studies.
- [Shlomo Moussaieff Center for Kabbalah Research](https://cris.biu.ac.il/en/organisations/the-shlomo-moussaieff-center-for-kabbalah-research/) - Produces critical editions, digital corpora, handbooks, and academic teaching through Bar-Ilan University.
- [Societas Magica](https://societasmagica.org/) - Supports scholarly research into magic's history and social contexts through publications, conferences, and research grants.
- [Sophia Centre](https://www.sophia-project.net/) - Studies astrology and cultural astronomy as historical and cultural phenomena rather than commercial divination services.

- [University of Exeter Centre for Magic and Esotericism](https://sites.exeter.ac.uk/magic/) - Coordinates interdisciplinary research on magic and esotericism across periods, languages, and cultures.

- [Humanist Chaplaincy at Harvard and MIT](https://www.humanistchaplaincy.org/) - Provides a campus-based model for secular reflection, counseling, ethical reasoning, community, and life celebration.
- [Humanists International](https://humanists.international/about/) - Represents humanist, atheist, rationalist, freethought, and Ethical Culture groups globally as an explicit advocacy organization.
- [JAINA Centers Directory](https://jaincenters.jaina.org/) - Lists Jain centers and organizations across North America.
- [Pagan Federation](https://www.paganfed.org/) - Provides community, education, and anti-discrimination support from an established Pagan insider organization rather than a scholarly authority.

## Spiritual Direction, Chaplaincy, and Care

Spiritual directors, chaplains, meditation teachers, and peer supporters are not mental-health clinicians unless separately licensed.

- [ACPE](https://acpe.edu/) - Accredits Clinical Pastoral Education and educator certification; public standards are available while programs charge institution-specific tuition.
- [Association of Muslim Chaplains](https://www.associationofmuslimchaplains.org/) - Maintains a North American professional network with public program information and member-only resources; dues and event fees apply.
- [Association of Professional Chaplains](https://www.apchaplains.org/) - Provides professional standards, continuing education, and board certification; much training and certification access is paid.
- [Chaplaincy Innovation Lab](https://chaplaincyinnovation.org/) - Offers research-driven working papers, webinars, and selected books on spiritual care, with additional subscriber or program content.
- [Humanist Society Directory](https://thehumanistsociety.org/directory/) - Lists endorsed humanist chaplains, celebrants, and lay leaders; individual fees vary and endorsement may not satisfy every employer's requirements.
- [Neshama: Association of Jewish Chaplains](https://najc.org/) - Provides Jewish chaplaincy standards, certification, education, and networking; membership and certification are paid.
- [SDI Ethical Guidelines](https://www.sdicompanions.org/media/guidelines-for-ethical-conduct/) - Provides a free ethical framework for spiritual companions and directors, with paid print copies available.
- [SDI: Finding a Spiritual Companion](https://www.sdicompanions.org/need-help-finding/) - Offers search and interview guidance for locating a spiritual director; SDI does not certify or independently verify directory members.
- [Spiritual Care Association](https://www.spiritualcareassociation.org/) - Provides evidence-informed quality indicators, education, and certification; some public resources are free but most training and membership are paid.

## Pilgrimage and Sacred Places

Sacred places require custodian consent, privacy, and cultural protocol. A public map or heritage designation does not imply unrestricted access.

- [Nusuk Umrah](https://umrah.nusuk.sa/) - Provides official Saudi guidance for Umrah planning, visas, and approved packages; travel is paid and eligibility rules can change.
- [Pilgrim Reception Office](https://oficinadelperegrino.com/en/) - Provides official Santiago Cathedral guidance on Camino credentials and the Compostela; credentials, lodging, and travel have separate costs.
- [Sacred Natural Sites Initiative](https://sacrednaturalsites.org/) - Shares custodian-led case studies and conservation guidance for sacred landscapes; it is not a tourism checklist or permission to visit restricted places.
- [Shikoku Henro](https://shikoku-tourism.com/en/shikoku-henro) - Provides an official regional guide to the 88-temple pilgrimage; cultural information is free while travel costs remain the visitor's responsibility.
- [UNESCO World Heritage List](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/) - Catalogs many sacred sites and cultural landscapes; inscription does not imply unrestricted visitor access.

## Safety, Ethics, and Critical Inquiry

“Cult” and “high-control group” are contested terms. Evaluate conduct and evidence rather than unfamiliar belief alone, and avoid unsourced blacklists. These services are not substitutes for emergency or clinical care.

- [Anomalistic Psychology at Goldsmiths](https://www.gold.ac.uk/psychology/staff/french/) - Surveys university research into cognitive and psychological explanations for ostensibly paranormal experiences, magical thinking, and conspiracy beliefs.
- [Brown University CLANlab](https://sites.brown.edu/britton/) - Publishes university research and tools on meditation experiences, including adverse effects and contemplative-practice safety.
- [Calling Bullshit](https://callingbullshit.org/) - Provides University of Washington course materials for evaluating statistical, visual, computational, and scholarly-looking claims.
- [Cheetah House](https://www.cheetahhouse.org/) - Offers evidence-informed education and support for meditation-related distress, spiritual abuse, and trauma; consultations and training may cost.
- [Cochrane Complementary Medicine](https://cam.cochrane.org/evidence) - Curates systematic-review evidence on complementary and integrative practices; summaries are often open while full reviews may require access.
- [Family Survival Trust Support](https://thefamilysurvivaltrust.org/support/) - Provides UK-based psychoeducational peer support for people affected by coercive or high-control groups; it is not therapy or crisis care.
- [GRACE](https://www.netgrace.org/) - Provides Christian-context abuse prevention, safeguarding, and institutional-response resources; training and consulting are paid.
- [International Cultic Studies Association](https://internationalculticstudies.org/) - Supports research, education, and recovery concerning coercive and high-control groups; some resources and services are paid.
- [INFORM](https://inform.ac/) - Supplies evidence-oriented information and an archive concerning new and minority religions while treating its group database as provisional.
- [NCCIH: Considering a Complementary Health Approach](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/are-you-considering-a-complementary-health-approach) - Offers evidence and safety questions for evaluating complementary health claims without delaying conventional care.
- [NCCIH Know the Science](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/providers/digest/know-the-science-of-complementary-health-approaches) - Provides an NIH toolkit for evaluating evidence, placebo effects, supplement interactions, “natural” claims, and health misinformation.
- [NCTSN: Trauma, Spirituality, and Provider Care](https://www.nctsn.org/resources/trauma-spirituality-and-provider-care) - Provides trauma-informed material through the National Child Traumatic Stress Network; account registration may be required.
- [OHCHR Report on Witchcraft Accusations and Ritual Attacks](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4004272) - Documents human-rights harms associated with witchcraft accusations and ritual attacks as protection issues rather than supernatural evidence.
- [Recovering from Religion](https://www.recoveringfromreligion.org/) - Offers a free peer hotline, chat, and support groups for people questioning or leaving religion; it is not emergency or clinical care.
- [Secular Therapy Project](https://www.recoveringfromreligion.org/seculartherapy) - Provides free directory access to screened secular, licensed therapists; costs vary and users should verify local licensure and insurance.
- [Skeptical Inquirer](https://skepticalinquirer.org/) - Examines paranormal, occult, pseudoscientific, and testable religious claims from a science-skeptic advocacy perspective rather than uniformly peer-reviewed scholarship.
- [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Religious Experience](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/religious-experience/) - Reviews philosophical arguments about mystical experience, testimony, perception, disagreement, and epistemic justification.
- [Violence Free Faith](https://violencefreefaith.org/) - Offers survivor-centered interfaith education, policy, and coaching for preventing abuse in faith communities; service pricing varies.
