# Awesome Work and Learning [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Job discovery, research opportunities, open-source career tools, public support, creator operations, learning resources, writing, and workspace tools.

Part of [akashic](../../README.md), the Ego Hygiene knowledge collection.

> **Creator-operations note:** Platform features, APIs, and prices change frequently. Never give an agency or collaborator your password, two-factor codes, payout access, or recovery keys; use scoped or delegated access where available. Monitoring and takedown services cannot guarantee removal or determine that a use is unlawful. Preserve original files and permissions, and remember that a DMCA notice is a legal declaration that may expose contact information—use qualified legal help for disputed or high-stakes cases.

> **Job-search safety note:** Confirm every opening on the employer's own careers page before sharing personal information. A legitimate employer does not charge an applicant for a job, send a check to buy equipment, or need bank, tax, or identity documents before verified onboarding. "Remote" does not necessarily mean work from any country; check residence, work-authorization, time-zone, travel, contract, compensation, clearance, and on-call requirements.

> **Automation note:** Prefer official APIs, feeds, alerts, and direct employer pages. Respect site terms, robots controls, rate limits, and login boundaries; do not bypass CAPTCHAs or access controls, evade blocking, or mass-submit applications. Treat generated matches and application materials as drafts, verify every factual claim, and keep a human approval step before applying or contacting anyone.

## Brand Monitoring and Content Rights

- [Brand24](https://brand24.com/) - Commercial web and social mention-monitoring platform with sentiment, reach, alerting, and reporting tools; the trial is time-limited and ongoing plans are paid.
- [Ceartas](https://www.ceartas.io/) - Paid creator-protection service for scanning and submitting search, social, impersonation, deepfake, and copyright removals; plans are costly and outcomes are not guaranteed.
- [Copyright Claims Board](https://ccb.gov/) - Official United States voluntary remote tribunal for certain copyright disputes seeking up to $30,000; filing fees apply, respondents may opt out, and its materials are not legal advice.
- [Google Alerts](https://www.google.com/alerts) - Free signed-in service for email or RSS alerts when Google finds new web, news, blog, video, book, or discussion results matching a name or keyword.
- [Lumen Database](https://lumendatabase.co/) - Independent research database collecting and analyzing legal removal requests, including copyright and privacy complaints; it does not provide individualized legal advice.
- [Rulta](https://www.rulta.com/) - Paid creator service for continuous piracy, impersonation, and repost monitoring plus takedown submissions across search, social, and creator platforms; results are not guaranteed.
- [Talkwalker Alerts](https://www.talkwalker.com/alerts) - Free email and RSS alerts for new web, news, blog, forum, and social mentions of a chosen name, brand, phrase, or URL.
- [TinEye](https://tineye.com/) - Reverse-image search engine for finding some reused or modified copies of an image; results are not exhaustive and commercial APIs are paid.
- [U.S. Copyright Office DMCA Agent Directory](https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/) - Official directory for locating a United States online service provider's designated copyright agent and reviewing notice requirements before submitting a takedown request.
- [Volunteer Lawyers for the Arts](https://vlany.org/) - New York nonprofit providing eligible artists with legal education, consultations, and pro bono or low-cost help; services are appointment-based and geographically limited.

## Career Platforms and Job Search

- [Built In Jobs](https://builtin.com/jobs) - Free technology-job search with company profiles, salary information, and role, location, experience, and work-model filters; coverage is strongest in United States technology markets.
- [Dice Jobs](https://www.dice.com/jobs) - Free technology-job board with permanent and contract roles across software, infrastructure, data, security, and enterprise IT; recruiter-heavy listings and duplicates require employer verification.
- [Greenhouse Candidate Portal](https://my.greenhouse.io/dashboard) - Candidate dashboard for tracking and managing job applications submitted through participating Greenhouse employers.
- [HiringCafe](https://hiringcafe.com/) - Searches jobs collected from employer career pages with unusually detailed filters for role, salary, experience, benefits, visa, and work environment.
- [Indeed Jobs](https://www.indeed.com/) - Free broad job search and alerts across industries, locations, and employment types; aggregated listings can be duplicated or stale, so apply through the verified employer page where possible.
- [Intch](https://futureofwork.intch.org/) - Professional networking and opportunity-matching platform organized around requests, introductions, jobs, and collaboration.
- [LinkedIn Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/) - Large job index with alerts and professional-network context; core search is free, Premium is paid, and profile visibility, recruiter identity, and the employer's canonical posting should be checked.
- [Product-Testing.com](https://product-testing.com/) - Market-research platform listing surveys, product samples, app tests, and focus-group opportunities for participants.
- [USAJOBS](https://www.usajobs.gov/) - Official United States federal employment portal for finding positions, understanding hiring paths, building profiles, and tracking applications.
- [Welcome to the Jungle Jobs](https://app.welcometothejungle.com/jobs) - Free technology and startup job matching with company, compensation, and work-environment context; a free account is required and coverage varies by geography.
- [Wellfound Jobs](https://wellfound.com/jobs) - Job marketplace focused on startup and technology roles, including remote opportunities and company profiles.
- [Work at a Startup](https://www.workatastartup.com/) - Y Combinator job platform for roles at startup companies across engineering, product, design, operations, and other functions.

## Remote and International Work

"Remote" listings can still require residence in a particular country, local payroll eligibility, overlap with specific hours, or occasional travel. For moving and worker-mobility planning, also see [Travel and Mobility](../travel-and-mobility/README.md), including its EURES living-and-working and mobility-support resources.

- [Devex Jobs](https://www.devex.com/jobs/search) - International-development vacancies across nonprofits, agencies, consulting firms, foundations, and multilateral organizations; some search and career features require a paid membership.
- [EU Blue Card](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/eu-blue-card_en) - Official European Commission overview and national links for qualifying non-EU workers; offer length, qualifications, salary threshold, and destination rules vary, and the route does not apply in Denmark or Ireland.
- [EU Careers](https://eu-careers.europa.eu/en) - Official portal for permanent, contract, temporary, specialist, and trainee roles across European Union institutions and agencies; citizenship, language, and selection requirements vary by route.
- [EURES](https://eures.europa.eu/) - Official European job-mobility portal with vacancies, advisers, job fairs, and country guidance; it primarily supports EU, EEA, and Swiss mobility, while other applicants need separate work authorization.
- [Himalayas](https://himalayas.app/jobs) - Free remote-job search with useful salary, timezone, location, visa, and company-context filters; each employer's residence and work-authorization restrictions still apply.
- [Impactpool](https://www.impactpool.org/search) - Search across United Nations, European Union, NGO, development-bank, and international-organization vacancies; basic search is free while advanced insights, coaching, and some programs are paid.
- [IND Highly Skilled Migrant Permit](https://ind.nl/en/residence-permits/work/highly-skilled-migrant) - Official Netherlands guidance on eligibility, recognized sponsors, income rules, applications, rights, and obligations for highly skilled migrants.
- [IND Recognised Sponsors Register](https://ind.nl/en/public-register-recognised-sponsors/public-register-work) - Official searchable register of organizations recognized to sponsor migrant employees for work in the Netherlands.
- [Make it in Germany Job Listings](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/working-in-germany/job-listings) - German federal portal combining job listings with official visa, qualification, application, and relocation guidance; a listed vacancy does not automatically include sponsorship.
- [NoDesk Remote Jobs](https://nodesk.co/remote-jobs/) - Free, low-friction remote board with category and location collections; inventory is smaller than major aggregators and country eligibility varies.
- [OECD Careers](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/careers.html) - Official, temporary, internship, and early-career routes in evidence, data, economics, digital policy, and international governance; many roles require member-country nationality and English or French.
- [ReliefWeb Jobs](https://reliefweb.int/jobs) - United Nations OCHA service aggregating humanitarian and development jobs, consultancies, and internships; confirm the hiring organization, duty station, contract, and security conditions.
- [Relocate.me](https://relocate.me/) - International technology-job board and relocation guide covering visas, moving logistics, taxes, salaries, and destination comparisons.
- [Remote in Tech](https://github.com/remoteintech/remote-jobs) - Community-maintained list of remote-friendly technology companies for building direct-employer watchlists; it is not a live vacancy feed and policies can become stale.
- [Remote OK](https://remoteok.com/) - Free remote-job search with public JSON and RSS feeds; listings can be aggregated or geographically restricted, and feed reuse requires attribution.
- [RemoteJobsFinder](https://remotejobsfinder.co/en) - Job-search aggregator for remote, hybrid, and location-based roles across employers and job boards.
- [Remotive](https://remotive.com/remote-jobs) - Curated remote-job board with categories, alerts, and a public API; the API delays listings and requires attribution, and vacancies can have geographic restrictions.
- [UIS Australia Skilled Migration](https://www.uisaustralia.com/lp/en/global/skilled/) - Commercial assessment and guidance service for exploring skilled-migration pathways to Australia; verify claims against official Australian immigration sources before paying.
- [UN Careers](https://careers.un.org/) - Official United Nations Secretariat portal for professional, temporary, consultant, internship, data, technology, and policy roles; other United Nations agencies maintain separate systems.
- [We Work Remotely](https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs) - Long-running remote board covering technical and nontechnical work; free Basic access requires a jobseeker account, Pro and TopAccess are paid, and location or timezone limits still apply.
- [Working Nomads](https://www.workingnomads.com/jobs) - Free remote-job browsing and email alerts across full-time, part-time, contract, technical, and nontechnical categories; verify duplicates and employer restrictions.
- [World Bank Group Careers](https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/careers) - Official staff, consultant, and talent-program openings across development research, economics, data, digital technology, and operations; eligibility and contract terms are posting-specific.

Search beyond the title "software engineer." Useful institutional terms include research software engineer, scientific programmer, research computing engineer, computational scientist, research data engineer, HPC application engineer, technical officer, and open-science infrastructure.

- [AAS Careers and Job Register](https://aas.org/careers) - Astronomy and astrophysics vacancies including scientific software, instrumentation, technical, observatory, fellowship, and faculty roles.
- [Alliance for Data Science and AI Jobs](https://alliance4datascience.ai/resources/jobs/) - Moderated academic and nonacademic data-science and AI board with filters for remote, postdoctoral, internship, temporary, and nonacademic roles.
- [APS Careers](https://www.aps.org/careers) - Physics and STEM board spanning academia, industry, and national laboratories; jobseeker access does not require American Physical Society membership.
- [CRA Career Center](https://careercenter.cra.org/) - Computing Research Association board for computer science, engineering, AI, machine learning, robotics, and related academic or research positions; public search is free.
- [ELIXIR Job Vacancies](https://elixir-europe.org/about-us/vacancies) - European life-science data-infrastructure vacancies including software engineering, bioinformatics, research software, data stewardship, and research roles.
- [HPC.social Jobs](https://hpc.social/projects/jobs/) - Community-maintained HPC, Linux, scientific applications, research software, and research-computing board with JSON and Atom feeds.
- [Research Software Engineers International](https://researchsoftware.org/) - Directory of national and regional research-software-engineering associations and communities; use member organizations to find local networks and boards.
- [Science Gateways Community Institute Jobs](https://sciencegateways.org/networking-community/jobs) - Community board for software, cyberinfrastructure, research computing, education, and support work around science gateways; it can be empty between postings and also offers submission and newsletter routes.
- [Society for Neuroscience NeuroJobs](https://www.sfn.org/neurojobs) - International neuroscience career center with research, computational, technical, and academic roles; some broader career content may require membership.
- [Society of Research Software Engineering Vacancies](https://society-rse.org/careers/vacancies/) - Moderated United Kingdom Society of RSE board for research-software and closely related vacancies worldwide, with remote filtering.
- [The Carpentries Opportunities](https://carpentries.topicbox.com/groups/opportunities) - Public community mailing-list archive for research software, data, training, education, and open-science opportunities; verify each post's status and deadline.
- [US-RSE Job Board](https://us-rse.org/jobs/) - Association-maintained listings for research-software and adjacent research-computing roles, primarily in the United States.

## Academic and Research Careers

University and research boards often mix faculty jobs with software, data, library, laboratory, technical, administrative, and project-funded roles. Filter by role type and read degree requirements instead of assuming every research position requires a doctorate.

- [Academic Positions](https://academicpositions.com/) - International academic and research board with filters for engineers, research assistants, postdocs, countries, employers, languages, and remote work; an account enables alerts and saved roles.
- [AcademicJobsOnline](https://academicjobsonline.org/ajo) - Centralized listings and application system used by universities, laboratories, and research organizations; an account is needed to submit dossiers and reference letters.
- [AcademicTransfer](https://www.academictransfer.com/en/jobs/) - Cooperative portal for Dutch universities, medical centers, KNAW and NWO institutes, and other research employers, covering scientific and professional roles.
- [EURAXESS Jobs and Opportunities](https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/jobs/search) - European Commission-backed search for research jobs, funding, and hosting offers with country, field, sector, researcher-profile, and offer-type filters.
- [HERC Jobs](https://www.hercjobs.org/) - United States Higher Education Recruitment Consortium listings for faculty, staff, research, technical, and executive roles, with regional and dual-career tools.
- [HigherEdJobs](https://www.higheredjobs.com/) - Large United States-centered higher-education board covering research, laboratory, IT, administrative, faculty, and remote positions; a free account enables alerts.
- [jobs.ac.uk](https://www.jobs.ac.uk/) - United Kingdom-centered academic, research, technical, IT, and professional-support vacancies with international listings and career guidance.
- [Nature Careers](https://www.nature.com/naturecareers) - Global scientific job board with subject, location, employer, and position-type filters plus alerts and career material.
- [Pathways to Science](https://www.pathwaystoscience.org/) - Institute for Broadening Participation directory of funded United States STEM opportunities for undergraduates, graduate students, postdocs, faculty, and administrators; it is not a conventional job board.
- [Science Careers](https://jobs.sciencecareers.org/) - AAAS and Science global STEM recruitment board for academic, industrial, government, nonprofit, and research roles.

## Fellowships, Apprenticeships, and Temporary Research

Fellowships and mentorships may be employment, stipends, grants, educational awards, or independent contracts. Verify taxes, benefits, hours, contract length, intellectual-property terms, student or degree status, citizenship, and whether outside work is allowed. Akashic also catalogs the [Apprenticeship Job Finder](../awesome-abundance/public-programs-and-services/README.md) and [Google Summer of Code](../open-source/README.md) without duplicating them here.

- [AAAS Science and Technology Policy Fellowships](https://www.aaas.org/programs/science-technology-policy-fellowships) - Yearlong United States federal-policy placements with track-specific eligibility; general routes require United States citizenship and a qualifying doctorate, or an engineering master's plus professional experience.
- [LFX Mentorship](https://mentorship.lfx.linuxfoundation.org/) - Linux Foundation, CNCF, kernel, and related open-source mentorships, commonly lasting 12 or 24 weeks; only opportunities explicitly marked with a stipend are funded.
- [Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships) - Competitive 12-to-24-month European fellowships or 24-to-36-month Global fellowships submitted with a host organization; a doctorate and mobility-rule compliance are required.
- [MLH Fellowship Open Source Track](https://fellowship.mlh.com/programs/open-source) - Remote 12-week open-source internship alternative with mentorship and rolling batches; eligibility, weekly hours, countries, time zones, and stipend availability vary by program.
- [NASA Postdoctoral Program Opportunities](https://npp.orau.org/applicants/opportunities.html) - Searchable NASA center, headquarters, and JPL research fellowships generally requiring a completed doctorate; citizenship rules vary and a Zintellect profile is required.
- [NIH Office of Intramural Training and Education](https://www.training.nih.gov/) - National Institutes of Health listings and guidance for summer, postbaccalaureate, graduate, postdoctoral, and other research appointments; degree and citizenship rules vary.
- [NIST Professional Research Experience Program](https://www.nist.gov/iaao/academic-affairs-office/prep) - Research appointments for students, recent graduates, postdocs, senior researchers, and faculty through participating universities; terms vary by location and university partner.
- [NRC Research Associateship Programs](https://www.nationalacademies.org/programs/PGA-FP-18-P-23) - Competitive postdoctoral and senior guest-research awards at participating United States federal laboratories and affiliates; doctoral and sponsor-specific citizenship requirements apply.
- [NSF Careers](https://www.nsf.gov/careers) - Official National Science Foundation source for permanent, temporary, scientist-rotator, and other staffing routes; openings and eligibility depend on current federal announcements.
- [Outreachy](https://www.outreachy.org/) - Paid remote three-month open-source and open-science internships for applicants affected by underrepresentation or systemic bias; eligibility, availability, contribution, and cohort windows are strict.
- [Sovereign Tech Fellowship](https://www.sovereign.tech/programs/fellowship) - Periodic paid routes for experienced open-source maintainers, community managers, and technical writers working on critical infrastructure; track, contribution-history, and employment-geography rules differ.
- [Zintellect Opportunity Catalog](https://zintellect.com/catalog) - ORAU and ORISE portal for government and private research internships, fellowships, scholarships, and appointments, with academic-level, discipline, citizenship, organization, and location filters.

- [Hacker News Who Is Hiring](https://news.ycombinator.com/submitted?id=whoishiring) - Monthly employer and candidate threads with direct access to founders and technical teams; posts are unstructured and unvetted, so protect contact details and verify every company.
- [Idealist Jobs](https://www.idealist.org/en/jobs) - Broad nonprofit and mission-driven search for jobs and internships with salary and remote filters that vary by organization.
- [Open Source JobHub](https://opensourcejobhub.com/) - Free Linux and open-source ecosystem job search with remote, country, and category filters; verify recency and apply through the employer's domain.
- [Tech Jobs for Good](https://www.techjobsforgood.com/) - United States-focused technology, data, design, and product roles organized by social-impact area; only part of the inventory is free and expanded access is paid.
- [Terra.do Climate Jobs](https://www.terra.do/climate-jobs/job-board/) - Large global climate-job aggregator with alerts and many discipline and location filters; courses and community products are paid and listings can be stale or loosely climate-related.
- [Work for Good](https://workforgood.org/) - Free United States nonprofit job browsing and alerts with mission, function, location, and salary-oriented navigation; regional coverage varies.

## Job-Search Tools and Automation

For monitoring and private workflows, cross-link the existing Akashic entries for [changedetection.io](../developer-tools/README.md), [n8n Community Edition](../awesome-abundance/developer-and-digital-resources/README.md), and [SearXNG](../self-hosting-and-homelab/README.md). Self-hosting gives control but also creates security, backup, maintenance, and privacy responsibilities.

- [AI Job Search](https://github.com/MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search) - Local Claude Code framework for evaluating postings, tailoring materials, and preparing for interviews; setup writes sensitive career data into tracked files, so use a private copy rather than a public fork and review every output.
- [AIApply](https://aiapply.co/) - Commercial AI job-search service for resumes, cover letters, interview preparation, matching, and application automation; pricing and automation behavior should be reviewed before use.
- [Ashby Public Job Posting API](https://developers.ashbyhq.com/docs/public-job-posting-api) - Official endpoint for currently published jobs, sometimes including compensation; it requires a known employer board name and should lead to the employer-hosted application.
- [CareerOneStop API Explorer](https://api.careeronestop.org/api-explorer/) - Official United States Department of Labor APIs for jobs, occupations, skills, training, centers, and labor data; developer registration and usage terms may apply.
- [FreshRSS](https://www.freshrss.org/) - Free AGPL self-hosted RSS and Atom reader with saved searches, OPML, feed generation, and limited XPath scraping; hosting and permitted-use checks are the user's responsibility.
- [Greenhouse API Overview](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/10568627186203-Greenhouse-API-overview) - Official overview of Greenhouse interfaces; published Job Board GET data is public, while application submission and other protected actions require employer authorization.
- [Huginn](https://github.com/huginn/huginn) - Free MIT-licensed self-hosted automation agents for permitted webpages, RSS, email, and webhooks; setup is technical and integrations can break.
- [JobSpy](https://github.com/speedyapply/JobSpy) - MIT-licensed Python library that gathers results from several boards for analysis and deduplication; source restrictions and blocking are common, so use it only where collection is permitted.
- [JobSync](https://github.com/Gsync/jobsync) - MIT-licensed self-hosted tracker with analytics, resume data, ATS discovery, MCP support, and optional local or cloud AI; deployment and model-key costs or privacy vary.
- [JSON Resume](https://jsonresume.org/) - Open, machine-readable resume schema with community themes and tools for portable career data; theme and client quality vary, so inspect every export.
- [Lever Postings API](https://github.com/lever/postings-api) - Official public interface for a known employer's published Lever jobs in JSON or HTML; it is not a universal employer index and should not be used to automate submissions.
- [OpenResume](https://www.open-resume.com/) - Free open-source browser-local resume builder and ATS-readability parser with no signup; browser storage should be backed up and ATS checks are diagnostic, not guarantees.
- [Reactive Resume](https://rxresu.me/) - Mature MIT-licensed resume builder with hosted and self-hosted use plus PDF and JSON export; hosted privacy differs from running it yourself.
- [RenderCV](https://rendercv.com/) - MIT-licensed command-line system that turns structured YAML into version-controlled resumes and academic CVs; it requires comfort with structured text and Python tooling.
- [Resume Matcher](https://resumematcher.fyi/) - Free open-source tool that compares a resume with a job description and helps tailor keywords, structure, and application materials without inventing experience.
- [Sonara](https://www.sonara.ai/) - Commercial AI job-search tool for discovering matching roles and automating applications; confirm current cost, data handling, and every submission before use.
- [USAJOBS Developer API](https://developer.usajobs.gov/) - Official United States federal search API supporting custom queries by agency, series, salary, location, and hiring path; registered credentials and required headers are needed.

## Career Research and Public Support

Public career centers can provide actual navigators, computers, workshops, training referrals, and local employer knowledge. Also see Akashic's [American Job Center Finder and public training resources](../awesome-abundance/public-programs-and-services/README.md).

- [BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) - Official United States occupation profiles covering duties, work environment, education, pay, employment, and outlook; national projections do not replace current local postings.
- [CareerOneStop](https://www.careeronestop.org/) - Free United States Department of Labor hub for occupations, wages, jobs, training, resumes, applications, interviews, offers, worksheets, and local human assistance.

## Applications, Interviews, and Job-Search Safety

Use interview preparation in proportion to the role: research, university, nonprofit, and public-sector processes can differ sharply from big-technology coding loops. For discrimination, accommodations, organizing, wage, retaliation, and workplace-safety pathways, see [Legal Help and Law](../legal-help-and-law/README.md) and the [Job Accommodation Network](../awesome-abundance/health-well-being-and-accessibility/README.md).
