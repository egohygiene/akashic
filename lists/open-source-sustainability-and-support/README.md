## Choosing a Support Path

| Need | Start here | Verify before committing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time-limited work with defined outputs | Grants and Public Investment | Applicant and geography rules, active deadline, award form, milestones, reporting, IP, and open-release terms |
| Recurring contributions from users or companies | Funding Discovery, Pledges, and Sponsorship | Payout countries, payment processing, platform or host fees, tax treatment, dependency-allocation method, and donor restrictions |
| A legal and financial home without incorporating | Fiscal Hosting and Organizational Support | Host mission, jurisdiction, fee, control of funds and assets, contracts, employment, exit terms, and tax deductibility |
| Hosting, CI, communication, monitoring, or testing | Infrastructure and Service Benefits | License and noncommercial rules, quotas, attribution, renewal, acceptable use, overages, and migration plan |
| Security fixes for software beyond upstream support | Maintenance Contracts and Earned Revenue | Covered versions and CVEs, patch delivery, SLAs, compatibility, price, term, termination, and relationship to upstream maintainers |
| Licensing, governance, enforcement, or patent exposure | Legal and Patent Support | Client or member eligibility, jurisdiction, scope, conflicts, fees, cross-license duties, and what is not represented |

Apply only through a canonical program page. No entry promises that an application is open or that the reader qualifies. When a program is periodic, upcoming, closed, selected by invitation, or undergoing a commercial transition, the description and structured status make that limitation visible.

## Metadata Guide

Every entry has a stable ID and records the support mechanism, applicant type, project stage, cadence, cost model, and material obligations. `programChecked` is the date primary program pages were checked for those operational facts. It is not a human truth review, endorsement, eligibility decision, or substitute for the current call text. `linkStatus` and `linkChecked` record only mechanical page availability.

`costModel: no-fee` means the program page does not charge to apply or use the benefit; payment processing, taxes, award administration, overages, or compliance costs can still apply. `in-kind` means the support is a service or credit rather than unrestricted cash. Commercial and nonprofit mechanisms are included together so readers can compare them without treating them as interchangeable.
