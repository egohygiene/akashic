# Awesome Travel and Mobility [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A cross-budget map for understanding a trip, getting permission to go, moving safely, finding somewhere to stay, living nomadically, recovering from disruption, and deciding when convenience or luxury is actually worth paying for.

Part of [akashic](../../README.md), the Ego Hygiene knowledge collection.

> **Scope and safety:** Travel rules, prices, routes, border controls, advisories, accessibility, insurance, and platform terms change constantly. A marketplace listing or booking confirmation does not prove that a stay, job, vehicle, visa, or activity is legal or safe. Official border and transport authorities control admission and operation; a clinician should handle individualized health decisions; and legal, tax, employment, tenancy, and residency questions can require qualified local help. This guide is an orientation layer, not a guarantee, endorsement, or substitute for current official instructions.

**Access language:** **Official** means a government, regulator, public operator, or intergovernmental source. **Open** means openly licensed software or knowledge. **Free** means no usage fee for the resource itself. **Freemium** means a continuing limited free path exists. **Metasearch** compares options and normally redirects elsewhere. **OTA/retailer** takes or services a booking as an intermediary. **Pay-per-use** has transaction or booking charges without a base subscription. **Membership** recurs or requires dues. **Advisor** may charge planning fees, earn supplier commission, or both. **Quote** means pricing is negotiated or itinerary-specific. **Eligibility** and **region** signal access limits.

## Orient First

### The travel system in one view

| Layer | Question to answer | What exists underneath the apps and packages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Permission | Can every traveler enter, remain, work, drive, bring medication, and return? | Passport validity, visas or authorizations, transit rules, permitted purpose, customs, health rules, licenses, and evidence requested at the border. |
| Door-to-door route | How does the trip work from the first address to the last? | Positioning time, transfers, terminal changes, minimum connection time, local transit, accessibility, luggage, check-in, and the operating carrier. |
| Stay | Is there a safe and lawful place to sleep? | The property or host, exact address, occupancy and local rules, total price, identity checks, accessibility, cancellation, deposit, and a backup. |
| Daily life | How will food, movement, communication, work, care, and rest happen? | Local prices and hours, maps, language access, payment methods, mobile data, workspace, medication, climate, and cultural context. |
| Money | What is the real cost and how can funds be reached? | Taxes, bags, seats, cleaning and resort fees, deposits, exchange spread, tips, insurance, transit to the airport, emergency reserve, and a second payment path. |
| Safety and access | What could make the trip unsafe or unusable for this person? | Advisories, weather, health needs, disability access, discrimination, scams, isolation, emergency numbers, trusted contacts, and evacuation limits. |
| Work and residency | Does earning money or staying longer change the legal category? | Work authorization, tax residence, local registration, tenancy, insurance, employer rules, and permanent-address obligations. |
| Exit and recovery | What happens if a person is denied, delayed, stranded, sick, robbed, or simply needs to leave? | Refund rights, insurance and assistance contacts, embassy help, replacement documents, alternate transport, spare funds, copies, and a check-in plan. |

### Choose the path that fits

**Almost no money.** Start with identity and eligibility, a genuinely safe destination, public or slow ground transport, hospitality or house-sitting communities, public lands, library and community resources, and a realistic emergency exit fund. “Free accommodation” can still require membership, labor, food, insurance, transport, trust, or legal work permission.

**Budget or slow travel.** Compare the complete door-to-door cost, search flexible dates and nearby stations, book direct when the support chain matters, combine hostels or longer stays with public transit, and value refundable plans over a fragile itinerary.

**Ordinary vacation or business trip.** Confirm passport and entry rules first, then choose the simplest route and cancellation terms that fit the risk. A slightly higher direct fare can be cheaper than a complicated intermediary when plans change.

**Digital nomad or long stay.** Treat the destination as temporary life rather than a vacation: verify the legal work and residence path, tax exposure, housing legality, reliable connectivity, health coverage, banking, mail, data security, and how to leave.

**Accessibility, family, health, or another specific need.** Verify the exact aircraft, room, route, equipment, companion, medication, food, sensory, and assistance requirements with the operator. A filter or “accessible” label is a lead, not proof.

**Premium, bespoke, or private travel.** Pay for expertise, privacy, time, complex coordination, access, or recovery—not merely a luxury label. Identify the actual operator, property, advisor compensation, cancellation terms, safety oversight, and every quote component before signing.

### Know who owns the booking

| Layer | What it does | Who normally changes or refunds the booking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct supplier | The airline, railway, hotel, host, tour operator, or vehicle company delivers and sells its own service. | The supplier, subject to its fare or contract and applicable law. |
| Metasearch | Compares prices or routes and sends the traveler to a supplier or seller. | The named supplier or seller—not usually the comparison site. |
| OTA or retailer | Sells a supplier's inventory under an intermediary booking and support process. | Usually the OTA first, even when the supplier operates the trip. |
| Peer marketplace | Introduces travelers to hosts, drivers, homes, vehicles, or experiences and supplies platform rules or payments. | The platform process and the individual provider both matter. |
| Membership | Sells recurring access, credits, alerts, exchanges, or negotiated benefits. | The membership and supplier may have separate cancellation rules. |
| Advisor or concierge | Researches, coordinates, advocates, and may book through suppliers or consortia. | Ask who is merchant of record, who holds funds, and who provides after-hours help. |
| Charter or bespoke contract | Coordinates a specific aircraft, vessel, villa, expedition, or multi-supplier itinerary. | The signed contract controls; broker, owner, and licensed operator may be different parties. |

### Minimum viable trip

1. Check the destination government's entry rules for each passport, transit point, purpose, and length of stay.
2. Price the entire trip, including ground connections, fees, deposits, food, data, insurance, and a safe way home.
3. Confirm the operating carrier or property, exact seller, cancellation rules, accessibility, and legal stay or work conditions.
4. Save confirmations, policy wording, document copies, emergency contacts, and critical addresses somewhere available offline.
5. Share an itinerary or check-in plan with a trusted person and keep backup money, identification, medication, and communications separate from the main set.
6. Recheck official alerts, weather, strikes, schedule changes, and border requirements shortly before departure and while traveling.

## Rights, Disruptions, Scams, and Insurance

Trip cancellation or interruption, emergency medical coverage, evacuation, a transport membership, supplier insolvency protection, and credit-card benefits solve different problems. Read the actual policy wording and underwriter, including residence, destination, dates, covered reasons, pre-existing-condition windows, primary or secondary status, activity exclusions, per-person caps, evacuation destination, deductibles, and claim deadlines.

- [AirHelp](https://www.airhelp.com/) - Commercial flight-compensation claims service with contingency-fee and membership paths; file directly with the airline or regulator first when feasible and compare the fee taken from any recovery.
- [Air Travel Consumer Reports](https://www.transportation.gov/individuals/aviation-consumer-protection/air-travel-consumer-reports) - Official United States data on delays, cancellations, baggage, wheelchairs, oversales, complaints, and airline service.
- [ATOL Check](https://www.atol.org/check-for-atol/) - Official United Kingdom lookup for flight-inclusive package protection; verify the company and certificate because accommodation-only, cruise-only, and other arrangements may differ.
- [Canada Air Passenger Protection](https://otc-cta.gc.ca/eng/air-passenger-protection-regulations-highlights) - Official Canadian passenger-rights summary for communication, delays, cancellations, denied boarding, baggage, seating, and complaints.
- [EU Passenger Rights](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/passenger-rights/index_en.htm) - Official European Union guidance for air, rail, bus, coach, and ship disruption and reduced mobility, with route and circumstance limits.
- [Flightright](https://www.flightright.com/) - Commercial contingency-fee service focused on eligible European flight claims; compare its fee and escalation path with filing directly.
- [FTC Vacation and Travel Scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/timeshares-vacation-clubs-and-related-scams) - Official United States warning signs for vacation rentals, clubs, prizes, timeshares, pressure sales, wire payments, and refund promises.
- [IATA Fake Travel Sites](https://www.iata.org/en/youandiata/travelers/fraud/) - Airline-industry guidance for checking agencies, accreditation claims, copied logos, payment requests, and suspicious booking sites.
- [InsureMyTrip](https://www.insuremytrip.com/) - Commercial commission-supported comparison marketplace for trip and travel-medical policies; the named insurer and policy wording provide coverage.
- [NAIC Travel Insurance Consumer Guide](https://content.naic.org/consumer/travel-insurance.htm) - United States regulator-association overview of coverage, exclusions, questions, and state insurance-department checks.
- [Squaremouth](https://www.squaremouth.com/) - Commercial commission-supported travel-insurance comparison and review marketplace; compare underwriter, wording, support, and direct price.
- [UK Air Passenger Travel Guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/air-passenger-travel-guide) - Official United Kingdom guide to booking, identity, baggage, accessibility, disruption, refunds, complaints, and travel-company failure.
- [U.S. DOT Airline Customer Service Dashboard](https://www.transportation.gov/airconsumer/airline-customer-service-dashboard) - Official comparison of participating airline commitments during controllable disruption; voluntary commitments are distinct from legal rights.
- [U.S. DOT Air Travel Complaint](https://secure.dot.gov/air-travel-complaint) - Official free complaint form for airline consumer, civil-rights, or disability issues; preserve confirmations, communications, photos, and receipts.
- [U.S. DOT Aviation Consumer Protection](https://www.transportation.gov/airconsumer) - Official United States hub for refunds, delays, cancellations, bumping, baggage, discrimination, family seating, disability, charters, and complaints.
- [USA.gov Travel Complaints](https://www.usa.gov/travel-complaints) - Official routing for United States air, security, highway, rail, bus, cruise, and travel-seller complaints.
- [VisitorsCoverage](https://www.visitorscoverage.com/) - Commercial marketplace specializing in visitor, travel-medical, and related policies; eligibility, network, exclusions, and underwriter control coverage.
- [World Nomads](https://www.worldnomads.com/) - Paid travel-insurance brand with residence-specific underwriters, policies, and activity coverage; affiliate marketing and a familiar name do not replace reading the contract.

## Flights

On separate tickets or a self-transfer, no carrier may be responsible for a missed connection; hidden-city ticketing can violate fare rules, strand checked bags, cancel later segments, and create border problems.

- [FlightConnections](https://www.flightconnections.com/) - Freemium route-map and nonstop-destination explorer; schedules and premium filters have limits and it is not a booking authority.
- [Flightradar24](https://www.flightradar24.com/) - Freemium aircraft and flight tracker with data gaps by aircraft, geography, and source.
- [Flighty](https://www.flighty.com/) - Freemium Apple-platform flight tracker with predictive alerts and paid features; it supplements rather than replaces carrier notifications.
- [Going](https://www.going.com/) - Free limited and paid flight-deal alerts by departure region; it finds opportunities but does not sell the ticket or guarantee a fare.
- [Google Flights](https://www.google.com/travel/flights) - Free metasearch with calendar, flexible-date, route, emissions, and price-tracking tools; the named airline or seller controls booking and support.
- [Hopper](https://www.hopper.com/) - App-based OTA with predictions and optional price, cancellation, and disruption products; compare every add-on and the intermediary support chain against booking direct.
- [ITA Matrix](https://matrix.itasoftware.com/) - Free research-only tool for advanced routing, dates, fare codes, and price construction; it does not issue tickets.
- [KAYAK](https://www.kayak.com/) - Free affiliate-supported metasearch across flights and other travel products, with some partner booking flows; identify the actual seller before paying.
- [Kiwi.com](https://www.kiwi.com/) - OTA known for combining separate tickets and self-transfers; read connection protection, visa, baggage, check-in, and disruption terms especially carefully.
- [momondo](https://www.momondo.com/) - Free KAYAK-owned affiliate metasearch across airlines and agencies; compare the final seller, currency, baggage, and support terms.
- [Secret Flying](https://www.secretflying.com/) - Free advertising- and affiliate-supported deal alerts; mistake fares can disappear or be canceled, so delay nonrefundable add-ons until ticketing is stable.
- [Skyscanner](https://www.skyscanner.com/) - Free affiliate metasearch that compares airlines and agencies; the selected seller normally handles payment, changes, and refunds.

## Rail, Bus, Ferry, and Cruise

Route aggregators are discovery layers, not proof of a connection. Confirm the station or port, operating company, boarding cutoff, accessibility, luggage, reservation requirement, through-ticket protection, strikes, and live timetable with the operator. A pass is valuable only when its eligible trips cost more than the pass plus required reservations and supplements.

- [12Go](https://12go.asia/) - Commercial ground and ferry OTA with particularly broad Asian coverage; fulfillment, operator confirmation, fees, and refund paths vary by booking.
- [Amtrak](https://www.amtrak.com/) - Official direct operator and booking source for United States intercity passenger rail, passes, alerts, accessibility, and current promotions.

## Accessibility and Additional Travel Needs

Ask for the exact measurement and process: doorway and turning space, bed and bathroom transfer, lift type and weight, battery handling, seating, service animal, sensory environment, food, communication, assistance handoff, companion, and failure plan. Record confirmations in writing. A search filter, crowd report, “accessible room,” badge, or special-service request is a lead—not proof that a trip works for a particular body or device.

- [AccessAble](https://www.accessable.co.uk/) - Free professionally surveyed United Kingdom venue access guides with detailed photos and measurements; confirm critical recent changes directly.
- [accessibleGO](https://accessiblego.com/) - Commercial disability-focused hotel, car, flight, and equipment marketplace whose staff can help confirm requests; property and partner performance still require verification.

- [Amtrak Passengers with Disabilities Discount](https://www.amtrak.com/deals-discounts/everyday-discounts/passengers-with-disabilities-discounts.html) - Official discount for eligible passengers and companions on many United States routes, with proof, service, and fare restrictions.
- [EU Reduced-Mobility Passenger Rights](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/transport-disability/reduced-mobility/index_en.htm) - Official European Union assistance and nondiscrimination rights across air, rail, bus, coach, and ship travel, with mode-specific notice and scope rules.
- [Euan's Guide](https://www.euansguide.com/) - Free disabled-access reviews and venue information from a charity and community; reports are experience-specific and can age.
- [FTA ADA Paratransit Rules](https://www.transit.dot.gov/regulations-and-guidance/civil-rights-ada/part-37-transportation-services-individuals-disabilities) - Official United States complementary-paratransit eligibility and service rules; local certification and reservation procedures apply.
- [FTA Reduced-Fare Requirement](https://www.transit.dot.gov/are-transit-providers-required-offer-reduced-transit-fares-seniors-people-disabilities-or-medicare) - Official explanation of off-peak reduced fares for seniors, disabled riders, and Medicare cardholders on federally assisted fixed-route transit; apply with the local agency.
- [Hidden Disabilities Sunflower](https://hdsunflower.com/) - Directory and explanation of a voluntary symbol used at participating places for non-visible disabilities; it does not guarantee expedited service or a specific accommodation.
- [Mobility International USA](https://www.miusa.org/) - Nonprofit resources, advising, and programs for disability-inclusive international study, volunteering, professional exchange, and travel.
- [Society for Accessible Travel and Hospitality](https://sath.org/) - Nonprofit accessible-travel education, industry information, and community resources; verify any provider and itinerary for the individual need.
- [U.S. DOT Airline Passengers with Disabilities Bill of Rights](https://www.transportation.gov/airconsumer/disabilitybillofrights) - Official plain-language rights to assistance, information, seating, devices, service-animal handling, and complaint resolution on covered United States air travel.
- [Wheel the World](https://wheeltheworld.com/) - Commercial accessible-travel marketplace and advisor using detailed accessibility data; confirm exact room, vehicle, equipment, and activity measurements in writing.
- [Wheelmap](https://wheelmap.org/) - Free worldwide OpenStreetMap-based wheelchair-accessibility map; unknown or missing tags are not evidence of access.

## Deals, Points, and Loyalty

**Points are not free travel.** Never borrow, carry interest, pay late, or overspend to earn a reward. Compare cash; do not transfer points until the exact flight or room is confirmed in the program; assume transfers are irreversible; and include taxes, surcharges, annual fees, cancellation, expiration, and devaluation. Search tools can show cached or phantom inventory, so the loyalty program is authoritative.

- [Amtrak Deals](https://www.amtrak.com/deals) - Official durable hub for current United States rail promotions and everyday discounts; route, advance-purchase, passenger, and fare rules vary.
- [AwardTool](https://www.awardtool.com/) - Freemium award-flight search and alert service; free limits, subscription features, cached data, and program coverage vary.
- [AwardWallet](https://awardwallet.com/) - Freemium loyalty-balance and expiration tracker; connecting accounts or email creates privacy and security tradeoffs and some programs restrict automated access.
- [MaxMyPoint](https://maxmypoint.com/) - Freemium hotel-award calendar and alert service; confirm the exact room, dates, points, fees, and cancellation with the hotel program.
- [point.me](https://www.point.me/) - Paid award-search membership with optional concierge services; results still require live confirmation and program transfer knowledge.
- [PointsYeah](https://www.pointsyeah.com/) - Freemium award-flight and hotel search with account limits and paid alerts; confirm live inventory before moving points.
- [Roame](https://roame.travel/) - Freemium award-flight search and alerts; route, date, program, and search-window coverage depends on plan.
- [seats.aero](https://seats.aero/) - Free limited cached award search with paid longer windows, filters, and alerts; always verify at the loyalty program before transferring.
- [StatusMatcher](https://statusmatcher.com/) - Free community reports about airline, hotel, and rental status matches; an old report is not an official current offer.
- [Where to Credit](https://www.wheretocredit.com/) - Free reference for estimating airline mileage earning across partner programs; tables can lag fare-class or program changes.

## Advisors, Tours, and Human Navigators

## Work, Study, Volunteering, and Relocation

Travel attached to a job, school, grant, service program, internship, au pair placement, or move is not ordinary tourism. ## Sustainable and Respectful Travel

The largest impact reduction is often a different mode, fewer flights, a longer stay, less extraction, and respect for local housing and water—not a badge or offset. Certification and calculators provide evidence or estimates, not moral immunity. Follow the land manager, protect wildlife, ask before photographing people or ceremony, pay local providers fairly, and learn whose land and labor make the trip possible.

- [atmosfair Flight Calculator](https://www.atmosfair.de/en/offset/flight/) - Nonprofit flight-emissions estimate with an optional paid offset path; calculation assumptions vary and an offset does not erase the flight.
- [EarthCheck](https://earthcheck.org/) - Commercial sustainability benchmarking, certification, and consulting for tourism organizations; inspect the current certification level, scope, criteria, and audit cycle.
- [Global Sustainable Tourism Council](https://www.gstcouncil.org/) - Global baseline standards and accreditation framework for sustainable travel and tourism; GSTC recognition is not itself certification of a particular property.
- [Green Key](https://www.greenkey.global/) - Environmental certification program and participating-property directory; criteria, national implementation, audit, scope, and current status matter.
- [ICAO Carbon Emissions Calculator](https://www.icao.int/environmental-protection/Carbonoffset/Pages/default.aspx) - Official free methodology and calculator for estimating passenger-flight emissions; results are estimates rather than a complete trip footprint.
- [Leave No Trace Seven Principles](https://lnt.org/why/7-principles/) - Free practical framework for reducing outdoor impacts; current laws, permits, closures, Indigenous guidance, and land-manager rules still control.
- [Native Land Digital](https://native-land.ca/) - Indigenous-led educational map of territories, languages, and treaties; it is a starting point for learning and does not establish legal boundaries or replace local nations' own sources.
- [Travel Impact Model](https://travelimpactmodel.org/) - Open methodology and data model for estimating flight emissions across travel-search systems; estimates depend on aircraft, load, cabin, route, and modeling assumptions.
