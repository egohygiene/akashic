## Discovery and Literature

- [bioRxiv Neuroscience](https://www.biorxiv.org/collection/neuroscience) - Browse preprints in the field while remembering that manuscripts have not yet completed peer review.
- [KnowledgeSpace](https://knowledge-space.org/) - Explore concepts alongside linked literature, datasets, models, and anatomical knowledge.
- [Neurosynth](https://neurosynth.org/) - Perform automated large-scale synthesis and meta-analysis across thousands of published fMRI studies.

## Atlases and Knowledge Maps

- [Allen Brain Map](https://brain-map.org/) - Explore open multimodal atlases and datasets spanning gene expression, connectivity, cells, development, and disease.
- [Allen Cell Types Database](https://celltypes.brain-map.org/) - Compare human and mouse cell morphology, electrophysiology, transcriptomics, and computational models.
- [BigBrain Project](https://bigbrainproject.org/) - Access an ultrahigh-resolution three-dimensional histological reference of the human brain.
- [BrainSpan Atlas of the Developing Human Brain](https://www.brainspan.org/) - Study gene expression and neuroanatomy across prenatal and postnatal human development.
- [EBRAINS Interactive Atlas Viewer](https://ebrains.eu/data-tools-services/tools/interactive-atlas-viewer) - Navigate multilevel human and rodent reference atlases with maps, regions, and linked datasets.
- [Jülich Brain Atlas](https://julich-brain-atlas.de/) - Explore a continuously expanding probabilistic three-dimensional atlas of human brain cytoarchitecture.
- [NeuroMorpho.Org](https://www.neuromorpho.org/) - Search and download curated three-dimensional reconstructions of neurons and glia from many species.
- [neuPrint](https://neuprint.janelia.org/) - Query and visualize connectomes stored in Janelia's graph-based connectomics platform.
- [Virtual Fly Brain](https://www.virtualflybrain.org/) - Explore Drosophila anatomy, expression, cell types, circuits, and connectomic datasets.

## Open Data Archives and Cohorts

- [BossDB](https://bossdb.org/) - Store, stream, and analyze large spatial datasets such as electron-microscopy and X-ray volumes.
- [Brain Image Library](https://www.brainimagelibrary.org/) - Discover large-scale optical-microscopy datasets produced by the BRAIN Initiative and related projects.
- [DABI](https://dabi.loni.usc.edu/) - Access human intracranial neurophysiology and invasive-device datasets through a managed archive.
- [DANDI Archive](https://dandiarchive.org/) - Publish and reuse BRAIN Initiative neurophysiology datasets organized primarily with Neurodata Without Borders.
- [EMBER Archive](https://emberarchive.org/) - Find multimodal brain and behavior datasets organized with community standards such as BIDS and NWB.
- [Human Connectome Project](https://www.humanconnectomeproject.org/) - Access multimodal imaging and behavioral data designed to map human brain connectivity.
- [International Brain Laboratory Data](https://www.internationalbrainlab.com/data) - Reuse standardized brain-wide electrophysiology and behavior data from a large collaborative program.
- [NeMO Archive](https://www.nemoarchive.org/) - Discover BRAIN Initiative genomics, epigenomics, transcriptomics, and related multimodal datasets.
- [NEMAR](https://nemar.org/) - Explore open EEG, MEG, iEEG, and other electrophysiology datasets organized with BIDS.
- [NeuroVault](https://neurovault.org/) - Share and retrieve unthresholded statistical maps, parcellations, and atlases from MRI and PET studies.
- [NIH BRAIN Data and Knowledge Resources](https://www.nih.gov/brain/research/data-knowledge-resources) - Use an official directory of BRAIN Initiative archives, informatics systems, standards, and sharing guidance.
- [NIMH Data Archive](https://nda.nih.gov/) - Request access to harmonized mental-health and neurodevelopmental data from studies including ABCD.
- [OpenNeuro](https://openneuro.org/) - Publish and reuse open neuroimaging datasets validated against the Brain Imaging Data Structure.
- [OpenNeuroPET](https://openneuropet.github.io/) - Find open positron-emission tomography datasets, standards guidance, and analysis resources.

## Neuroimaging and Electrophysiology Tools

- [AFNI](https://afni.nimh.nih.gov/pub/dist/doc/htmldoc/) - Process, statistically analyze, and visualize functional and structural MRI with an NIH-developed toolkit.
- [ANTs](https://stnava.github.io/ANTs/) - Perform image registration, segmentation, normalization, and template construction across biomedical modalities.
- [Brainstorm](https://neuroimage.usc.edu/brainstorm/) - Analyze MEG, EEG, intracranial recordings, and related signals through an accessible graphical environment.
- [DIPY](https://dipy.org/) - Build diffusion-MRI and tractography workflows with an open Python library.
- [EEGLAB](https://sccn.ucsd.edu/eeglab/) - Process and visualize EEG data with a mature MATLAB-based environment and plugin ecosystem.
- [FieldTrip](https://www.fieldtriptoolbox.org/) - Analyze MEG, EEG, iEEG, and other electrophysiological data with a modular MATLAB toolbox.
- [fMRIPrep](https://fmriprep.org/) - Produce robust, transparent, and BIDS-compatible preprocessing for functional MRI.
- [FreeSurfer](https://www.freesurfer.net/) - Reconstruct cortical surfaces and analyze structural, functional, and diffusion MRI.
- [FSL](https://fsl.fmrib.ox.ac.uk/fsl/docs/) - Run a comprehensive suite of MRI analysis, statistics, registration, segmentation, and visualization tools.
- [Kilosort](https://github.com/MouseLand/Kilosort) - Perform GPU-assisted, drift-aware spike sorting for high-channel-count extracellular recordings.
- [MNE-Python](https://mne.tools/stable/) - Analyze EEG, MEG, iEEG, ECoG, and NIRS data with an extensive Python ecosystem.
- [MRIQC](https://mriqc.readthedocs.io/) - Generate automated image-quality metrics and visual reports for MRI datasets.
- [MRtrix3](https://www.mrtrix.org/) - Analyze diffusion MRI, fiber orientation, tractography, and structural connectomes.
- [NiBabel](https://nipy.org/nibabel/) - Read, write, and manipulate common neuroimaging file formats from Python.
- [Nilearn](https://nilearn.github.io/) - Apply statistical learning, decoding, connectivity analysis, and visualization to neuroimaging data.
- [Open Ephys GUI](https://open-ephys.org/gui/) - Acquire, visualize, and process extracellular electrophysiology through a modular open-source application.
- [Phy](https://phy.readthedocs.io/) - Manually inspect and curate high-density extracellular spike-sorting results.
- [QSIPrep](https://qsiprep.readthedocs.io/) - Build standardized BIDS-compatible preprocessing pipelines for diffusion MRI.
- [SpikeInterface](https://spikeinterface.readthedocs.io/) - Read, sort, benchmark, curate, and compare extracellular electrophysiology data through one Python API.
- [SPM](https://www.fil.ion.ucl.ac.uk/spm/software/) - Analyze brain-imaging time series with statistical parametric mapping and a large methods ecosystem.

## Computational Modeling

- [Arbor](https://docs.arbor-sim.org/) - Simulate morphologically detailed neuronal networks efficiently across CPUs, GPUs, and HPC systems.
- [Brain Modeling Toolkit](https://alleninstitute.github.io/bmtk/) - Build and simulate multiscale neural networks with Allen Institute workflows and SONATA files.
- [Brian 2](https://briansimulator.org/) - Express and simulate spiking neural-network models directly from equations in Python.
- [ModelDB](https://modeldb.science/) - Find executable computational models associated with published research.
- [NEST Simulator](https://www.nest-simulator.org/) - Simulate large networks of point neurons and synapses on workstations or supercomputers.
- [NetPyNE](https://www.netpyne.org/) - Specify, simulate, analyze, and optimize multiscale networks built on NEURON.
- [NEURON](https://www.neuronsimulator.org/) - Simulate biophysically detailed neurons and neural networks using Python, HOC, and parallel computing.
- [NeuroML](https://docs.neuroml.org/) - Describe neuronal and network models in a simulator-independent standardized format.
- [Open Source Brain](https://www.opensourcebrain.org/) - Share, reproduce, and collaboratively run neural models, datasets, and analysis code.
- [The Virtual Brain](https://docs.thevirtualbrain.org/) - Construct whole-brain simulations from connectomes and neural-mass models.

## Standards, Reproducibility, and Infrastructure

- [Brain Imaging Data Structure](https://bids.neuroimaging.io/) - Organize neuroimaging and neurophysiology datasets with community-governed file and metadata conventions.
- [INCF Standards and Best Practices](https://incf.org/activities/standards-and-best-practices) - Find endorsed specifications and guidance for FAIR, interoperable neuroscience.
- [Neurodata Without Borders](https://nwb.org/) - Standardize neurophysiology data and metadata with an extensible format and software ecosystem.
- [Neurodesk](https://neurodesk.org/) - Run containerized neuroimaging applications consistently across local, cloud, and HPC environments.
- [Neurodocker](https://www.repronim.org/neurodocker/) - Generate reproducible Docker and Apptainer recipes for neuroscience software stacks.
- [NeuroLibre](https://neurolibre.org/) - Publish executable, reproducible research narratives backed by code, data, and interactive notebooks.
- [NITRC](https://www.nitrc.org/) - Discover neuroimaging software, datasets, computational environments, and community projects.
- [ReproNim](https://repronim.org/) - Adopt tools and practices for reproducible, FAIR neuroimaging research.
- [TemplateFlow](https://www.templateflow.org/) - Retrieve versioned neuroimaging templates, atlases, masks, and metadata programmatically.

## Learning and Education

- [BrainFacts](https://www.brainfacts.org/) - Learn foundational neuroscience through Society for Neuroscience-supported articles, explainers, and educator materials.
- [ENIGMA-U](https://enigma.ini.usc.edu/training/enigma-u/) - Study neuroimaging, neuroscience, and neuropsychology through free lectures from ENIGMA researchers.
- [Harvard Fundamentals of Neuroscience](https://harvardonline.harvard.edu/program/microbachelors-program-in-introduction-to-neuroscience) - Follow a three-course introduction from cellular electrophysiology through networks, sensation, and behavior.
- [INCF TrainingSpace](https://training.incf.org/) - Browse free courses, lectures, tutorials, and laboratory exercises in neuroscience and neuroinformatics.
- [Neuromatch Computational Neuroscience Coursebook](https://compneuro.neuromatch.io/) - Learn through open, code-first tutorials, exercises, and projects.
- [Neuronal Dynamics](https://neuronaldynamics.epfl.ch/) - Read a free interactive textbook on computational neuroscience with equations, exercises, and videos.
- [Neuroscience Online](https://nba.uth.tmc.edu/neuroscience/) - Study an open-access electronic textbook covering cellular, systems, and clinical neuroscience.

## Organizations, Journals, and Conferences

- [Computational and Systems Neuroscience](https://www.cosyne.org/) - Attend an annual single-track meeting and workshops spanning computational and systems neuroscience.
- [eNeuro](https://www.eneuro.org/) - Read an open-access Society for Neuroscience journal welcoming methods, replications, and well-supported negative results.
- [Federation of European Neuroscience Societies](https://www.fens.org/) - Find European training, networking, grants, advocacy, and the biennial FENS Forum.
- [International Brain Research Organization](https://ibro.org/) - Access global training, fellowships, schools, networks, and capacity-building programs.
- [Journal of Neuroscience](https://www.jneurosci.org/) - Read peer-reviewed research across the breadth of the field from the Society for Neuroscience.
- [Organization for Computational Neurosciences](https://www.cnsorg.org/) - Join the community behind the annual CNS meeting, educational courses, and computational-neuroscience forum.
- [Organization for Human Brain Mapping](https://humanbrainmapping.org/) - Connect with the international human-neuroimaging community and its annual meeting.
- [Society for Neuroscience](https://www.sfn.org/) - Explore the largest professional society in the field, its annual meeting, journals, advocacy, and public resources.

## Clinical and Translational Neuroscience

- [Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative](https://adni.loni.usc.edu/) - Request longitudinal imaging, biomarker, genetic, cognitive, and clinical data for Alzheimer's disease research.
- [ENIGMA Consortium](https://enigma.ini.usc.edu/) - Join large-scale collaborative working groups for imaging genetics and studies of brain disorders.
- [National Alzheimer's Coordinating Center](https://www.naccdata.org/) - Request standardized multimodal Alzheimer's and related-dementias data collected across research centers.
- [NINDS Common Data Elements](https://commondataelements.ninds.nih.gov/) - Reuse standardized definitions, forms, and outcome measures for neurological clinical research.
- [PPMI](https://www.ppmi-info.org/) - Explore a longitudinal Parkinson's biomarker study combining clinical, imaging, omics, and biospecimen data.
- [SPARC Portal](https://sparc.science/) - Access peripheral nervous system datasets, computational models, anatomical maps, and analysis tools.

Some archives require registration, institutional approval, or a data-use agreement. Some tools depend on commercial software or use separate commercial licenses; verify the current terms before adopting them.
