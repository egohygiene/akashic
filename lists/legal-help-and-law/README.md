# Awesome Legal Help and Law [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A practical map for recognizing a legal problem, protecting a deadline, finding legitimate help, researching the law, using courts and complaint systems, following legal change, and understanding what professional tools add.

Part of [akashic](../../README.md), the Ego Hygiene knowledge collection.

<!-- site-guide:start -->

> **Scope and safety:** This guide is primarily United States-oriented, with international and comparative gateways near the end. It provides legal information and navigation—not individualized legal advice, representation, or a prediction of outcome. Law changes across federal, state, tribal, territorial, local, and international systems, and small factual differences can change the answer. A hotline, intake form, complaint, negotiation, mediation, benefits application, or AI conversation usually does not pause a court, agency, eviction, immigration, election, appeal, or limitation deadline. Read every notice, calendar the earliest possible deadline, and use a qualified lawyer or authorized representative for consequential decisions.

**Access language:** **Official** means a court, government, regulator, or intergovernmental source. **Nonprofit** describes the operator, not guaranteed free service. **Free** means no charge for the resource itself. **Eligibility** signals income, geography, issue, status, age, case-stage, or capacity limits. **Brief advice** is narrower than ongoing representation. **Directory** or **referral** does not endorse every provider or guarantee acceptance. **Commercial** may involve advertising, lead generation, subscriptions, transaction fees, or paid placement. **Library access** means a paid database may be available without direct charge through a participating institution. **Sensitive** signals that intake may request identity, financial, immigration, health, family, or case information.

## Orient First

### The legal system in one view

| Layer | Question to answer | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Safety | Is anyone in immediate physical danger, being monitored, detained, displaced, or denied urgent care? | Whether emergency services, a survivor advocate, appointed criminal counsel, a safer device, shelter, or another immediate pathway comes before ordinary research. |
| Jurisdiction | Which sovereign and place control: federal, state, tribal, territorial, local, foreign, or several at once? | The controlling law, court or agency, authorized professionals, forms, remedies, and deadlines. |
| Legal posture | Is this a question, notice, investigation, charge, lawsuit, appeal, benefits decision, contract, complaint, or enforcement problem? | What must be answered, preserved, filed, served, exhausted, appealed, or negotiated—and by whom. |
| Clock | What is the earliest plausible deadline? | Whether there is time to research, seek counsel, request an extension, preserve a claim, answer a case, or appeal. |
| Goal | What concrete outcome would help? | Advice, safety, records, payment, repair, accommodation, dismissal, defense, injunction, benefits, license, status, settlement, appeal, or policy change. |
| Evidence | What reliable record exists? | Notices, envelopes, contracts, messages, photographs, medical or payment records, witnesses, metadata, timelines, and proof of delivery. |
| Help level | Is information, brief advice, document review, limited-scope help, or full representation needed? | Eligibility, price, attorney-client relationship, confidentiality, responsibility, and who appears or signs. |
| Forum | Who can actually grant the remedy? | A court, agency, regulator, ombuds, arbitrator, mediator, employer, school, platform, insurer, benefits office, legislature, or private party. |
| Cost and collection | What fees, exposure, and enforcement remain after a decision? | Filing, service, experts, discovery, fee shifting, taxes, appeal bonds, collection, insolvency, insurance, and practical value. |
| Privacy and access | What information or accommodation is needed to participate safely? | Privilege, public filings, redaction, interpreters, disability access, monitored devices, identity exposure, and data retention. |

### Know what kind of help you are using

| Layer | What it can do | What it usually cannot do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal information | Explain general rules, terms, forms, agencies, and research sources. | Apply the law reliably to all facts, create representation, or preserve a deadline. |
| Court clerk or self-help center | Explain procedure, provide official forms, accept filings, and identify public resources. | Recommend strategy, predict results, choose claims, or act as a lawyer. |
| Hotline or navigator | Triage, provide safety planning, explain options, and make referrals. | Guarantee confidentiality, acceptance, emergency response, or representation unless it explicitly says so. |
| Regulator or complaint portal | Receive reports, request a company response, investigate, mediate, or enforce public law. | Serve as the complainant's private lawyer, award every remedy, or pause another deadline. |
| Document assembly | Turn answers into a form or draft. | Verify every fact, local rule, edition, service step, legal consequence, or strategic choice. |
| Brief or limited-scope advice | Answer a defined question or perform specified tasks. | Handle everything outside the written scope or automatically appear in court. |
| Full representation | Advise, communicate, negotiate, file, and appear within a written engagement. | Guarantee an outcome or cover matters outside the agreement. |
| Public defender or appointed counsel | Defend qualifying people in covered criminal or related proceedings. | Provide general civil legal aid or continue into every appeal, collateral case, or civil problem. |

### Choose the path that fits

**Immediate danger or detention.** Contact local emergency services when safe. If arrested, questioned in custody, or charged, clearly request a lawyer and use the court's appointed-counsel process; ordinary civil legal aid is a different system. If a device may be monitored, use a safer device and remember that quick-exit buttons do not erase browser, call, text, email, cloud, or account history.

**A paper, hearing, removal, shutoff, charge, or deadline just arrived.** Read the exact notice and envelope, photograph or copy everything, calendar the earliest date, and contact the issuing clerk or agency plus qualified legal help today. Do not assume an intake request, complaint, payment plan, or conversation pauses the deadline.

**No money for a civil problem.** Begin with the Legal Services Corporation locator and LawHelp.org. Eligibility and capacity differ by program; submitting intake is not acceptance. Try several legitimate pathways without sending unnecessary sensitive information.

**A brief civil question.** ABA Free Legal Answers may provide limited advice in participating jurisdictions for eligible users. It does not cover criminal defense, promise a response, or create ongoing representation.

**A form or self-represented case.** Start at the exact court's official self-help and forms pages. Confirm the current edition, local rules, filing fee or waiver, service, signature or notarization, redaction, interpreter or accommodation request, and deadline.

**Hiring counsel.** Use a public-service bar referral or a specialty directory to discover candidates, then independently verify every jurisdictional license and public disciplinary record with the official regulator. Interview more than one lawyer when time permits and get scope, fees, costs, staffing, communication, files, termination, and refund terms in writing.

**Researching or following the law.** Begin with an official source, public law library, Cornell LII, or a nonprofit case-law tool. Secondary explanations and news help identify issues; only current controlling primary authority answers what the law says. Check jurisdiction, effective date, amendments, court level, later treatment, local rules, and whether a source is complete.

### Minimum viable legal response

1. Address immediate safety, detention, housing, health, or evidence-destruction risks without escalating danger.
2. Identify every possible jurisdiction, case or account number, party, notice, and earliest deadline.
3. Preserve originals and create a factual timeline with copies, dates, senders, recipients, witnesses, payments, and proof of delivery.
4. Find the official court, agency, contract, policy, or primary law that controls the process.
5. Seek the right level of qualified help and verify the person's license, authorization, scope, and conflicts before disclosing more than intake requires.
6. Choose the forum and remedy deliberately; a complaint, appeal, lawsuit, negotiation, and public report are not interchangeable.
7. Calendar filing, service, response, evidence, hearing, follow-up, appeal, and renewal dates; save confirmation receipts and current copies of everything submitted.

### Scam, privacy, and AI guardrails

- Verify a lawyer through the official licensing authority and call contact information obtained independently; scammers can impersonate a real lawyer, office, judge, court, or bar number.
- A United States notary or “notario,” document preparer, consultant, chatbot, or paralegal cannot independently choose legal strategy or forms unless separately authorized under the controlling jurisdiction. Immigration accreditation is a distinct system.
- “Free consultation” does not mean a free case. Get the retainer, limited scope, hourly or flat fee, contingency percentage, expenses, refund rules, and responsible people in writing.
- Never pay an unsolicited legal helper, court, police agency, or immigration service with gift cards, cryptocurrency, cash courier, or an unverifiable wire; never sign blank or false forms or surrender irreplaceable originals.
- Hotlines, directories, public forums, form tools, AI systems, initial intake, and communications with nonlawyers are not automatically attorney-client privileged. Avoid unnecessary Social Security, A-number, child, health, financial, intimate, or witness information.
- Court filings are often public. Use the court's redaction rules and secure filing path; do not assume deleting a local copy removes a public filing.
- AI can invent authorities, quotes, deadlines, and forms while sounding certain. Trace every legal proposition to a current primary source, read the source in context, check later treatment, and never file generated text without competent review.
- Guarantees, “special access,” pressure to act secretly, fake badges, paid government forms, demands to move off a verified channel, and refusal to provide a written agreement are serious warning signs.

## Urgent Help, Safety, and Deadlines

Immediate violence, fire, medical danger, or a crime in progress belongs with local emergency services when safe. Survivor, trafficking, sexual-assault, stalking, image-abuse, child-safety, and crime-victim pathways already curated in [Security](../security/README.md), [Health and Well-Being](../health-and-well-being/README.md), and [Public Programs and Services](../awesome-abundance/public-programs-and-services/README.md) provide confidential support and referrals, but they are not automatically emergency dispatch, legal counsel, or privileged communications.

- [DOJ Hate Crimes Get Help Now](https://www.justice.gov/hatecrimes/get-help-now) - Official United States reporting, local-FBI, emergency, victim-assistance, and civil-rights pathways for hate crimes; a report is not private representation or deadline protection.
- [National Crime Victim Law Institute](https://ncvli.org/) - Nonprofit crime-victim-rights research, education, technical assistance, and attorney-network resources; it is not emergency dispatch or a general promise of individual representation.
- [OVC Directory of Crime Victim Services](https://www.ovc.ojp.gov/directory-crime-victim-services/search) - Official searchable directory of victim-service programs in the United States and abroad; listings and intake should be independently confirmed.
- [VictimLaw](https://victimlaw.org/) - Free Office for Victims of Crime-supported database of federal and state crime-victim statutes, constitutional provisions, rules, and decisions; it supplies legal information rather than counsel.
- [WomensLaw](https://www.womenslaw.org/) - Survivor-centered state and federal information on abuse, protection orders, custody, housing, immigration, and local help, with a separate attorney-reviewed email hotline; it is not emergency dispatch or guaranteed representation.
- [WomensLaw Email Hotline](https://hotline.womenslaw.org/public) - Account-based written questions reviewed by attorneys for United States users, generally answered after a delay; messages may leave device traces and do not toll deadlines or create representation.

## Find Free and Low-Cost Legal Help

Civil legal aid is not one universal entitlement. Programs may screen income, household, assets, residence, citizenship or eligible status, subject, conflict, case posture, likely benefit, funder restrictions, and capacity. Intake is not acceptance, and “no” from one organization does not mean every legitimate pathway is closed.

- [ABA Directory of Law School Public Interest and Pro Bono Programs](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/center-pro-bono/resources/directory_of_law_school_public_interest_pro_bono_programs/) - Directory for identifying nearby law-school clinics and programs; public intake, subjects, semesters, supervision, income, and geography vary by school.
- [ABA Home Front](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/legal_services/milvets/aba_home_front/) - Military and veteran legal-information library plus service directories; eligibility, subject, local capacity, and representation vary.
- [ImmigrationLawHelp.org](https://www.immigrationlawhelp.org/) - Nonprofit directory of free or low-cost authorized immigration providers searchable by place, detention facility, issue, and language; confirm current intake, authorization, fees, and capacity.
- [Pro Bono Net](https://www.probono.net/) - Nonprofit network supporting statewide legal-help portals, volunteer-lawyer projects, disaster aid, and technology for access to justice; most individual help is delivered through linked local programs.
- [Stateside Legal](https://www.statesidelegal.org/) - Free legal information and provider navigation for United States service members, veterans, and families; issue, status, income, location, and capacity rules vary.

## Find and Verify a Lawyer

A directory is a discovery layer, not proof of fit, authorization, insurance, competence, discipline history, conflicts, price, or outcome. Verify every jurisdiction where advice or appearance is required through the official regulator, then ask about direct experience with the issue, forum, opposing party, accessibility, language, timeline, staffing, fees, and realistic options.

- [ABA Hire a Lawyer](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/legal_services/flh-home/flh-hire-a-lawyer/) - Public-service referral, bar-directory, and prepaid-plan orientation; referral programs may charge a modest consultation fee and later work is normally paid.
- [ABA Lawyer Licensing Directory](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/legal_services/flh-home/flh-lawyer-licensing/) - Links every United States state licensing authority for current status and public discipline checks; confirm each jurisdiction independently.
- [AILA Lawyer Search](https://www.ailalawyer.com/) - American Immigration Lawyers Association member directory; membership is not government authorization or a guarantee, so verify bar status and immigration experience.
- [Avvo Lawyer Directory](https://www.avvo.com/find-a-lawyer) - Commercial lawyer profiles, reviews, ratings, and sponsored visibility; verify claims, licensing, discipline, fee terms, and who receives an inquiry independently.
- [FindLaw Lawyer Directory](https://lawyers.findlaw.com/) - Commercial practice-area and location directory with advertising and lead-generation features; official regulator records remain authoritative.
- [Justia Lawyer Directory](https://www.justia.com/lawyers) - Commercial directory with free and paid profiles, reviews, practice areas, and jurisdictions; a listing is not vetting or endorsement.
- [Martindale Lawyer Directory](https://www.martindale.com/) - Commercial lawyer and firm directory with peer and client ratings; ratings and profiles do not replace regulator, conflict, insurance, or fee checks.
- [NACDL Find a Lawyer](https://www.nacdl.org/Content/FindaLawyer) - National Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers member directory; membership does not establish appointment eligibility, specialization, price, or fit.
- [National Academy of Elder Law Attorneys Find a Lawyer](https://www.naela.org/Web/Shared_Content/Directories/Find-a-Lawyer.aspx) - Member directory for elder and special-needs law; verify state license, relevant experience, credentials, and fee structure.
- [National Association of Consumer Advocates Find an Attorney](https://www.consumeradvocates.org/findanattorney/) - Member directory for consumer matters such as debt, credit, vehicles, fraud, and abusive practices; service scope, fees, and acceptance vary.
- [National Association of Consumer Bankruptcy Attorneys Attorney Finder](https://www.nacba.org/find-an-attorney/) - Member directory for consumer bankruptcy counsel; compare chapters, alternatives, total attorney and court costs, and local experience.
- [National Crime Victim Bar Association Attorney Referral](https://victimbar.org/ars-form) - Referral request for civil attorneys representing crime victims; submitting information does not create representation or guarantee a match.
- [National Employment Lawyers Association Find a Lawyer](https://engagement.nela.org/NELA/findalawyer) - Member directory focused on employee-side workplace matters; membership, profile, and intake do not guarantee case acceptance or quality.
- [NOSSCR Lawyer Referral Service](https://nosscr.org/referral-service/) - National Organization of Social Security Claimants' Representatives referral pathway for disability claims and appeals; representatives may be attorneys or eligible nonattorneys and fee rules apply.
- [Special Needs Alliance Find an Attorney](https://www.specialneedsalliance.org/find-an-attorney/) - Invitation-based member directory focused on disability and public-benefits planning; membership is not a public regulator or outcome guarantee.

## Working With a Lawyer

- [ABA Client Protection Funds](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/legal_services/flh-home/flh-trouble-with-a-lawyer/flh-client-protection/) - State fund directory for some losses caused by dishonest lawyers; it generally does not cover malpractice, ordinary fee disputes, or every loss.
- [ABA Fee Arbitration](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/legal_services/flh-home/flh-trouble-with-a-lawyer/flh-fee-arbitration/) - State and local programs for resolving lawyer-client fee disputes, often free or low-cost; participation and binding effect vary.
- [ABA Law Issues for Consumers](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/public_education/resources/law_issues_for_consumers/) - Plain-language legal and working-with-lawyers orientation across common life problems; articles can age and are not individualized advice.
- [ABA Model Rules of Professional Conduct](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/) - Influential model ethics rules on competence, confidentiality, conflicts, fees, communication, safekeeping, and withdrawal; the rules actually adopted in the licensing jurisdiction control.
- [ABA Trouble With a Lawyer](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/legal_services/flh-home/flh-trouble-with-a-lawyer/) - Routes users to discipline agencies, fee arbitration, and client-protection funds while explaining that those remedies address different problems.
- [ABA When You Need a Lawyer](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/public_education/resources/public-information/when-you-need-a-lawyer/) - Consumer orientation for recognizing when counsel may be useful and preparing to hire; verify any time-sensitive decision locally.

Before signing, identify the client, exact matter and exclusions, responsible lawyer and staff, jurisdiction, tasks, communication cadence, decision authority, hourly or flat rates, contingency percentage, retainers, expenses, experts, fee shifting, file access, trust-account handling, settlement authority, termination, withdrawal, refund, lien, appeal, and data or AI use. Keep the signed agreement and invoices. “No recovery, no fee” can still leave expenses or liens, and a limited-scope lawyer is responsible only for the written tasks.

## Courts, Forms, and Self-Representation

Self-representation can be rational for some routine or low-stakes matters and dangerous for others. The clerk can explain procedure but not choose claims, defenses, evidence, strategy, or settlement. Local rules and judge-specific requirements can control even when a national form exists.

- [Access to Justice Network](https://a2jnetwork.org/) - Successor community to the Self-Represented Litigation Network, with research, events, working groups, court-service models, and practitioner resources for user-centered access; it is not individual counsel.
- [Federal Court Finder](https://www.uscourts.gov/federal-court-finder/find) - Official locator for federal district, bankruptcy, appellate, and specialty courts plus their local websites; it does not select venue or find state courts.
- [Federal Court Fee Waiver Forms](https://www.uscourts.gov/forms-rules/forms/fee-waiver-application-forms) - Official applications to ask a federal court to proceed without prepaying fees or costs; approval is not automatic and sensitive financial disclosure is required.
- [LawHelp Interactive Forms](https://www.lawhelp.org/findforms) - Free guided interviews that assemble forms for participating states and topics; saving may require an account and users must verify edition, jurisdiction, filing, service, and privacy.
- [PACER](https://pacer.uscourts.gov/) - Official account-based access to federal court dockets and documents with usage fees and limited waiver or exemption paths; it does not cover state courts and filings are often public.
- [PACER Access Options When Fees Are Unaffordable](https://pacer.uscourts.gov/my-account-billing/billing/options-access-records-if-you-cannot-afford-pacer-fees) - Official explanation of court-granted exemptions, courthouse terminals, opinion access, and other lower-cost federal-record pathways.
- [U.S. Courts Forms](https://www.uscourts.gov/forms-rules/forms) - Official national federal forms by category; check the specific court first because local forms, instructions, rules, fees, and electronic-filing policies may control.
- [U.S. Courts Understanding the Federal Courts](https://www.uscourts.gov/about-federal-courts/court-role-and-structure) - Official explanation of federal court roles, levels, judges, jurisdiction, and the difference from state systems.

## Mediation, Arbitration, Ombuds, and Complaints

Mediation uses a neutral facilitator; arbitration gives a private decision-maker power defined by an agreement or law; an ombuds may investigate, navigate, recommend, or informally resolve within a defined system; and a regulator protects a public mandate. None is automatically the user's lawyer. A signed settlement or arbitration award can bind, compelled arbitration may replace court or jury access, and safety screening matters where coercion or violence is present.

- [American Arbitration Association Consumer Arbitration](https://www.adr.org/consumer) - Rules, fees, due-process standards, filing, and case information for consumer disputes administered by AAA; arbitration clauses, cost allocation, deadlines, and court-review limits require careful review.
- [DOJ ADA Mediation Program](https://www.ada.gov/mediation-qa/) - Free voluntary mediation when the Department of Justice selects and refers an eligible ADA complaint; it is not on-demand mediation, enforcement, or personal counsel.
- [EEOC Mediation](https://www.eeoc.gov/questions-and-answers-about-mediation) - Free voluntary and confidential mediation for eligible employment-discrimination charges when both sides agree; filing a charge, eligibility, and investigation remain separate.
- [Federal Mediation and Conciliation Service](https://www.fmcs.gov/services/) - Official labor-management mediation, training, arbitration-roster, and conflict-resolution services; jurisdiction, fees, and eligibility depend on the service.
- [FINRA Dispute Resolution](https://www.finra.org/arbitration-mediation) - Official securities-industry arbitration and mediation forum for covered investor, broker, and employment disputes; filing fees, eligibility, time limits, agreements, and award-review rules apply.
- [NAFCM Locate a Program](https://www.nafcm.org/page/Locate_A_Program) - National Association for Community Mediation directory of nonprofit and public local centers; subjects, languages, fees, remote access, safety screening, and capacity vary.
- [USDA Certified Mediation Program](https://www.fsa.usda.gov/resources/general-programs/certified-mediation-program) - Official state mediation pathway for covered agricultural, credit, conservation, and rural disputes; certified programs may charge a nominal fee and participation rules vary.
- [USA.gov Complaints](https://www.usa.gov/complaints) - Official router for complaints about businesses, products, government agencies, discrimination, housing, travel, and other systems; the receiving body and remedy differ by subject.
- [United States Ombudsman Association](https://www.usombudsman.org/) - Public-sector ombuds professional association with standards, education, and member context; it is not a universal complaint portal and each office's mandate differs.

## Primary Law, Cases, Dockets, and Research

Research from secondary sources toward primary authority: issue and search terms → governing jurisdiction → current constitution, code, regulation, rule, or treaty → relevant decisions and later treatment → local procedure → facts and remedy. ## Immigration, Citizenship, and Refugee Help

United States immigration proceedings generally do not provide appointed government counsel. Only a licensed attorney or properly accredited representative may give legal advice; a notary or “notario,” visa consultant, travel agent, or document preparer is not authorized merely by that title. Keep every notice, confirm the court or agency address, and protect hearing, filing, appeal, and address-change deadlines.

- [ASAP Asylum Seeker Advocacy Project Resources](https://help.asylumadvocacy.org/) - Free multilingual guides, updates, and community resources for asylum seekers; membership and direct legal services have separate limits.
- [EOIR Automated Case Information](https://acis.eoir.justice.gov/en/) - Official limited immigration-court status lookup using an A-number and nationality; protect the identifier and treat written court or Board notices as authoritative.
- [EOIR Can Someone Represent You](https://www.justice.gov/eoir/can-someone-represent-you-eoir) - Official explanation of attorneys, fully and partially accredited representatives, law students, reputable individuals, and prohibited representation before immigration courts and the Board.
- [EOIR Find Legal Representation](https://www.justice.gov/eoir/find-legal-representation) - Official hub for pro bono providers, recognized organizations and accredited representatives, and disciplined or ineligible practitioners; listing is not endorsement or guaranteed intake.
- [EOIR Pro Bono Legal Service Providers](https://www.justice.gov/eoir/list-pro-bono-legal-service-providers) - Court-specific official list of nonprofit organizations and lawyers who may provide free services in immigration proceedings; contact and acceptance rules vary.
- [EOIR Respondent Access](https://respondentaccess.eoir.justice.gov/en/) - Official portal for case details, forms, and EOIR-33 court or Board contact updates, generally required within five working days; file separately for every affected person and forum.
- [ICE Detention Reporting and Information Line](https://portal.ice.gov/detainee/facility-detention) - Official 1-888-351-4024 information, legal-access, disability, family-separation, custody-condition, abuse, and trafficking support line with interpreters; it is not appointed counsel.
- [ICE Online Change of Address](https://portal.ice.gov/ocoa) - Official address-update tool for released people reporting to ICE; people in detention do not use it, and EOIR, USCIS, appointments, and hearings require separate action.
- [ICE Online Detainee Locator](https://locator.ice.gov/odls/#/search) - Official search for adults in ICE custody and some people held by CBP longer than 48 hours; people under 18 are excluded, records can lag, and families can use the ICE information line or ORR National Call Center at 800-203-7001 as appropriate.
- [Immi](https://www.immi.org/) - Free nonprofit screening, rights information, learning center, and legal-help connections for some immigration options; results are not a status determination or legal advice.
- [National Immigration Law Center Resources](https://www.nilc.org/resources/) - Nonprofit legal and policy materials on immigrant rights, benefits, work, health, enforcement, and litigation; NILC is not a general direct-services provider.
- [USCIS Avoid Immigration Scams](https://www.uscis.gov/scams-fraud-and-misconduct/avoid-scams) - Official warning signs, authorized-helper rules, form and fee facts, and reporting paths for notario, impersonation, payment, and document scams.
- [USCIS Change of Address](https://www.uscis.gov/addresschange) - Official online and paper address-update guidance, generally required within ten days for people subject to registration rules; include every pending receipt and review exceptions.
- [USCIS Find Legal Services](https://www.uscis.gov/scams-fraud-and-misconduct/avoid-scams/find-legal-services) - Official locator pathway to free or low-cost lawyers and DOJ-recognized organizations; confirm authorization and current intake.

Updating USCIS does not update ICE or EOIR, and updating ICE does not update the immigration court, Board, USCIS, appointments, or hearing location. Follow each notice and file every required update. If EOIR sends notice to the address on record and a person misses a hearing, the court can proceed and order removal in the person's absence. The primary nationwide nonprofit directory is [ImmigrationLawHelp.org](#find-free-and-low-cost-legal-help). Refugees, asylum seekers, and stateless people outside the United States can begin with [UNHCR Help](#international-comparative-and-human-rights-law).

Grievance exhaustion, direct appeal, habeas, parole, disciplinary, innocence, and civil-rights deadlines are separate.

- [Jailhouse Lawyer's Handbook](https://www.jailhouselaw.org/) - Free guide to federal civil-rights lawsuits challenging prison or jail conditions; it is not individualized advice and exhaustion, filing, fee, and remedy rules are complex.

- [Restoration of Rights Project](https://ccresourcecenter.org/state-restoration-profiles/) - State profiles on record relief, pardons, voting, firearms, licensing, employment, and other restoration mechanisms; verify current statutes, forms, and local practice.

- [U.S. Courts CJA Appointment Guidance](https://www.uscourts.gov/administration-policies/judiciary-policies/guidelines-administering-cja-and-related-statutes-5) - Official federal policy on timely appointed counsel for financially eligible people in covered proceedings; the court determines eligibility and scope.

- [New Zealand Get Legal Aid](https://www.justice.govt.nz/courts/going-to-court/legal-aid/get-legal-aid/) - Official criminal, family, and civil legal-aid pathway; eligibility applies and some grants may be repayable depending on circumstances.
- [UNHCR Help](https://help.unhcr.org/) - Official country selector for asylum seekers, refugees, and stateless people, often including rights, legal partners, detention or deportation contacts, and service routes; availability and privacy conditions are local.

### National and comparative law gateways

- [AfricanLII](https://africanlii.org/) - Free African legislation, judgments, law journals, gazettes, and national LII connections; coverage, currency, source status, and languages vary by jurisdiction.
- [AustLII](https://www.austlii.edu.au/) - Free Australasian legislation, cases, treaties, journals, and legal databases; verify official text, amendments, publication status, and current treatment.
- [BAILII](https://www.bailii.org/) - Charitable free British and Irish judgments and legislation across multiple jurisdictions; coverage differs by court and year and issuing authorities control.
- [CanLII](https://www.canlii.org/) - Nonprofit Canadian judgments, tribunal decisions, consolidated legislation, commentary, and citator tools; historical and unpublished coverage varies by jurisdiction.
- [CommonLII](https://www.commonlii.org/) - Free search across Commonwealth and common-law legal databases; contributor coverage and currency vary, so verify national official sources.
- [Constitute](https://www.constituteproject.org/) - Free searchable and comparable constitutions with topic tagging, versions, and downloads; translations and amendment currency require official verification.
- [GlobaLex](https://www.nyulawglobal.org/globalex) - Free expert research guides for foreign, comparative, and international law with revision dates and source pathways; it is secondary guidance and links can age.
- [NZLII](https://www.nzlii.org/) - Free New Zealand cases, legislation, reports, and legal scholarship; verify official versions and later developments.
- [SAFLII](https://www.saflii.org/) - Free Southern African judgments and legal material across multiple countries and regional bodies; coverage and official status vary.
- [WorldLII](https://www.worldlii.org/) - Free cross-jurisdiction search and gateway connecting national Legal Information Institutes, treaties, decisions, and secondary sources; completeness and authenticity vary by contributor.

### Treaties, international courts, and human rights

- [African Court on Human and Peoples' Rights](https://www.african-court.org/) - Official cases, decisions, basic texts, hearings, publications, and state-participation information; jurisdiction and individual or NGO access depend on treaty declarations and procedure.
- [Court of Justice of the European Union](https://curia.europa.eu/) - Official judgments, orders, opinions, cases, calendars, procedural material, and multilingual search for the Court of Justice and General Court.
- [ECOLEX](https://www.ecolex.org/) - Free FAO, IUCN, and UNEP gateway to environmental treaties, laws, cases, literature, and country profiles; source status and update timing vary.
- [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/) - Official European Union treaties, legislation, preparatory acts, case law, agreements, and authentic Official Journal; consolidated text is a convenience and national transposition may matter.
- [FAOLEX](https://www.fao.org/faolex/) - Free FAO database of national laws, regulations, policies, and treaties on food, agriculture, natural resources, land, fisheries, and environment; translations and coverage vary.
- [HUDOC](https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/) - Official European Court of Human Rights judgments, decisions, communicated cases, advisory material, filters, RSS, and exports; most non-English or French translations are unofficial.
- [Inter-American Court of Human Rights](https://www.corteidh.or.cr/) - Official judgments, orders, advisory opinions, hearings, publications, and case information; Spanish is the primary working language and jurisdiction rules apply.
- [International Court of Justice Cases](https://www.icj-cij.org/cases) - Official applications, pleadings, hearings, orders, judgments, and advisory opinions; contentious jurisdiction is between consenting states and advisory opinions generally differ in legal effect.
- [International Criminal Court Legal Tools](https://legal-tools.org/) - Free ICC and international-criminal-law decisions, treaties, national materials, and research collections; inclusion is not endorsement and issuing-body originals control.
- [NATLEX](https://natlex.ilo.org/) - Free International Labour Organization database of national labor, social-security, and related laws by country and subject; source language, translations, and currency vary.
- [OHCHR Treaty Body Jurisprudence](https://juris.ohchr.org/) - Official database of United Nations treaty-body decisions and views on individual communications; admissibility, state participation, legal effect, language, and coverage differ.
- [Refworld](https://www.refworld.org/) - UNHCR-curated refugee, statelessness, asylum, country, policy, legislation, and case material; it mixes official and analytical sources, so inspect provenance and date.
- [United Nations Digital Library](https://digitallibrary.un.org/) - Official search for UN documents, resolutions, voting records, speeches, maps, and metadata; document series, revisions, language versions, and organ authority matter.
- [United Nations Treaty Collection](https://treaties.un.org/) - Official depositary status, notifications, certified text, treaty series, reservations, declarations, and entry-into-force information for covered multilateral treaties.
- [United Nations Audiovisual Library of International Law](https://legal.un.org/avl/) - Free historic archives, lectures, research library, and legal instruments produced by the UN Office of Legal Affairs; educational content is not a currentness service.
- [WIPO Lex](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/) - Free international intellectual-property treaties, national laws, judgments, and member profiles; verify current domestic implementation and official text.

## Professional Research and Practice Systems

These systems explain what law firms, legal departments, schools, agencies, and some libraries buy: editorial citators, treatises, practice guidance, dockets, analytics, news, document tools, client management, billing, and discovery. Access is usually contractual and package-limited. Compare included jurisdictions, content, seats, storage, exports, overages, training, auto-renewal, retention, confidentiality, security, AI terms, and offboarding. Never treat AI-generated analysis as verified authority.

### Research, citators, and guidance

- [Bloomberg Law](https://pro.bloomberglaw.com/) - Paid integrated primary and secondary law, dockets, BNA news, practical guidance, analytics, business intelligence, and AI; demo, contract, and institutional access vary.
- [HeinOnline](https://heinonline.com/) - Subscription true-to-print journals, historical government documents, treaties, cases, and specialty archives commonly available through academic or public law libraries; it is not a complete live docket or citator.
- [Legal Bluebook](https://www.legalbluebook.com/) - Proprietary legal-citation manual and online subscription used by many United States courts and journals; local rules may control and free alternatives appear earlier in this guide.
- [Lexis+](https://www.lexisnexis.com/en-us/products/lexis-plus.page) - Paid primary and secondary law, Shepard's citator, practical guidance, news, analytics, and AI options; packages, trials, library access, and document limits vary.
- [Practical Law](https://legal.thomsonreuters.com/en/products/practical-law) - Paid practice notes, standard documents, clauses, checklists, trackers, and jurisdiction comparisons; templates require factual, legal, and local review.
- [vLex](https://vlex.com/) - Clio-owned global legal research, Fastcase, Docket Alarm, analytics, and AI across many jurisdictions; coverage, tiers, trials, and official-source status vary.
- [Westlaw](https://legal.thomsonreuters.com/en/westlaw) - Paid primary and secondary law, KeyCite, editorial material, litigation tools, document analysis, and AI; content, overages, contracts, and institutional access vary.

### Practice management and discovery

- [MyCase](https://www.mycase.com/) - Paid law-practice management, intake, communication, documents, calendars, billing, payments, and accounting tools; tier, migration, support, and client-data controls vary.
- [PracticePanther](https://www.practicepanther.com/) - Paid cloud case, contact, calendar, document, time, billing, payment, workflow, and client-portal system; integrations and features depend on plan.
- [Relativity](https://www.relativity.com/) - Enterprise e-discovery, investigations, legal hold, review, analytics, and AI platform delivered through cloud and partners; deployment, certifications, pricing, and data governance vary.
- [Smokeball](https://www.smokeball.com/) - Paid small-law-firm practice management with document automation, matter content, time, billing, intake, and AI features; jurisdictional libraries and plan terms vary.

## Legal Technology, Data, and Access to Justice

These projects build public tools, court forms, datasets, measurement, and infrastructure. A software license or open dataset does not make generated legal content accurate, secure, maintained, accessible, or authorized in a jurisdiction. Deployments need lawyer-reviewed content, threat modeling, privacy, accessibility, plain-language testing, versioning, backups, maintenance, and an exit path.

- [Bureau of Justice Statistics](https://bjs.ojp.gov/) - Official crime, courts, corrections, law-enforcement, victimization, tribal-justice, and justice-system data, publications, downloads, and methodology.
- [Civil Rights Litigation Clearinghouse](https://clearinghouse.net/) - Free searchable University of Michigan archive of documents and structured information from major civil-rights cases; coverage is selective and official dockets control.
- [Court Statistics Project](https://www.ncsc.org/resources-courts/data) - National Center for State Courts data and dashboards on state court caseloads, clearance, dispositions, and trends; jurisdictional definitions and reporting completeness differ.
- [docassemble](https://docassemble.org/) - Free open-source guided-interview, expert-system, document-assembly, signature, multilingual, and API framework; hosting and legally valid content require expertise and ongoing work.
- [Free Law Project](https://free.law/) - Nonprofit organization behind CourtListener, RECAP, citation tools, legal datasets, and advocacy for accessible court records.
- [IAALS](https://iaals.du.edu/) - Nonpartisan institute researching and testing civil justice, family justice, legal education, regulation, and user-centered court reform; projects are not direct representation.
- [LSC Technology Initiative Grant Program](https://www.lsc.gov/grants/technology-initiative-grant-program) - Grants, reports, and community resources for technology used by LSC-funded legal-aid organizations; funding is not available directly to individuals.
- [Measures for Justice](https://measuresforjustice.org/) - Nonprofit local criminal-justice data, methodology, dashboards, and data-capacity work; coverage and agency participation vary and metrics require context.
- [National Center for Access to Justice](https://ncaj.org/) - Research and policy organization measuring access to civil and criminal justice, including the state Justice Index; it does not provide individual legal help.
- [NCAJ Justice Index](https://ncaj.org/state-rankings/justice-index) - State policy and source-data comparisons on attorney access, self-help, language and disability access, fines and fees, and consumer debt; component years and currentness vary.
- [NLADA](https://www.nlada.org/) - National Legal Aid and Defender Association training, policy, standards, events, and community for civil legal aid and public defense; it is not a national client-intake portal.
- [Stanford Legal Design Lab](https://legaltechdesign.com/) - Academic legal-design research and prototypes for court, eviction, debt, privacy, and access-to-justice problems; projects vary in maturity and availability.
- [Suffolk LIT Lab](https://suffolklitlab.org/) - Academic lab creating open-source guided interviews, court forms, data, and legal-service tools; experimental projects require local legal and technical validation.
- [World Justice Project](https://worldjusticeproject.org/) - Independent global rule-of-law data, country profiles, surveys, reports, methods, and programs; index scores summarize perceptions and experiences rather than resolve an individual case.
