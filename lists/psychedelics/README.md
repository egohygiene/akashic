## Research Centers and Institutes

- [Beckley Foundation](https://www.beckleyfoundation.org/) - Coordinates psychedelic neuroscience research and drug-policy work through a United Kingdom nonprofit.
- [Center for Psychedelic Drug Research & Education](https://www.cpdre.org/) - Conducts interdisciplinary research, education, and crisis-response workforce training at Ohio State.
- [CU Denver Center for Psychedelic Research](https://clas.ucdenver.edu/psychedelic-research/) - Studies mechanisms, public health, policy, and regulated facilitation through a transdisciplinary center.
- [Exeter Psychedelic Interdisciplinary Centre](https://www.exeter.ac.uk/research-centres/psychedelic-interdisciplinary-centre/) - Joins neuroscience, medicine, philosophy, anthropology, history, and social research in a United Kingdom academic hub.
- [Heffter Research Institute](https://www.heffter.org/) - Designs, reviews, and funds academic research on classic psychedelics through a nonprofit institute.
- [Imperial Centre for Psychedelic Research](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/psychedelic-research-centre/) - Conducts clinical psychopharmacology, neuroimaging, consciousness, and mental-health research in the United Kingdom.
- [Johns Hopkins Center for Psychedelic and Consciousness Research](https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/psychiatry/research/psychedelics-research) - Studies effects on brain function, cognition, behavior, and potential clinical applications through controlled research.
- [Mass General Center for the Neuroscience of Psychedelics](https://www.massgeneral.org/psychiatry/treatments-and-services/center-for-the-neuroscience-of-psychedelics) - Uses neuroimaging, chemical neurobiology, and psychiatry to study psychedelic-induced plasticity.
- [Michigan Psychedelic Center](https://michiganpsychedelic.med.umich.edu/) - Spans neuroscience, therapy research, education, and community engagement at the University of Michigan.
- [Monash Clinical Psychedelic Lab](https://www.monash.edu/medicine/scs/clinical-psychedelic-lab) - Focuses on trials, risks, best practices, training, access, and affordability at an Australian university.
- [NYU Langone Center for Psychedelic Medicine](https://med.nyu.edu/departments-institutes/psychiatry/research/psychedelic-medicine) - Runs preclinical through late-phase research and trains scientists in psychedelic medicine.
- [The Parsons Research Center for Psychedelic Healing](https://icahn.mssm.edu/research/center-psychedelic-parsons-research) - Studies psychedelic-assisted approaches to PTSD and trauma-related conditions through Mount Sinai and the VA.
- [UC Berkeley Center for the Science of Psychedelics](https://psychedelics.berkeley.edu/) - Combines basic science, clinical research, journalism, public education, and policy resources.
- [UC Davis Institute for Psychedelics and Neurotherapeutics](https://neurotherapeutics.ucdavis.edu/) - Studies neurobiology and develops psychedelic-inspired neurotherapeutic tools and compounds.
- [UC San Diego Center for Psychedelic Research](https://cpr.ucsd.edu/) - Supports cross-species and translational research on psychological, physiological, and neural effects.
- [UCSF Translational Psychedelic Research Program](https://psychedelics.ucsf.edu/) - Investigates psychedelics across brain, body, clinical, and translational research using open-science principles.
- [Usona Institute](https://www.usonainstitute.org/) - Supports clinical research, investigator training, and investigational-material supply through a nonprofit institute.
- [UW–Madison Center for Psychedelic Research and Education](https://research.pharmacy.wisc.edu/cpre/) - Supports clinical, basic, cultural, legal, and educational research across the university.
- [Yale Program for Psychedelic Science](https://medicine.yale.edu/psychiatry/research/clinics-and-programs/psychedelic/) - Connects multidisciplinary research in neuroscience, psychology, therapeutics, society, and education.

## Literature and Clinical-Trial Discovery

- [Australian New Zealand Clinical Trials Registry](https://www.anzctr.org.au/) - Provides a WHO-recognized primary registry for Australian, New Zealand, and international trials.
- [Ayahuasca Research Portal](https://www.ayahuasca-research.com/) - Indexes DOI- or PubMed-verified ayahuasca papers independently; verify metadata against the original publication.
- [Blossom Psychedelic Research Papers](https://blossomanalysis.com/papers/) - Organizes a paper database with study context and linked trial metadata; some broader features are paid.
- [Clinical Trials Information System](https://euclinicaltrials.eu/) - Opens the official public gateway to searchable European Union and European Economic Area trial records.
- [ClinicalTrials.gov: Psychedelic Studies](https://clinicaltrials.gov/search?term=psychedelic) - Searches the official United States registry and results database for relevant clinical studies.
- [Europe PMC: Psychedelic Research](https://europepmc.org/search?query=psychedelic) - Searches papers, preprints, grants, and cited datasets across the life sciences.
- [Journal of Psychedelic Studies](https://akjournals.com/view/journals/2054/2054-overview.xml) - Publishes peer-reviewed open-access scientific and interdisciplinary research; authors may face an article-processing charge.
- [MAPS Psychedelic Bibliography](https://bibliography.maps.org/) - Provides a long-running searchable bibliography whose update cadence should be checked against newer databases.
- [PubMed: Psychedelic Research](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=psychedelic) - Searches the National Library of Medicine's biomedical index; verify study design and publication type.
- [UC Berkeley Clinical Trials Map](https://psychedelics.berkeley.edu/clinical-trials-map/) - Maps and filters studies sourced from ClinicalTrials.gov; confirm status in the original registry.
- [WHO International Clinical Trials Registry Platform](https://trialsearch.who.int/) - Searches trial records supplied by qualifying registries worldwide.

## Open Data and Research Tools

- [Altered States Database](https://asdb.info/) - Provides an open-science psychometric database built from peer-reviewed studies and updated through systematic reviews.
- [Blossom Originals](https://blossomanalysis.com/originals/) - Offers a downloadable dataset mapping controlled human trials, unique samples, reanalyses, dosing, and outcomes.
- [LSD Alters the Effects of Brain Stimulation in Rodents](https://doi.gin.g-node.org/10.12751/g-node.xrxt9n/) - Shares a CC0 electrophysiology and machine-learning dataset with linked analysis code.
- [Psilocybin Precision Functional Mapping](https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds006072/versions/1.1.0) - Shares the CC0 BIDS neuroimaging dataset behind research on acute and persistent psilocybin-related brain changes.

## Harm Reduction, Safety, and Participant Support

- [Coalition for Psychedelic Safety and Education](https://www.coalitionforpsychedelicsafety.org/) - Promotes balanced risk communication, public education, and evidence-based policy through a nonprofit coalition.
- [DrugsData.org](https://www.drugsdata.org/index.php) - Searches Erowid's independent laboratory-results archive; check current submission availability before sending a sample.
- [Fireside Project Psychedelic Support Line](https://firesideproject.org/support-line) - Offers free, confidential, non-clinical peer support in the United States during or after experiences; it is not an emergency service.
- [ICEERS Integration Sessions for Challenging Experiences](https://www.iceers.org/en/support-and-integration/) - Offers a limited number of free online risk-reduction sessions worldwide, subject to capacity; it is not therapy or emergency care.
- [International Energy Control](https://energycontrol-international.org/) - Provides paid international testing and harm-reduction information subject to jurisdiction, shipping, and current analytical-coverage restrictions.
- [Perception Restoration Foundation](https://www.perception.foundation/) - Supports HPPD research, awareness, and resources through a volunteer-run nonprofit that is not an urgent-response service.
- [Poison Control: Magic Mushrooms](https://www.poison.org/articles/magic-mushrooms) - Explains poisoning risks and connects United States readers to poison-help services; emergencies still require local emergency care.
- [PsyCare UK Welfare & Harm Reduction](https://www.psycareuk.org/) - Provides festival crisis support, education, and date-dependent integration circles, primarily in the United Kingdom.
- [Psychedelic Participant Advocacy Network](https://www.psypanglobal.org/) - Advocates for safeguards in legal treatment and clinical trials through a lived-experience-led network; it is not a crisis service.
- [Psilocybin for Mental Health and Addiction: What You Need To Know](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/psilocybin-for-mental-health-and-addiction-what-you-need-to-know) - Summarizes NIH evidence, research limits, adverse effects, and interaction risks.
- [SAMHSA Hallucinogens](https://www.samhsa.gov/substance-use/learn/hallucinogens) - Explains hallucinogen risks, warning signs, and treatment resources through a United States government source.
- [Trans-European Drug Information Network](https://tedinetwork.org/) - Shares drug-checking methods, literature, services, and aggregated trend information through a European network.
- [The Loop Drug Checking](https://www.wearetheloop.org/drug-checking) - Provides free licensed community testing in selected United Kingdom cities, with location and sample-handling restrictions.
- [WEDINOS](https://wedinos.wales/) - Publishes anonymous testing results and trend data with Wales-only submissions and possible reporting delays.
- [Zendo Project](https://zendoproject.org/) - Provides peer-support education, event services, a free introductory course, and paid live training.

## Law, Policy, and Regulated Access

- [Colorado Natural Medicine Program](https://dpo.colorado.gov/NaturalMedicine) - Provides official facilitator licensing, training-program approval, rules, forms, and consumer information for Colorado.
- [European Union Drugs Agency FAQ: Therapeutic Use of Psychedelic Substances](https://www.euda.europa.eu/publications/frequently-asked-questions-faq/faq-therapeutic-use-psychedelic-substances_en) - Reviews European evidence, risks, and regulation; verify newer national and European Union rules separately.
- [FDA Psychedelic Drugs: Considerations for Clinical Investigations](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/psychedelic-drugs-considerations-clinical-investigations) - Gives nonbinding federal guidance for sponsors designing clinical investigations; it does not constitute drug approval.
- [Health Canada: Psilocybin and Psilocin](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/substance-use/controlled-illegal-drugs/magic-mushrooms.html) - Explains Canadian risks, legal status, exemptions, and case-by-case Special Access Program pathways.
- [Oregon Psilocybin Services](https://www.oregon.gov/oha/ph/preventionwellness/pages/psilocybin-what-are-psilocybin-services.aspx) - Explains official rules for supervised adult services at licensed Oregon centers while noting federal legal status.
- [Australian TGA MDMA and Psilocybine Access Pathways](https://www.tga.gov.au/products/unapproved-therapeutic-goods/mdma-and-psilocybine/access-pathways) - Describes access through authorized specialist psychiatrists and clinical trials for these unapproved therapeutic goods.
- [UC Berkeley Psychedelic Law and Policy Map](https://psychedelics.berkeley.edu/law-and-policy-map/) - Tracks United States state and local reforms with legal partners; it is informational rather than legal advice.

## Education and Professional Training

- [California Institute of Integral Studies Certificate in Psychedelic-Assisted Therapies and Research](https://www.ciis.edu/continuing-education/center-for-psychedelic-therapies-and-research/about-the-certificate) - Offers a paid, twelve-month professional certificate with hybrid and online cohorts; applications are time limited.
- [CU Denver Psychedelic Facilitator Training Program](https://clas.ucdenver.edu/psychedelic-research/education-and-training-psychedelic-science-and-facilitation) - Provides paid, state-approved didactic training toward Colorado licensure; practicum costs and application windows are separate.
- [Monash Psychedelic Education Program](https://www.monash.edu/medicine/scs/clinical-psychedelic-lab/education-program) - Offers paid university courses with global online access that do not alone qualify someone as a therapist.
- [MSc Psychedelics: Mind, Medicine, and Culture](https://www.exeter.ac.uk/masters-degrees/msc-psychedelics-mind-medicine-and-culture/) - Covers science, therapy, philosophy, history, anthropology, and ethics through a paid United Kingdom degree.
- [OPEN Foundation ADEPT Training](https://open-foundation.org/adept-psychedelic-therapy-training/) - Offers paid professional training through a Netherlands-based hybrid program and a separate online pathway with eligibility restrictions.
- [Psychedelic Science and Medicine](https://www.coursera.org/learn/psychedelic-science-and-medicine) - Covers history, trials, neuroscience, risks, ethics, and misconceptions in a Johns Hopkins-led course; audit access and certificates vary.
- [Psychoactive Pharmaceutical Investigation, MS](https://pharmacy.wisc.edu/psychoactive-pharmaceutical-investigation/) - Provides a paid online master's covering drug development, regulation, culture, ethics, and pharmacology.
- [uOttawa MA in Psychedelics and Consciousness Studies](https://www.uottawa.ca/faculty-social-sciences/psychology/graduate/psychedelics-consciousness-studies-programs) - Offers a paid online interdisciplinary master's with English and French pathways and admissions requirements.

## Culture, History, Ethics, and Advocacy

- [Chacruna Institute for Psychedelic Plant Medicines](https://chacruna.net/) - Emphasizes Indigenous knowledge, equity, reciprocity, ethics, and cultural context through nonprofit education and advocacy.
- [Drug Science](https://www.drugscience.org.uk/) - Publishes independent United Kingdom research, policy analysis, education, and commentary that does not replace clinical guidance.
- [Erowid Center](https://www.erowid.org/) - Archives literature, history, images, testing records, and first-person reports; community reports are not clinical evidence.
- [Indigenous Reciprocity Initiative](https://www.chacruna-iri.org/) - Channels unrestricted support to Indigenous-led biocultural projects connected to psychedelic plant traditions.
- [Interdisciplinary Conference on Psychedelic Research](https://www.icpr-conference.com/) - Convenes a paid biennial European research conference organized by OPEN Foundation.
- [Multidisciplinary Association for Psychedelic Studies](https://maps.org/) - Supports research, education, harm-reduction, and policy work through a longstanding nonprofit; verify advocacy claims against primary records.
- [OPEN Foundation](https://open-foundation.org/) - Supports European research, professional community, education, and responsible clinical integration through a nonprofit.
- [Psychedelic Access and Research European Alliance](https://parea.eu/) - Advocates for scientifically grounded, regulated, equitable, and reimbursed psychedelic-assisted care in Europe.
- [Psychedelic Press](https://psychedelicpress.co.uk/) - Publishes paid works on the history, literature, and philosophy of psychoactive substances alongside digital articles.
- [Psymposia](https://www.psymposia.com/) - Produces critical nonprofit journalism and analysis about science, power, harms, and commercialization; its work is editorial rather than peer reviewed.
