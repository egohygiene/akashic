# Awesome Commerce and Marketplaces [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Shopping discovery, independent marketplaces, creator storefronts, custom products, print-on-demand, manufacturing, wholesale, payments, and fulfillment.

Part of [akashic](../../README.md), the Ego Hygiene knowledge collection.

> **Commerce and safety note:** A listing platform, storefront, payment processor, or fulfillment provider is infrastructure—not a guarantee of authenticity, quality, legality, labor conditions, delivery, profit, or dispute resolution. Check the current seller, item, return policy, total landed cost, intellectual-property rights, production sample, payout schedule, account-export path, and country-specific terms before committing money or customer orders. Keep conversations and payments inside a marketplace when buyer protection depends on doing so. Custom and made-to-order goods may have narrower return rights, while sellers remain responsible for product rules, taxes, truthful claims, privacy, accessibility, and customer service. For the underlying business and regulatory system, use [Business and Entrepreneurship](../business-and-entrepreneurship/README.md).

## Shop Safely and Compare

- [B Corp Directory](https://www.bcorporation.net/en-us/find-a-b-corp/) - Searches companies certified by B Lab against its social and environmental impact standard; certification is not a product-level guarantee.
- [BBB Scam Tracker](https://www.bbb.org/scamtracker) - Crowdsourced reports and search tools for recognizing scam patterns; reports are not independently verified findings or a substitute for official reporting.
- [CamelCamelCamel](https://camelcamelcamel.com/) - Tracks historical Amazon prices and sends price-drop alerts; marketplace availability and regional coverage vary.
- [Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/) - Independent nonprofit product testing, reliability data, safety reporting, and buying guidance; much of the detailed content requires membership.
- [Fair Trade Certified Shopping Guide](https://www.fairtradecertified.org/shop/) - Finds products carrying Fair Trade USA certification across participating brands and retailers; verify the mark on the specific product.
- [FTC Online Shopping Guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/online-shopping) - Official United States guidance on seller checks, payment safety, shipping promises, refunds, and reporting online-shopping problems.
- [Good On You](https://directory.goodonyou.eco/) - Rates many fashion brands on published labor, environmental, and animal-impact criteria; coverage and evidence quality vary by brand.
- [Google Shopping](https://shopping.google.com/) - Compares products and sellers through Google's shopping index; listings may be sponsored and purchase protection depends on the merchant.
- [Keepa](https://keepa.com/) - Provides Amazon price histories, alerts, product tracking, and seller data through web, extension, and paid data products.
- [Shop](https://shop.app/) - Shopify-operated shopping and order-tracking marketplace for participating merchants; merchant terms and return policies remain controlling.
- [Slickdeals](https://slickdeals.net/) - Community deal discovery with voting, alerts, and editorial posts; affiliate economics and fast-changing prices warrant direct verification.
