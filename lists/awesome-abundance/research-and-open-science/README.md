## Scholarly Discovery and Legal Access

- [OpenDOAR](https://v2.sherpa.ac.uk/opendoar/) - Quality-controlled global directory of academic open-access repositories searchable by location, content, software, and repository type.
- [Science.gov](https://www.science.gov/) - Federated search across United States federal science databases, websites, reports, data, patents, and research results.
- [WorldWideScience](https://worldwidescience.org/) - Multilingual federated search across national and international scientific databases and portals.

## Open Data

- [World Bank Open Data](https://data.worldbank.org/) - Free access to global development indicators, country datasets, downloadable tables, visualizations, and APIs.
- [NASA Earthdata](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/) - Open access to NASA Earth-observation data, search tools, visualizations, tutorials, and cloud analysis services, with an account required for many downloads.
- [NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/) - Free public archive for weather, climate, oceanographic, geophysical, and environmental data from NOAA and partners.
- [USGS Science Data Catalog](https://data.usgs.gov/datacatalog/) - Searchable catalog of publicly released United States Geological Survey datasets with metadata and download links.
- [data.europa.eu](https://data.europa.eu/) - Official portal aggregating openly reusable datasets from European Union institutions and participating countries.
- [Registry of Open Data on AWS](https://registry.opendata.aws/) - Catalog of public datasets hosted on AWS for scientific, geospatial, genomic, climate, machine-learning, and public-interest use.
- [Google Cloud Public Datasets](https://cloud.google.com/datasets) - Catalog of public datasets accessible through Google Cloud tools, with querying or compute beyond free allowances potentially billable.
- [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/) - Openly licensed research and datasets about global health, economics, population, energy, environment, and social conditions.
- [GBIF](https://www.gbif.org/) - Global Biodiversity Information Facility providing free access to species occurrence records, datasets, and derived citable downloads.

## Methods and Reproducibility

- [OpenWetWare](https://openwetware.org/) - Community wiki for openly sharing laboratory protocols, notebooks, materials, courses, and biological research knowledge.
- [Research Software Directory](https://research-software-directory.org/) - Open catalog connecting research software with documentation, contributors, institutions, publications, and impact.
- [Code Ocean Open Capsules](https://codeocean.com/capsules) - Browsable reproducibility capsules that package published code, data, dependencies, and executable workflows; running resources may have limits.
- [Renku](https://renkulab.io/) - Open-source collaborative data-science platform with a public free service for reproducible projects, notebooks, environments, and datasets.
- [Whole Tale](https://wholetale.org/) - Free research environment for creating, running, publishing, and verifying reproducible computational narratives from data and code.
- [Open Science Framework Registries](https://osf.io/registries) - Free discovery and creation of time-stamped research registrations and preregistrations across communities and disciplines.

## Citizen Science

- [Zooniverse](https://www.zooniverse.org/) - Free people-powered research platform where volunteers classify authentic data across science, history, language, and the arts.
- [SciStarter](https://scistarter.org/) - Searchable hub for discovering citizen-science projects by topic, location, age, required equipment, and participation mode.
- [iNaturalist](https://www.inaturalist.org/) - Free community platform for recording organisms, receiving identification help, and contributing observations to biodiversity research.
- [eBird](https://ebird.org/) - Cornell Lab of Ornithology platform for recording bird observations, exploring maps and trends, and contributing data to science and conservation.
- [NASA Citizen Science](https://science.nasa.gov/citizen-science/) - Official directory of NASA projects inviting the public to analyze data, make observations, build instruments, and support discoveries.
- [GLOBE Observer](https://observer.globe.gov/) - Free mobile citizen-science program for collecting environmental observations used by NASA and the broader GLOBE community.
- [CitSci.org](https://www.citsci.org/) - Free platform for finding, creating, managing, and analyzing community and citizen-science projects and datasets.
- [Project Sidewalk](https://sidewalk-chicago.cs.washington.edu/) - Browser-based citizen-science project for remotely auditing pedestrian accessibility in participating cities using street imagery.
- [Foldit](https://fold.it/) - Free protein-folding puzzle game whose player solutions contribute to biochemistry and structural-biology research.

## Scientific Tools and Reference Data

- [PhET Interactive Simulations](https://phet.colorado.edu/) - University of Colorado Boulder collection of free, research-based interactive simulations for science and mathematics education.
- [ChemCollective](https://chemcollective.org/) - Free Carnegie Mellon collection of virtual chemistry laboratories, scenario activities, tutorials, and concept tests.
- [NIST Chemistry WebBook](https://webbook.nist.gov/chemistry/) - Free reference database of thermochemical, spectroscopic, ion-energetics, and physical-property data from NIST.
- [PubChem](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) - Free NIH chemical information system connecting structures, properties, identifiers, bioassays, safety records, patents, and literature.
- [RCSB Protein Data Bank](https://www.rcsb.org/) - Free public archive and visualization platform for experimentally determined 3D structures of proteins, nucleic acids, and complexes.
- [Galaxy](https://usegalaxy.org/) - Free public instance of the open Galaxy platform for accessible, reproducible biological data analysis through browser workflows.
- [SageMathCell](https://sagecell.sagemath.org/) - Free browser interface for evaluating SageMath code without installing software or creating an account.
- [Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/) - Account-based hosted Jupyter notebooks with a continuing free compute tier whose availability, accelerators, and usage limits vary.
- [WolframAlpha](https://www.wolframalpha.com/) - Computational knowledge engine with free interactive queries and answers, while step-by-step and advanced features require paid access.
- [Desmos](https://www.desmos.com/calculator) - Free browser-based graphing calculator with expressions, tables, regressions, sliders, accessibility features, and classroom activities.
