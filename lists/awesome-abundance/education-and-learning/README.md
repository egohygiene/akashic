## University and Open Courseware

- [MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/) - Publishes free materials from more than 2,500 MIT courses without enrollment, registration, or tuition.
- [OpenLearn](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/) - The Open University provides hundreds of free courses, articles, activities, and digital badges across academic and practical subjects.
- [Saylor Academy](https://www.saylor.org/) - Nonprofit platform offering free self-paced college and professional courses, with optional paid proctoring only when credit is sought.
- [Open Yale Courses](https://oyc.yale.edu/) - Free video lectures, syllabi, readings, and transcripts from selected introductory Yale College courses without registration.
- [Harvard Online Free Courses](https://pll.harvard.edu/catalog/free) - Catalog of Harvard courses that can be taken online at no cost, with paid certificates optional where offered.
- [Carnegie Mellon Open Learning Initiative](https://oli.cmu.edu/) - Research-based online courses with a collection of self-guided materials available to independent learners for free.
- [NPTEL](https://nptel.ac.in/) - Indian Institutes of Technology and Indian Institute of Science platform offering free engineering, science, humanities, and management courses.
- [SWAYAM](https://swayam.gov.in/) - Government of India learning platform with free enrollment in university and school courses, while proctored certification may cost extra.
- [University of Helsinki MOOC Center](https://www.mooc.fi/en/) - Free university courses in programming, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, data, and education, many with exercises and credits.
- [OpenCourseWare Consortium Search](https://www.oercommons.org/hubs/ocw) - OER Commons hub for discovering openly licensed courseware from institutions around the world.

## General Learning

- [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/) - Nonprofit learning platform with free lessons, practice, and progress tracking across mathematics, science, computing, economics, and humanities.
- [GCFGlobal](https://edu.gcfglobal.org/) - Nonprofit library of free tutorials on technology, workplace skills, reading, mathematics, personal finance, and everyday life.
- [Open Culture](https://www.openculture.com/) - Curated directory of free courses, ebooks, audiobooks, films, language lessons, textbooks, and cultural material from legitimate sources.
- [Academic Earth](https://academicearth.org/) - Directory of free university lectures and course collections organized by institution and academic subject.
- [Crash Course](https://thecrashcourse.com/) - Free educational video series covering humanities, sciences, social sciences, study skills, and professional subjects.
- [TED-Ed](https://ed.ted.com/) - Free animated lessons, educator-created lesson pages, discussion prompts, and learning series across a wide range of subjects.
- [CK-12](https://www.ck12.org/) - Free adaptive learning platform with digital FlexBooks, simulations, practice, and teacher resources for K–12 STEM subjects.
- [SkillsCommons](https://www.skillscommons.org/) - Repository of openly licensed workforce-training materials created through United States Department of Labor grant programs.
- [IBM SkillsBuild](https://skillsbuild.org/) - Free account-based courses and credentials in technology, job readiness, sustainability, and professional skills for eligible learners and organizations.
- [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/training/) - Free interactive learning paths, modules, sandboxes, and exam preparation for Microsoft technologies and general development skills.

## Computing and Technology

- [freeCodeCamp](https://www.freecodecamp.org/) - Nonprofit, account-based curriculum with thousands of free coding lessons, projects, articles, videos, and certifications.
- [The Odin Project](https://www.theodinproject.com/) - Free, open-source full-stack web-development curriculum built around projects, documentation, and a learner community.
- [Full Stack Open](https://fullstackopen.com/en/) - University of Helsinki course teaching modern JavaScript web development, APIs, testing, containers, GraphQL, and TypeScript for free.
- [CS50 OpenCourseWare](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/) - Harvard's free introductory computer-science course with lectures, assignments, tools, and an optional verified certificate.
- [Exercism](https://exercism.org/) - Free nonprofit platform for practicing programming languages through exercises, automated analysis, and volunteer mentoring.
- [Code.org](https://code.org/) - Nonprofit provider of free computer-science courses, activities, teacher materials, and Hour of Code resources for K–12 learners.
- [Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu/) - Free MIT visual-programming community where learners create and remix interactive stories, games, and animations.
- [Cisco Skills for All](https://skillsforall.com/) - Free account-based courses and career paths in networking, cybersecurity, Python, data science, and digital skills.
- [Google for Developers Learning](https://developers.google.com/learn) - Free codelabs, pathways, documentation, and training for Android, web, cloud, AI, and other Google developer technologies.
- [AWS Educate](https://aws.amazon.com/education/awseducate/) - Free account-based cloud-learning content and hands-on labs for students and new learners, with no credit card required.

## Open Textbooks and Teaching Materials

- [OpenStax](https://openstax.org/) - Rice University nonprofit publishing peer-reviewed, openly licensed textbooks that can be read online or downloaded free.
- [Open Textbook Library](https://open.umn.edu/opentextbooks) - Catalog of openly licensed textbooks that may be downloaded, adapted, and distributed at no cost.
- [OER Commons](https://www.oercommons.org/) - Public digital library for finding, evaluating, adapting, and creating openly licensed educational resources.
- [LibreTexts](https://libretexts.org/) - Nonprofit platform of open textbooks, homework systems, visualizations, and course materials across many disciplines.
- [MERLOT](https://www.merlot.org/) - Free peer-reviewed collection of online teaching and learning materials maintained by the California State University system.
- [Pressbooks Directory](https://pressbooks.directory/) - Searchable catalog of openly licensed books published across participating Pressbooks networks.
- [BCcampus Open Collection](https://collection.bccampus.ca/) - Curated collection of adaptable open textbooks, courses, and ancillary materials for postsecondary education.
- [eCampusOntario Open Library](https://openlibrary.ecampusontario.ca/) - Free collection of openly licensed textbooks, modules, courses, and learning resources from Ontario institutions.
- [Open Music Theory](https://viva.pressbooks.pub/openmusictheory/) - Openly licensed interactive textbook for college-level music theory and aural-skills study.
- [Chemistry LibreTexts](https://chem.libretexts.org/) - Free, openly licensed chemistry textbooks, reference material, homework, visualizations, and laboratory resources.

## Languages and Communication

- [Language Transfer](https://www.languagetransfer.org/) - Donation-supported project offering complete audio courses and downloadable lessons without accounts, ads, or fees.
- [Live Lingua FSI Courses](https://www.livelingua.com/fsi/) - Hosts public-domain Foreign Service Institute text and audio courses for more than 40 languages without charge.
- [GLOSS](https://gloss.dliflc.edu/) - Defense Language Institute collection of thousands of free reading and listening lessons across many languages and proficiency levels.
- [Deutsche Welle Learn German](https://learngerman.dw.com/) - Free multimedia German courses, placement tests, news-based lessons, podcasts, and worksheets from Germany's public broadcaster.
- [BBC Learning English](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/) - Free lessons, videos, audio, quizzes, pronunciation practice, and news-based English instruction.
- [USA Learns](https://www.usalearns.org/) - Free account-based English, citizenship, and job-skills courses created with public and nonprofit partners.
- [ASL University](https://www.lifeprint.com/) - Free American Sign Language lessons, dictionaries, fingerspelling practice, videos, and instructor resources.
- [Peace Corps Language Courses](https://www.livelingua.com/peace-corps/) - Free archive of public-domain Peace Corps language manuals and audio materials for independent study.
