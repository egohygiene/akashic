# Awesome Creative Tools and Production [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A practical route from an idea to a finished, shareable creative work using legitimate no-cost software, free tiers, devices people already own, and public or community infrastructure.

Part of [Awesome Abundance](../README.md) and [akashic](../../../README.md), the Ego Hygiene knowledge collection.

This list is for making music, podcasts, video, animation, images, books, games, 3D work, and accessible media when money, hardware, internet, private workspace, or conventional industry access is limited. No-cost does not always mean unrestricted: account, device, storage, public-file, noncommercial, attribution, revenue, and hardware conditions are stated where they matter.

## Start With What You Have

The existing [Creative Work](../free-and-open-source-software/README.md#creative-work) section is the durable local-first baseline: Blender, GIMP, Krita, Audacity, LMMS, MuseScore Studio, OBS Studio, Kdenlive, Shotcut, OpenShot, HandBrake, darktable, RawTherapee, Scribus, Natron, Godot, FreeCAD, and KiCad. They are not duplicated below.

**Phone or tablet only:** Record with the built-in camera and microphone, then begin with BandLab, GarageBand, iMovie, Adobe Express, Adobe Fresco, Kleki, or Piskel according to the device you have.

**Browser or shared computer only:** Choose browser tools that export the project or standard files during the same session. Do not assume a school, library, shelter, or workplace computer will retain browser storage after sign-out.

**Old or low-spec computer:** Start with a single-purpose tool such as Audacity, BeepBox, Avidemux, Pencil2D, Pinta, or LibreCAD. Use proxy media, smaller canvases, and lower preview resolution before replacing hardware.

**Capable desktop:** Use the open-source baseline or a continuing free edition such as DaVinci Resolve, Affinity, Waveform Free, LUNA, or Maxon Autograph after checking operating-system and hardware requirements.

**No suitable equipment or private space:** Search libraries, memory labs, community media centers, community radio, makerspaces, and Fab Labs before buying or financing equipment.

**Every path:** Keep original recordings and editable project files, export a widely supported final format, maintain a second copy when possible, and record the license or permission for every outside asset.

## Access Key

**Open:** Software or infrastructure is available under an open-source or free-software license.

**Free edition:** A proprietary application has a continuing no-cost edition rather than a temporary trial.

**Free tier:** A hosted service has a continuing no-cost plan with quotas or paid upgrades.

**Included:** No additional payment is required on a compatible device someone already has.

**Public:** A public institution or publicly funded service provides the access path.

**Local:** Equipment, training, eligibility, hours, or fees vary by participating location.

**Account:** Registration, activation, or cloud identity is required.

**Restricted:** Commercial use, revenue, export, file privacy, or another material condition applies.

## Browser, Phone, and Shared Computers

- [Adobe Express Free](https://www.adobe.com/express/pricing) - **Free tier · Account · Browser/mobile.** Creates and edits images, short video, documents, and social posts with 5 GB of cloud storage and 10-day version history; premium assets, advanced features, and larger quotas are paid.
- [AudioMass](https://audiomass.co/) - **Open · Browser.** Runs a multitrack waveform editor in a modern browser without installation and can be self-hosted; available memory and browser storage limit larger projects.
- [BandLab Studio](https://www.bandlab.com/studio) - **Free tier · Account · Browser/mobile.** Records, sequences, edits, and collaborates on music projects in a browser, iOS, or Android; free projects are limited to 16 tracks and 15 minutes, platform features differ, and active internet is required.
- [Flat](https://flat.io/pricing) - **Free tier · Account · Browser/mobile.** Provides collaborative music notation and playback; the Basic plan limits the number of scores and adds branding to some print or export output.
- [GarageBand](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/garageband/id408709785) - **Included · Apple mobile.** Turns a compatible iPhone or iPad into a recording studio with instruments, loops, effects, and up to 32 tracks; device, operating-system, and storage requirements apply.
- [iMovie](https://www.apple.com/imovie/index.html) - **Included · Apple desktop/mobile.** Edits video with titles, soundtracks, green screen, and local export up to 4K on supported Mac, iPhone, and iPad hardware; there is no cross-platform edition.
- [Kleki](https://kleki.com/) - **Open · Browser/mobile.** Provides a touch- and pressure-friendly painting workspace with layers and local image export, without requiring an account.
- [Magma](https://magma.com/pricing) - **Free tier · Account · Browser.** Supports real-time collaborative drawing; the Spark plan limits storage, history, imports, layers, and canvas size while paid plans increase quotas.
- [Microsoft Clipchamp](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/clipchamp/what-clipchamp-products-are-there-and-what-s-their-cost) - **Free tier · Account · Browser/Windows/iOS.** Edits and exports personal videos through 1080p with owned or free-stock media; 4K, premium stock and effects, and cloud content backup require Microsoft 365 or Premium.
- [Piskel](https://www.piskelapp.com/) - **Open · Browser.** Creates pixel art and frame animation with GIF, PNG, and spritesheet export; the optional offline desktop builds are older and maintained on a best-effort basis.
- [PortableApps.com App Directory](https://portableapps.com/apps) - **Open platform · Shared/Windows.** Runs screened portable graphics, audio, video, office, and accessibility applications from a removable or local folder without conventional installation; individual licenses vary and public-computer policies may prohibit executables or USB media.

## Music Production and Recording

- [Cakewalk Sonar Free Tier](https://www.cakewalk.com/sonar) - **Free edition · Account · Windows.** Records, edits, mixes, saves, and exports with unlimited audio, MIDI, and instrument tracks; periodic activation and a BandLab account are required, and Membership unlocks additional workflow tools and content.

- [LUNA](https://www.uaudio.com/products/luna) - **Free edition · Account · Desktop.** Provides an unlimited-track DAW for Windows and macOS without requiring Universal Audio hardware; UA Connect and an iLok account are required, while hardware inserts and additional content are paid.
- [Mixxx](https://mixxx.org/) - **Open · Desktop.** Supports DJ mixing, recording, broadcasting, library management, and many optional controllers on Windows, macOS, and Linux.
- [ocenaudio](https://www.ocenaudio.com/en/) - **Free edition · Desktop.** Offers a focused waveform editor with real-time effect preview on Windows, macOS, and Linux; the application is proprietary freeware rather than open source.
- [Pro Tools Intro](https://www.avid.com/pro-tools/intro) - **Free edition · Account · Desktop.** Provides a track- and I/O-limited edition of Pro Tools for Windows and macOS; Avid installation, activation, iLok licensing, and relatively capable hardware are required.
- [Pure Data](https://github.com/pure-data/pure-data) - **Open · Desktop.** Uses visual patching to build synthesizers, effects, installations, instruments, and interactive audio systems across major desktop platforms.
- [Sonic Pi](https://sonic-pi.net/) - **Open · Desktop/Raspberry Pi.** Combines a live-coding language, synthesizers, samples, effects, and performance tools for making music through code.
- [Strudel](https://strudel.cc/) - **Open · Browser.** Brings TidalCycles-inspired pattern-based live coding to a browser without a local audio-development setup.
- [Tenacity](https://tenacityaudio.org/) - **Open · Desktop.** Provides a privacy-conscious multitrack recorder and editor in the Audacity family with plugin and FFmpeg support.
- [Waveform Free](https://www.tracktion.com/products/waveform-free) - **Free edition · Account · Desktop.** Supplies an unlimited-track DAW with instruments and effects for Windows 10 or 11 and macOS 13 or later; optional feature expansions are paid and Linux support is currently listed as coming soon.

## Rights, Exports, and Staying Free

**Prefer ownership and exit:** Before investing time, confirm that a tool exports the editable project or an interoperable format, not only a flattened preview or platform link.

**Keep three layers when possible:** Preserve original recordings or scans, editable project files, and final exports. A temporary transfer link is not a backup.

**Use the narrowest workable rights path:** Public-domain or CC0 material is simplest; CC BY requires credit; CC BY-SA also requires compatible sharing terms; CC BY-NC excludes commercial use; stock and custom licenses must be read individually.

**Do not redistribute raw assets:** Royalty-free samples, fonts, templates, and stock usually permit use inside a finished work, not resale or reposting as a competing library.

**Record provenance:** Save the creator, source URL, asset title, license, and access date beside the project. Keep releases or written permission for identifiable people and private property where needed.

**Check downgrade behavior:** Learn what becomes hidden, public, read-only, branded, compressed, deleted after inactivity, or impossible to export before relying on a free tier.

**Avoid accidental subscriptions:** Choose the continuing free plan directly, decline trial-only premium features, and do not add a payment card merely to explore a supposedly no-cost workflow.
