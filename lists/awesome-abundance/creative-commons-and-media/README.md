# Awesome Creative Commons and Media [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Reusable images, art, audio, music, video, fonts, icons, 3D assets, and cultural media with free access or open licensing.

Part of [Awesome Abundance](../README.md) and the [Ego Hygiene Awesome collection](../../../README.md).

“Free to download” does not always mean “free to reuse.” Check the license attached to each individual asset, preserve required attribution, and confirm whether commercial use, modification, or redistribution is permitted.

## Cross-Media Discovery

- [Openverse](https://openverse.org/) - Open-source search engine across hundreds of millions of openly licensed and public-domain images and audio records from external providers.
- [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/) - Multilingual repository of freely licensed and public-domain images, audio, video, documents, and structured media metadata.
- [Creative Commons Platform Toolkit](https://creativecommons.org/platform/toolkit/) - Official guidance and links for discovering, attributing, and using Creative Commons content across major platforms.
- [Public Domain Review](https://publicdomainreview.org/) - Curated essays and collections highlighting unusual public-domain art, literature, film, audio, and historical material.
- [Public Domain Image Archive](https://pdimagearchive.org/) - Curated high-resolution collection of historical public-domain images organized for creative discovery and reuse.
- [Creative Commons](https://creativecommons.org/) - Nonprofit steward of the Creative Commons licenses, with free tools and plain-language explanations for sharing and reusing work legally.
- [Library of Congress Free to Use](https://www.loc.gov/free-to-use/) - Rotating curated sets of Library of Congress items with no known copyright restrictions, prepared for free download and reuse.

## Photography and Illustration

- [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/) - Large library of free photographs under the Unsplash License, which allows broad use but is not a Creative Commons license.
- [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/) - Free stock photographs and videos under the Pexels License, with no attribution required and restrictions on unaltered resale and harmful use.
- [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/) - Free images, illustrations, vectors, video, music, and sound effects under the Pixabay Content License, with item-level restrictions to review.
- [Burst](https://www.shopify.com/stock-photos) - Shopify collection of high-resolution stock photography available for free commercial and noncommercial use under its published license.
- [StockSnap](https://stocksnap.io/) - Searchable collection of photographs released under CC0 for broad reuse without required attribution.
- [Nappy](https://nappy.co/) - Free stock photography centering Black and Brown people, with images available under a broad reuse license.
- [Openclipart](https://openclipart.org/) - Community library of vector clip art dedicated to the public domain through CC0.
- [unDraw](https://undraw.co/illustrations) - Customizable SVG illustration collection free for personal and commercial use without attribution under its license.
- [Humaaans](https://www.humaaans.com/) - Mix-and-match human illustration library released under CC0 for design and prototyping.
- [NASA Image and Video Library](https://images.nasa.gov/) - Searchable NASA collection of images, video, and audio; most NASA-created material is reusable under agency media guidelines.
- [NOAA Photo Library](https://photolib.noaa.gov/) - Free collection of weather, ocean, coast, climate, fisheries, and environmental imagery with source and credit information.
- [USGS Multimedia Gallery](https://www.usgs.gov/products/multimedia-gallery) - Public science images, video, audio, maps, and animations, with item-level usage guidance and most USGS-created works in the public domain.
- [Flickr Commons](https://www.flickr.com/commons) - Program where participating archives and museums publish photographs identified as having no known copyright restrictions.

## Museums and Public-Domain Art

- [Smithsonian Open Access](https://www.si.edu/openaccess) - More than five million Smithsonian 2D and 3D items designated CC0 for downloading, sharing, and reuse without permission.
- [The Met Open Access](https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access) - Hundreds of thousands of public-domain artwork images and collection data available under CC0 for unrestricted reuse.
- [National Gallery of Art Open Access](https://www.nga.gov/artworks/free-images-and-open-access) - High-resolution images of public-domain works that are free for commercial and noncommercial use.
- [Getty Open Content Program](https://www.getty.edu/projects/open-content-program/) - Downloadable images of public-domain Getty collection objects available without charge for any purpose.
- [Rijksmuseum Collection](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/collection) - High-resolution collection images with rights and download information, including many public-domain works available for reuse.
- [Art Institute of Chicago Open Access](https://www.artic.edu/open-access/open-access-images) - Public-domain artwork images and metadata distributed under CC0 through downloads, datasets, and an API.
- [Cleveland Museum of Art Open Access](https://www.clevelandart.org/open-access) - High-resolution images and metadata for public-domain works released under CC0 with bulk data and API access.
- [Yale University Art Gallery Open Access](https://artgallery.yale.edu/collections/using-collection/open-access-policy) - Free downloads and unrestricted reuse of qualifying public-domain collection images under Yale's open-access policy.
- [Cooper Hewitt Collection](https://collection.cooperhewitt.org/) - Searchable design collection with downloadable public-domain and CC0 images, structured metadata, and an open API.
- [Los Angeles County Museum of Art Public Domain Images](https://collections.lacma.org/) - Searchable collection whose public-domain works can be filtered and downloaded, with rights shown per object.

## Audio, Music, and Scores

- [Freesound](https://freesound.org/) - Account-based collaborative database of sound effects and recordings under CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-NC licenses shown per file.
- [Musopen](https://musopen.org/) - Nonprofit library of free classical recordings, sheet music, textbooks, and music-learning material with item-level rights information.
- [LibriVox](https://librivox.org/) - Volunteer recordings of public-domain books released as free public-domain audiobooks.
- [Free Music Archive](https://freemusicarchive.org/) - Searchable music library whose tracks use varied Creative Commons, public-domain, or custom licenses that must be checked individually.
- [ccMixter](https://ccmixter.org/) - Community music and remix library where samples and tracks are shared under Creative Commons licenses.
- [IMSLP](https://imslp.org/) - Petrucci Music Library of public-domain and permission-granted scores and recordings, with copyright status varying by country.
- [Mutopia Project](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/) - Free sheet-music editions typeset from public-domain works and distributed under Creative Commons licenses.
- [BBC Sound Effects](https://sound-effects.bbcrewind.co.uk/) - Thousands of BBC archival sound effects available for personal, educational, and research use under the RemArc license.
- [National Jukebox](https://www.loc.gov/collections/national-jukebox/) - Library of Congress streaming collection of historical commercial recordings with catalog data and rights notes.
- [NASA Sounds](https://www.nasa.gov/audio-and-ringtones/) - Free downloads of mission audio, spacecraft sounds, historic speech, and ringtones subject to NASA media-usage guidelines.
- [YouTube Audio Library](https://www.youtube.com/audiolibrary) - Account-based catalog of music and sound effects cleared for YouTube creation, with attribution requirements shown per track.
- [FreePD](https://freepd.com/) - Music explicitly dedicated to the public domain through CC0 for unrestricted reuse.
- [Jamendo Music](https://www.jamendo.com/) - Community platform where independent artists publish tracks under Creative Commons licenses for free personal listening and download.
- [Incompetech](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/) - Kevin MacLeod's library of original production music released under a Creative Commons attribution license.
- [Live Music Archive](https://archive.org/details/etree) - Internet Archive collection of concert recordings shared with the permission of artists who allow taping and free distribution.

## Video

- [Mixkit](https://mixkit.co/) - Free stock video, music, sound effects, and templates under Mixkit's item-specific licenses and usage restrictions.
- [Coverr](https://coverr.co/) - Free stock video and music for personal and commercial projects under the Coverr license, with redistribution restrictions.
- [Internet Archive Moving Image Archive](https://archive.org/details/movies) - Large free-access collection of films, news, animation, ephemera, and community video with rights varying by item.
- [Prelinger Archives](https://archive.org/details/prelinger) - Downloadable historical advertising, educational, industrial, and amateur films with license information on each item.
- [Library of Congress National Screening Room](https://www.loc.gov/collections/national-screening-room/) - Free streaming and downloadable historical films, with rights and access notes supplied per work.
- [National Film Board of Canada](https://www.nfb.ca/) - Free streaming access to a large collection of Canadian documentaries, animation, and interactive works; reuse rights remain restricted.

## Fonts and Icons

- [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/) - Catalog of open-source font families that can be downloaded, self-hosted, embedded, and reused under their listed licenses.
- [Font Library](https://fontlibrary.org/) - Community catalog of freely licensed fonts with downloads and license metadata for each family.
- [Velvetyne Type Foundry](https://velvetyne.fr/) - Collective designing and distributing experimental fonts under libre licenses with source files available.
- [The League of Moveable Type](https://www.theleagueofmoveabletype.com/) - Open-source type foundry offering freely licensed font families and educational material.
- [Font Awesome Free](https://fontawesome.com/search?o=r&m=free) - Free subset of Font Awesome icons available under open licenses for code, fonts, and documentation.
- [Tabler Icons](https://tabler.io/icons) - Large open-source set of customizable SVG interface icons released under the MIT License.
- [Heroicons](https://heroicons.com/) - Free MIT-licensed SVG icon set from Tailwind Labs in outline, solid, and miniature styles.
- [Lucide](https://lucide.dev/) - Community-maintained open-source SVG icon toolkit derived from Feather with packages for many frameworks.
- [Phosphor Icons](https://phosphoricons.com/) - Flexible MIT-licensed icon family with multiple weights, SVG downloads, and framework packages.
- [Remix Icon](https://remixicon.com/) - Open-source neutral-style icon system for interfaces, distributed as SVGs, fonts, and packages.
- [OpenMoji](https://openmoji.org/) - Open-source emoji and symbol library from Hochschule der Medien released under CC BY-SA.
- [Material Symbols](https://fonts.google.com/icons) - Google's Apache-licensed variable icon family with adjustable fill, weight, grade, and optical size.
- [The Noun Project](https://thenounproject.com/) - Massive icon library with free downloads available under Creative Commons attribution terms; paid plans remove attribution requirements.
- [Fontsource](https://fontsource.org/) - Open-source packages that let developers self-host freely licensed font families without third-party font services.
- [Fontshare](https://www.fontshare.com/) - Free font service from the Indian Type Foundry offering professional typefaces licensed for personal and commercial use.
- [SVG Repo](https://www.svgrepo.com/) - Searchable collection of hundreds of thousands of SVG icons and vectors with per-item license information, including many open licenses.
- [Bootstrap Icons](https://icons.getbootstrap.com/) - Free MIT-licensed SVG icon library from the Bootstrap team, usable with or without the Bootstrap framework.

## 3D and Game Assets

- [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/) - Community-funded library of high-resolution HDRIs, textures, and 3D models released under CC0.
- [Kenney Assets](https://kenney.nl/assets) - Large collection of game graphics, interfaces, audio, and 3D assets released primarily under CC0.
- [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/) - Community repository of 2D art, 3D models, textures, music, and sound effects under varied open licenses.
- [BlenderKit Free Assets](https://www.blenderkit.com/asset-gallery?query=order:_score%20is_free:true) - Searchable free tier of Blender-ready models, materials, brushes, scenes, and HDRIs with asset-specific licenses.
- [Smithsonian 3D](https://3d.si.edu/) - Interactive Smithsonian 3D collection with downloadable models, tours, and CC0 assets where marked.
- [NASA 3D Resources](https://nasa3d.arc.nasa.gov/) - Downloadable NASA models, textures, and printable resources for spacecraft, terrain, astronomy, and mission hardware.
- [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com/) - Free low-poly 3D asset packs released under CC0 for games, animation, and visualization.
- [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza/) - Search and download low-poly 3D models with clear CC0 or Creative Commons attribution terms per asset.
