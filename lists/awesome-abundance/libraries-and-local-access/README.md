# Awesome Libraries and Local Access [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Library discovery, digital borrowing, research databases, learning platforms, devices, museum passes, and surprising things available through local membership.

Part of [Awesome Abundance](../README.md) and [akashic](../../../README.md), the Ego Hygiene knowledge collection.

Library services vary by system, consortium, residency, age, and current vendor contracts. A listed platform is free to patrons only when their library participates; always begin with the digital-resources and lending pages for the library that issued the card.

## Find Libraries and Materials

- [USA.gov Libraries and Archives](https://www.usa.gov/libraries-and-archives) - Official starting point for finding local public libraries, federal depository libraries, national libraries, and public archive services in the United States.
- [IMLS Library Search and Compare](https://www.imls.gov/search-compare) - Official search across United States public-library systems with locations, service-area data, staffing, revenue, visits, programs, and circulation metrics.
- [WorldCat](https://search.worldcat.org/) - Free global catalog for finding books, recordings, films, articles, maps, archives, and other materials held by nearby libraries.
- [Federal Depository Library Directory](https://ask.gpo.gov/s/FDLD) - Official locator for libraries providing free public access and research help for United States federal government information.
- [State Libraries Directory](https://www.loc.gov/flicc/state-library-agencies/) - Library of Congress directory linking to state library agencies that coordinate catalogs, databases, special collections, and statewide services.
- [LibWeb](https://www.lib-web.org/) - International directory linking to public, academic, national, state, school, and special libraries with browsable geographic indexes.
- [Library Technology Guides Libraries.org](https://librarytechnology.org/libraries/) - Searchable international directory of libraries, branches, consortia, automation systems, and service organizations.

## Books, Audiobooks, Comics, and Media

- [Libby](https://www.overdrive.com/apps/libby) - Free app for borrowing participating libraries' ebooks, audiobooks, and magazines with a valid card, subject to catalog and lending limits.
- [Hoopla](https://www.hoopladigital.com/) - Participating libraries provide cardholders a monthly allowance for instant digital borrowing of ebooks, audiobooks, comics, music, films, and television.
- [Kanopy](https://www.kanopy.com/) - Ad-free film streaming funded by participating public libraries and universities, with monthly credits or catalog limits set by the institution.
- [The Palace Project](https://thepalaceproject.org/) - Library-centered app that combines participating ebook and audiobook collections with additional open and library-provided content.
- [cloudLibrary](https://www.yourcloudlibrary.com/) - Ebook and audiobook borrowing application available without direct charge to patrons of participating library systems.
- [Boundless](https://www.boundlessapp.com/) - Baker & Taylor library app for borrowing ebooks and audiobooks from participating institutions with a library account.
- [BiblioBoard Library](https://library.biblioboard.com/) - Digital books and local-creator collections available through participating libraries, with some statewide programs offering geolocation-based access.
- [Comics Plus](https://librarypass.com/comicsplus/) - Library-funded digital comics, graphic novels, and manga with simultaneous access when offered by a school or public library.
- [Freegal Music](https://www.freegalmusic.com/) - Music streaming and downloads offered to cardholders where a library subscribes, with allowances determined by the local institution.
- [PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/catalog) - Digital newspapers and magazines from around the world available through participating library cards or on-site library access.
- [Flipster](https://www.ebsco.com/products/flipster) - Digital magazine platform whose participating libraries provide cardholders free issue reading and mobile downloads.
- [Sora](https://www.soraapp.com/) - Student reading app for borrowing ebooks and audiobooks supplied by a participating school, with public-library cards linkable where allowed.
- [TumbleBook Library](https://www.tumblebooklibrary.com/) - Animated talking picture books, read-alongs, and children's ebooks licensed by participating schools and public libraries.

## Learning and Research Platforms

- [Mango Languages for Libraries](https://mangolanguages.com/libraries/) - Conversation-focused language courses funded for patrons by participating public, academic, and government libraries.
- [Transparent Language Online](https://www.transparent.com/libraries/) - Language-learning courses and practice tools available remotely to cardholders when their library subscribes.
- [LinkedIn Learning for Libraries](https://www.linkedin.com/learning-login/go) - Portal for entering a participating library identifier and card credentials to access professional video courses without a personal subscription.
- [Gale Courses](https://education.gale.com/l-gale/) - Instructor-led online courses provided at no cost to patrons through participating libraries, with enrollment windows and local availability.
- [Universal Class for Libraries](https://www.universalclass.com/libraries.htm) - Self-paced continuing-education courses with certificates offered through subscribing public libraries.
- [Brainfuse HelpNow](https://www.brainfuse.com/highed/helpNow.asp) - Live tutoring, writing feedback, study tools, and language support available through participating library and school portals.
- [Brainfuse JobNow](https://www.brainfuse.com/highed/jobNow.asp) - Library-funded live help with resumes, interviews, job searching, career resources, and adult learning where subscribed.
- [Tutor.com for Libraries](https://www.tutor.com/libraries) - On-demand tutoring, writing review, test preparation, and career support supplied through participating libraries.
- [Morningstar Investment Research Center](https://www.morningstar.com/products/direct/investment-analysis-platforms/investment-research-center) - Investment research platform commonly licensed by libraries for patron access; coverage and remote-login rules vary locally.
- [Gale Presents: Udemy](https://www.gale.com/elearning/udemy) - On-demand Udemy business and technology courses available to patrons through participating public libraries.
- [NoveList](https://www.ebsco.com/products/novelist) - Readers' advisory database of book recommendations, read-alikes, series lists, and reading guides licensed by participating libraries.
- [Beanstack](https://www.beanstack.com/) - Reading-challenge and tracking platform that libraries and schools license so patrons can join free summer and year-round reading programs.
- [DigitalLearn](https://www.digitallearn.org/) - Public Library Association courses and coaching materials that teach basic computer, internet, and online-service skills at no cost.

## Devices, Tools, and Things

- [Local Tools](https://localtools.org/find/) - Map and directory for finding tool libraries and lending programs where members can borrow equipment instead of purchasing it.
- [Shareable Library of Things Guide](https://www.shareable.net/how-to-start-a-library-of-things/) - Free guide and examples for finding or creating community lending collections for tools, appliances, recreation, and household items.
- [Library Telescope](https://www.librarytelescope.org/) - Program directory and resources for libraries that lend portable telescopes to cardholders for no-cost astronomy access.
- [Seed Library Network](https://www.seedlibraries.net/) - Community hub and map for libraries that share free seeds and teach seed saving, gardening, and local biodiversity.
- [Mobile Beacon Hotspot Lending](https://www.mobilebeacon.org/hotspot-lending/) - Information about nonprofit-supported hotspot-lending programs operated by participating libraries and community institutions.
- [National Cristina Foundation](https://www.cristina.org/) - Nonprofit technology-reuse network connecting organizations with donated computers and equipment for people with disabilities, students, and communities.

## Museums, Parks, and Local Experiences

- [Discover and Go](https://discoverandgo.org/) - Platform used by participating library systems to reserve free or reduced-price passes for local museums and cultural attractions.
- [Culture Pass NYC](https://www.culturepass.nyc/) - New York City cardholders from participating library systems can reserve free admission to cultural institutions across the city.
- [Georgia Public Libraries Experience Passes](https://georgialibraries.org/passes/) - Georgia library cards can unlock free passes for participating state parks, historic sites, museums, theater, and other destinations.
- [Check Out Louisiana](https://library.la.gov/check-out-louisiana/) - Louisiana residents with participating library cards can borrow free day passes for state parks and museums.
- [California State Library Parks Pass](https://www.parks.ca.gov/?page_id=30806) - Participating California public libraries lend free vehicle day-use passes for more than 200 state parks.

## Specialized Library Services

- [Ask a Librarian](https://ask.loc.gov/) - Free Library of Congress question service connecting the public with reference specialists across subjects and collection areas.
- [Law Library of Congress Research Help](https://ask.loc.gov/law/) - Free legal-reference questions and research guidance from Law Library of Congress specialists, without providing legal advice.
- [National Library Service for the Blind and Print Disabled](https://www.loc.gov/nls/) - Eligibility-based free braille and talking-book program with mailed materials, playback equipment, and downloadable BARD access.
- [National Library of Medicine Customer Support](https://support.nlm.nih.gov/) - Free help using PubMed, MedlinePlus, clinical terminology, biomedical data resources, and other National Library of Medicine services.
- [Foundation Center Funding Information Network](https://candid.org/find-us) - Libraries and partner organizations offering free on-site access to Candid's fundraising databases, training, and nonprofit research tools.
