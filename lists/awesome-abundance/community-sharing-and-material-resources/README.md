# Awesome Community Sharing and Material Resources [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Local gifting, reuse networks, food sharing, repair help, clothing, household goods, technology, and other physical resources available without ordinary retail cost.

Part of [Awesome Abundance](../README.md) and [akashic](../../../README.md), the Ego Hygiene knowledge collection.

Physical-resource availability is inherently local and inventory-dependent. Confirm pickup rules, eligibility, safety, condition, transportation, and whether requested parts or materials carry a cost. Never send deposits, verification codes, gift cards, or sensitive identity information to strangers offering “free” goods.

## Gifting and Reuse Networks

For finding a local group, checking who organizes it, and choosing a safe first contact, use the [community-connection guide](../../../docs/community-connection.md). Directory listings do not establish that a group is active or vetted.

- [Buy Nothing Project](https://buynothingproject.org/) - Free account-based network where neighbors give away items, request needs, lend belongings, and share services without buying or selling.
- [Freecycle](https://freecycle.org/) - Nonprofit network of free local groups where members offer and request reusable goods to keep them out of landfills.
- [Trash Nothing](https://trashnothing.com/) - Free account-based interface for local giveaway groups and direct offers of household goods, furniture, clothing, and other reusable items.
- [OLIO](https://olioapp.com/) - Free core app for neighbors and participating businesses to give away surplus food and household items locally.
- [Freegle](https://www.ilovefreegle.org/) - United Kingdom nonprofit network for giving and receiving reusable items locally without payment.
- [Craigslist Free Stuff](https://www.craigslist.org/about/sites#US) - Local classifieds whose “free” category can surface no-cost items; users must independently screen listings and arrange safe pickup.
- [Facebook Marketplace Free Listings](https://www.facebook.com/marketplace/category/free/) - Account-based local marketplace filter for zero-price listings, with availability, trustworthiness, and pickup safety left to participants.
- [Little Free Library World Map](https://littlefreelibrary.org/map/) - Map for finding volunteer-run book-sharing boxes where visitors can take or leave books without membership or checkout.
- [Free Stores and Really Really Free Markets](https://localwiki.org/main/Really_Really_Free_Market) - Community-maintained starting point for finding or organizing gift-economy events where goods and skills are shared without payment.
- [Mutual Aid Hub](https://www.mutualaidhub.org/) - Map and directory of community mutual-aid networks that share food, supplies, transportation, and neighbor-to-neighbor support.
- [Shareable](https://www.shareable.net/) - Nonprofit news and how-to library about sharing economies, cooperatives, mutual aid, and community-run resource projects.
- [Falling Fruit](https://fallingfruit.org/) - Collaborative world map of publicly accessible edible plants, community orchards, and municipal foraging data.

## Food and Household Essentials

- [Feeding America Food Bank Locator](https://www.feedingamerica.org/find-your-local-foodbank) - Finds regional food banks that connect people with free groceries, pantries, meal programs, and local eligibility information.
- [FoodFinder](https://foodfinder.us/) - Free United States map of food pantries and distribution sites searchable by ZIP code and location.
- [Little Free Pantry](https://www.littlefreepantry.org/) - Grassroots mini-pantry movement with a location map and free plans for neighborhood food and essential-item sharing boxes.
- [Freedge](https://freedge.org/locations/) - Community-fridge map and open resources for finding or starting public refrigerators that share free food locally.
- [Food Not Bombs](https://foodnotbombs.net/new_site/map.html) - Map and contacts for autonomous chapters that recover food and share free vegetarian meals in public spaces.
- [Lasagna Love](https://lasagnalove.org/request/) - Volunteer network allowing households in participating areas to request a free home-cooked meal without income verification.
- [USDA Summer Meals for Kids Site Finder](https://www.fns.usda.gov/summer/sitefinder) - Seasonal official locator for free summer meals and snacks offered to children and teens at approved sites.

- [National Diaper Bank Network Member Directory](https://nationaldiaperbanknetwork.org/member-directory/) - Finds local member organizations distributing free diapers and related basic necessities through community partners.
- [Alliance for Period Supplies Member Directory](https://allianceforperiodsupplies.org/member-directory/) - Directory of community programs distributing free period products through local partner organizations.
- [AmpleHarvest.org](https://ampleharvest.org/) - Free directory connecting home gardeners with nearby food pantries that accept surplus fresh produce donations.

## Repair and Maintenance

- [Repair Café](https://www.repaircafe.org/en/visit/) - Global locator for free volunteer repair events covering electronics, appliances, clothing, bicycles, furniture, toys, and other items.
- [iFixit Repair Guides](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide) - Free community-authored, step-by-step repair manuals and troubleshooting information for electronics, appliances, vehicles, clothing, and household equipment.
- [The Restart Project](https://therestartproject.org/parties/) - Directory and resources for community electronics-repair events where volunteers help diagnose and fix devices.
- [Fixit Clinic](https://fixitclinic.blogspot.com/) - Volunteer-run repair events and online coaching that help participants troubleshoot and repair broken household items.
- [Bike Collectives Network](https://bikecollectives.org/wiki/Community_Bicycle_Organizations) - Community-maintained directory of nonprofit bicycle kitchens and collectives offering tools, workspace, used parts, education, and earn-a-bike programs.
- [Repair.org Community](https://www.repair.org/community) - Right-to-repair coalition directory and guidance connecting people with repair groups, events, policy work, and educational resources.
- [Precious Plastic](https://preciousplastic.com/) - Open-source plastic-recycling machines, build documentation, and a community map of local workspaces that share equipment and knowledge.

## Clothing, Furniture, and Family Supplies

- [Dress for Success](https://dressforsuccess.org/affiliate-list/) - Affiliate directory for programs providing eligible women with professional clothing, styling, coaching, and employment support at no charge.
- [One Warm Coat Agency Locator](https://www.onewarmcoat.org/share-warmth/agency-locator/) - Finds nonprofit agencies that receive donated coats and distribute them free within their communities.
- [Furniture Bank Network](https://furniturebanks.org/furniture-banks/) - Directory of organizations providing donated household furniture to referred or eligible families and individuals.
- [Cradles to Crayons](https://www.cradlestocrayons.org/what-we-do/) - Supplies clothing, shoes, diapers, school items, and essentials to children through service partners in participating regions.
- [Baby2Baby National Network](https://baby2baby.org/national-network/) - Partner network distributing diapers, clothing, hygiene items, and emergency supplies to children through local nonprofit organizations.
- [Operation PROM](https://operationprom.org/) - Local chapters provide donated prom dresses and formalwear to students who meet program requirements, subject to seasonal inventory.

## Pet Support

- [Pet Help Finder](https://www.pethelpfinder.org/) - Free locator for pet food, veterinary care, temporary housing, transportation, supplies, and other local support services.
- [Pets of the Homeless Resource Map](https://petsofthehomeless.org/get-help/find-locations/) - Finds pet-food donation sites, wellness clinics, and veterinary partners serving people experiencing homelessness.
- [The Pet Fund](https://www.thepetfund.com/for-pet-owners) - Application-based nonprofit assistance for qualifying non-emergency veterinary care that owners cannot afford.
- [RedRover Relief Urgent Care Grants](https://redrover.org/relief/urgent-care-grants/) - Small eligibility-based grants for urgent veterinary care when a funding gap would otherwise prevent treatment.
