# Awesome Health, Well-Being, and Accessibility [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Free clinics, evidence-informed public tools, treatment locators, crisis support, accessibility technology, and disability services.

Part of [Awesome Abundance](../README.md) and the [Ego Hygiene Awesome collection](../../../README.md).

These resources provide information, access, or support—not diagnosis or individualized medical advice. Eligibility, service scope, clinical evidence, privacy, and local availability vary. In an emergency, use the emergency service for your location; in the United States, call or text 988 for a suicide or mental-health crisis and 911 for immediate danger.

## Free and Charitable Care

- [National Association of Free and Charitable Clinics](https://nafcclinics.org/find-clinic/) - Locator for nonprofit clinics and pharmacies providing free or charitable medical, dental, behavioral-health, and medication services across the United States.
- [Remote Area Medical](https://www.ramusa.org/clinic-schedule/) - Schedule of volunteer mobile clinics offering free medical, dental, and vision care without requiring insurance.
- [Free Clinics](https://www.freeclinics.com/) - Independent directory of free and income-based clinics; users should confirm listings, cost, eligibility, and services directly with each provider.
- [Mission of Mercy Clinics](https://www.amissionofmercy.org/locations/) - Faith-based mobile clinics providing free primary medical care and medications at participating sites without insurance or income qualification.
- [America's Dentists Care Foundation Events](https://adcf.net/clinics/) - Calendar of large volunteer dental clinics that provide free care through state Mission of Mercy and partner events.
- [Give Kids A Smile](https://www.adafoundation.org/give-kids-a-smile) - American Dental Association program connecting eligible children with free dental screening, prevention, and treatment through local events.
- [VSP Eyes of Hope](https://www.vspvision.com/eyes-of-hope/get-help.html) - Eligibility-based access to no-cost eye exams and glasses through participating community partners and disaster-response programs.
- [Lions Clubs Vision Services](https://www.lionsclubs.org/en/start-our-approach/club-locator) - Club locator for asking about locally available vision screening, eyeglass recycling, hearing, and other charitable assistance programs.
- [Planned Parenthood Health Centers](https://www.plannedparenthood.org/health-center) - Locator for reproductive and sexual health services, with free or reduced-cost care available at some affiliates based on program and eligibility.
- [Title X Family Planning Clinic Locator](https://opa-fpclinicdb.hhs.gov/) - Official locator for federally supported clinics providing confidential family-planning and preventive health services, with fees based on income.

## Mental Health and Self-Help Tools

- [VA Mental Health Apps](https://mobile.va.gov/app-store/mental-health) - Public collection of free evidence-informed self-help and treatment-companion applications from the United States Department of Veterans Affairs.
- [PTSD Coach](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/appvid/mobile/ptsdcoach_app.asp) - Free VA and Department of Defense app for learning about trauma reactions, tracking symptoms, practicing coping skills, and finding support.
- [Mindfulness Coach](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/appvid/mobile/mindfulcoach_app.asp) - Free VA app with gradual mindfulness training, guided exercises, education, tracking, and reminders for the public as well as veterans.
- [Insomnia Coach](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/appvid/mobile/insomnia_coach.asp) - Free VA self-care app based on cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia, with important suitability cautions published on the official page.
- [Coping Coach](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/appvid/mobile/coping_coach_app.asp) - Free VA app for stress education, self-care tools, mood tracking, goal progress, and support-resource planning.
- [PTSD Family Coach](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/appvid/mobile/familycoach_app.asp) - Free VA app offering education, coping tools, relationship guidance, and support resources for families of people with PTSD.
- [CBT-i Coach](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/appvid/mobile/cbticoach_app_public.asp) - Free VA treatment-companion app intended for people completing cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia with a clinician.
- [Centre for Clinical Interventions Self-Help Resources](https://www.cci.health.wa.gov.au/Resources/Looking-After-Yourself) - Government of Western Australia workbooks and worksheets based on evidence-informed psychological interventions for common mental-health concerns.
- [NHS Every Mind Matters](https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/) - Free National Health Service guidance, self-care plans, videos, and practical tools for sleep, anxiety, mood, stress, and life changes.
- [Healthy Minds Program](https://hminnovations.org/meditation-app) - Donation-supported free meditation app from a nonprofit research center, with training in awareness, connection, insight, and purpose.
- [Medito](https://meditofoundation.org/medito-app) - Nonprofit free and open-source meditation app with guided courses, sleep content, breathing exercises, and no advertising or subscription.
- [Smiling Mind](https://www.smilingmind.com.au/smiling-mind-app) - Free nonprofit mindfulness app and programs for individuals, families, schools, and workplaces.
- [Therapist Aid](https://www.therapistaid.com/) - Library of free printable psychoeducation and therapy worksheets, with additional resources reserved for paid members.
- [NAMI](https://www.nami.org/) - National Alliance on Mental Illness offering free education, support groups, local affiliate services, and a nationwide helpline.
- [Mental Health America Screening](https://screening.mhanational.org/) - Free anonymous validated screening questionnaires for depression, anxiety, and related concerns, followed by referral information.
- [NIMH Health Topics](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics) - National Institute of Mental Health explanations of mental-health conditions, treatments, statistics, and clinical-trial participation.
- [SMART Recovery](https://smartrecovery.org/) - Free science-based mutual-support meetings, tools, and worksheets for people changing addictive behaviors, available in person and online.
- [Alcoholics Anonymous Meeting Finder](https://www.aa.org/find-aa) - Official directory for locating free local and online Alcoholics Anonymous meetings and service offices worldwide.

## Crisis and Immediate Support

- [988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline](https://988lifeline.org/) - Free confidential United States call, text, and chat support available around the clock for suicidal, mental-health, and substance-use crises.
- [Crisis Text Line](https://www.crisistextline.org/) - Free text-based crisis support from trained volunteers in the United States and participating international regions.
- [Trevor Project](https://www.thetrevorproject.org/get-help/) - Free confidential crisis counseling by phone, text, and chat for LGBTQ+ young people in the United States.
- [Trans Lifeline](https://translifeline.org/hotline/) - Peer-support phone service run by and for trans people, with published hours and country-specific United States and Canada numbers.
- [RAINN National Sexual Assault Hotline](https://rainn.org/resources) - Free confidential United States phone and chat support plus referrals for people affected by sexual violence.
- [SAMHSA National Helpline](https://www.samhsa.gov/find-help/helplines/national-helpline) - Free confidential 24-hour treatment referral and information service for mental-health and substance-use concerns in English and Spanish.
- [FindTreatment.gov](https://findtreatment.gov/) - Confidential official locator for mental-health and substance-use treatment facilities across the United States and its territories.
- [Poison Help](https://www.poisonhelp.org/) - Free United States poison-center hotline and prevention information staffed around the clock by medical experts.
- [Alzheimer's Association Helpline](https://www.alz.org/help-support) - Free around-the-clock helpline, care navigation, local programs, and caregiver education for dementia-related concerns.

## Health Information and Everyday Well-Being

- [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/) - Free National Library of Medicine information about health conditions, medicines, tests, genetics, wellness, and medical terminology.
- [NIH Wellness Toolkits](https://www.nih.gov/health-information/your-healthiest-self-wellness-toolkits) - Free evidence-informed checklists and resources for emotional, physical, social, environmental, and disease-prevention wellness.
- [MyPlate](https://www.myplate.gov/) - Free USDA nutrition guidance, budget tools, meal-planning resources, recipes, and personalized goal features.
- [Nutrition.gov](https://www.nutrition.gov/) - Federal portal for reliable food, nutrition, physical activity, dietary supplement, and healthy-living information.
- [Move Your Way](https://odphp.health.gov/moveyourway) - Free federal physical-activity planner, fact sheets, videos, and guidance based on United States activity recommendations.
- [Smokefree.gov](https://smokefree.gov/) - Free National Cancer Institute quit plans, text programs, apps, medication information, and specialized tobacco-cessation resources.
- [1-800-QUIT-NOW](https://www.cdc.gov/tobacco/campaign/tips/quit-smoking/quitline/index.html) - Connects United States callers with free state quitline coaching and locally available cessation benefits.
- [Darebee](https://darebee.com/) - Donation-supported library of free workout programs, individual routines, challenges, meal-plan examples, and fitness reference material.
- [Fitness Blender Free Workouts](https://www.fitnessblender.com/videos?exclusive%5B%5D=0) - Filtered catalog of free follow-along exercise videos, with optional paid programs and features clearly separated.

## Vision, Reading, and Communication Access

- [Be My Eyes](https://www.bemyeyes.com/) - Free app connecting blind and low-vision users with sighted volunteers, participating organizations, and AI-assisted visual descriptions.
- [Seeing AI](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/ai/seeing-ai) - Free Microsoft application that narrates text, currency, products, scenes, people, colors, and images for blind and low-vision users.
- [Bookshare](https://www.bookshare.org/cms/bookshare-me/who-qualifies) - Accessible ebook library that is free for qualifying United States students and schools, with eligibility and fees differing for other members.
- [Described and Captioned Media Program](https://dcmp.org/) - Eligibility-based free streaming library of captioned and audio-described educational media for families, educators, and people with disabilities.
- [Learning Ally Audiobook Solution](https://learningally.org/Solutions-for-Home) - Human-read educational audiobooks for qualifying readers, with school sponsorships and financial assistance potentially covering membership costs.
- [National Deaf-Blind Equipment Distribution Program](https://www.fcc.gov/general/national-deaf-blind-equipment-distribution-program) - FCC program, known as iCanConnect, providing eligible low-income deaf-blind people communications equipment and training at no cost.
- [Relay Services](https://www.fcc.gov/consumers/guides/telecommunications-relay-service-trs) - Free FCC-regulated services enabling people with hearing or speech disabilities to place and receive telephone calls in accessible formats.

## Assistive Technology and Disability Services

- [AT3 Center](https://at3center.net/state-at-programs/) - Directory of state and territory assistive-technology programs offering demonstrations, short-term loans, reuse, financing, and training, often free.
- [Job Accommodation Network](https://askjan.org/) - Free confidential expert guidance about workplace accommodations, disability employment, accessible technology, and related United States law.
- [Computer/Electronic Accommodations Program](https://www.cap.mil/) - Provides assistive technology and accommodations at no cost to eligible Department of Defense personnel and partner federal agencies.
- [AbleGamers Player Panels and Services](https://ablegamers.org/our-services/) - Disability-gaming nonprofit offering peer support, accessible-play consultations, equipment guidance, and limited grant-supported solutions.
- [Free Wheelchair Mission](https://www.freewheelchairmission.org/receive-a-wheelchair/) - Partner-distributed wheelchairs provided without charge to eligible people in countries where the nonprofit operates.
- [AMBUCS Amtryke Program](https://ambucs.org/amtryke/) - Local chapters provide adaptive tricycles through evaluation, fundraising, and wish-list programs, with availability and cost support varying.
- [Disability Information and Access Line](https://dial.acl.gov/) - Free United States call, text, and email service connecting disabled people with local community resources and accessible information.
- [ADA National Network](https://adata.org/) - Free regional technical assistance, training, and materials about Americans with Disabilities Act rights, responsibilities, and implementation.
