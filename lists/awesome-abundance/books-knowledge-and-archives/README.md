## Books and Long-Form Reading

- [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org/) - Produces carefully formatted, proofread editions of public-domain books that are free to download and reuse in the United States.
- [HathiTrust Digital Library](https://www.hathitrust.org/) - Provides free full-text reading for millions of volumes identified as public domain, with search access across a much larger preservation collection.
- [Global Grey](https://www.globalgreyebooks.com/) - Offers free downloads of public-domain and openly reusable ebooks, with the copyright status documented for its collection.
- [Online Books Page](https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/) - University of Pennsylvania index linking to more than three million freely readable books and serials from legitimate hosts.
- [Perseus Digital Library](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/) - Tufts University collection of free Greek, Roman, Arabic, Germanic, Renaissance, and American primary texts with language tools.
- [Internet Sacred Text Archive](https://sacred-texts.com/) - Long-running free archive of public-domain religious, mythological, folkloric, philosophical, and esoteric texts.
- [International Children's Digital Library](https://www.childrenslibrary.org/) - Research collection of digitized children's books from around the world that can be read online without charge.
- [Baldwin Library of Historical Children's Literature](https://ufdc.ufl.edu/juv) - University of Florida digital collection providing free access to historical children's books and periodicals.
- [Digital Comic Museum](https://digitalcomicmuseum.com/) - Account-based archive of downloadable Golden Age comic books believed to be in the United States public domain.
- [Comic Book Plus](https://comicbookplus.com/) - Free account-supported archive of public-domain comics, pulp fiction, newspapers, and related historical material.
- [Online Library of Liberty](https://oll.libertyfund.org/) - Free scholarly editions of historically significant works about individual liberty, government, economics, law, and political thought.
- [Marxists Internet Archive](https://www.marxists.org/) - Volunteer nonprofit archive providing free access to multilingual texts by Marxist and related writers under varied copyright permissions.
- [Alice and Books](https://aliceandbooks.com/) - Browser-based library of public-domain classics with readable typography and free EPUB downloads.
- [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/) - Internet Archive catalog aiming to describe every published book, with public-domain reading and controlled digital lending for other titles.
- [ManyBooks](https://manybooks.net/) - Long-running catalog of public-domain and author-authorized ebooks organized by genre, with free downloads in common formats.
- [Feedbooks Public Domain](https://www.feedbooks.com/catalog/public_domain) - Curated catalog of public-domain literature prepared for e-readers and downloadable without charge.

## Open-Access Books and Monographs

- [Directory of Open Access Books](https://www.doabooks.org/) - Community-governed discovery index for peer-reviewed scholarly books published under open-access licenses.
- [OAPEN Library](https://library.oapen.org/) - Nonprofit repository of freely accessible, peer-reviewed academic books with clear licensing and downloadable files.
- [National Academies Press](https://nap.nationalacademies.org/) - Makes thousands of authoritative science, engineering, health, and policy reports free to read online or download as PDFs.
- [Open Book Publishers](https://www.openbookpublishers.com/) - Scholar-led nonprofit press publishing peer-reviewed books that can be read online and downloaded in open formats at no cost.
- [JSTOR Open Content](https://about.jstor.org/oa-and-free/) - Collects open-access journals, books, research reports, images, and primary sources that can be used without an institutional subscription.
- [Project MUSE Open Access Books](https://muse.jhu.edu/search?action=oa_browse) - Provides free full-text scholarly books from participating university presses and nonprofit publishers.
- [Open Research Library](https://openresearchlibrary.org/) - Aggregates peer-reviewed open-access books from multiple publishers into a free reading and discovery platform.
- [Unglue.it](https://unglue.it/) - Catalogs legally free ebooks and community-funded editions released under Creative Commons licenses.

## Reference and Primary Sources

- [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/) - Free, continuously updated scholarly reference work whose entries are written and reviewed by subject experts.
- [Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://iep.utm.edu/) - Free peer-reviewed reference work covering philosophers, movements, concepts, and problems across philosophy.
- [Wiktionary](https://www.wiktionary.org/) - Multilingual, collaboratively edited dictionary released under open licenses and available without an account.
- [Wikibooks](https://www.wikibooks.org/) - Community library of openly licensed textbooks, manuals, and instructional books that can be read and adapted freely.
- [Avalon Project](https://avalon.law.yale.edu/) - Yale Law School collection of freely accessible historical documents in law, diplomacy, politics, economics, and government.
- [Digital Public Library of America](https://dp.la/) - Free discovery portal connecting digitized materials from United States libraries, archives, museums, and cultural institutions.

## Libraries, Museums, and Cultural Archives

- [Europeana](https://www.europeana.eu/) - Searches cultural heritage from thousands of European institutions, with item-level rights statements governing reuse.
- [Library of Congress Digital Collections](https://www.loc.gov/collections/) - Free access to digitized photographs, manuscripts, maps, recordings, books, newspapers, and other United States cultural materials.
- [National Archives Catalog](https://catalog.archives.gov/) - Searches digitized records and descriptions from the United States National Archives, with free public viewing and downloads when available.
- [NYPL Digital Collections](https://digitalcollections.nypl.org/) - Free access to digitized prints, photographs, maps, manuscripts, streaming media, and public-domain downloads from the New York Public Library.
- [Trove](https://trove.nla.gov.au/) - National Library of Australia discovery service for digitized newspapers, books, images, maps, music, archives, and web history.
- [Gallica](https://gallica.bnf.fr/) - Bibliothèque nationale de France digital library offering millions of freely accessible books, manuscripts, maps, images, newspapers, and recordings.
- [DigitalNZ](https://digitalnz.org/) - Free search across New Zealand digital collections from libraries, museums, government agencies, media organizations, and community groups.
- [Biodiversity Heritage Library](https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/) - Consortium library providing free access to digitized biodiversity literature, illustrations, field notes, and scientific archives.
- [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/) - Nonprofit digital library offering free access to archived web pages, texts, audio, moving images, and software, with rights varying by item.
- [Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/collections) - Free searchable library and museum collections on health and human experience, with many digitized items openly licensed for reuse.
- [Deutsche Digitale Bibliothek](https://www.deutsche-digitale-bibliothek.de/) - German national portal aggregating digitized books, archives, images, recordings, and museum objects from cultural institutions.

## Newspapers and Periodicals

- [Chronicling America](https://www.loc.gov/collections/chronicling-america/) - Library of Congress collection of searchable digitized historical United States newspapers with free page images and OCR.
- [Google News Newspaper Archive](https://news.google.com/newspapers) - Legacy collection of digitized historical newspapers that remains freely viewable despite limited search and no new additions.
- [Elephind](https://elephind.com/) - Free federated search across digitized historical newspaper collections hosted by libraries and archives around the world.
- [World Radio History](https://www.worldradiohistory.com/) - Free research library of scanned broadcasting, electronics, music, and communications periodicals and books.
- [Internet Archive Scholar](https://scholar.archive.org/) - Experimental full-text search across scholarly literature preserved in Internet Archive collections, including older journals and proceedings.
- [Papers Past](https://paperspast.natlib.govt.nz/) - National Library of New Zealand collection of digitized newspapers, magazines, letters, diaries, and parliamentary papers available free online.

## Government and Declassified Archives

- [GovInfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/) - Free authenticated access to official publications from all three branches of the United States federal government.
- [NASA Technical Reports Server](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/) - Searches NASA aerospace research metadata and downloadable technical reports, conference papers, images, and historical documents.
- [CIA FOIA Reading Room](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/) - Public database of declassified and frequently requested Central Intelligence Agency records released through FOIA processes.
- [FBI Vault](https://vault.fbi.gov/) - Free collection of FBI records and media released proactively or through Freedom of Information Act requests.
- [National Security Archive](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/) - George Washington University repository publishing curated sets of declassified United States government documents and analysis.
- [The National Archives](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/) - United Kingdom national archive with free catalogue searching, research guides, and many digitized records available to download without charge.

## Shadow Libraries and Legal Risk

Shadow libraries distribute copyrighted material without permission from rights holders. They are not endorsed or linked here. The entries below point only to encyclopedia articles for readers who need neutral background on how these services work, the litigation surrounding them, and why their legal status differs by jurisdiction.

- [Sci-Hub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sci-Hub) - Encyclopedia article describing the Sci-Hub article repository, its history, court rulings against it, and the access debates that surround it.
- [Library Genesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Library_Genesis) - Encyclopedia article documenting the Library Genesis file collection, its mirrors, and the copyright litigation and blocking orders it has faced.
- [Anna's Archive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna%27s_Archive) - Encyclopedia article covering the Anna's Archive metadata and file aggregator, its relationship to other shadow libraries, and its contested legal position.
