# Awesome Research Funding and Grants [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Funding discovery for research, education, mental health, science, open source, creative work, journalism, nonprofits, community projects, startups, and public-interest innovation worldwide.

Part of [akashic](../../README.md), the Ego Hygiene knowledge collection.

Funding availability, deadlines, eligibility, geographic scope, and application requirements change frequently. Prefer the linked official source, verify the current call before preparing an application, and never pay an unsolicited fee to receive a government grant.

## Funding Discovery and Aggregators

- [Candid](https://candid.org/) - Nonprofit and grantmaker database for researching foundations, prior awards, organizations, and requests for proposals.
- [Candid Near You](https://candid.org/resources/candid-near-you/) - Map of libraries and nonprofit centers providing free onsite access to Candid's funding research products.
- [CareerOneStop Scholarship Finder](https://www.careeronestop.org/Toolkit/Training/find-scholarships.aspx) - U.S. Department of Labor search covering thousands of scholarships, fellowships, grants, and financial-aid awards.
- [Community Foundation Locator](https://cof.org/page/community-foundation-locator) - Searchable map for finding accredited U.S. community foundations and locally focused grantmakers.
- [GrantForward](https://www.grantforward.com/index) - Academic funding search engine with a free basic search across government, foundation, and institutional opportunities.
- [IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/tax-exempt-organization-search) - Official lookup for tax-exempt status, determination letters, Form 990 filings, and revocation records.
- [Philanthropy News Digest RFPs](https://philanthropynewsdigest.org/rfps) - Public listings of requests for proposals from foundations and other philanthropic funders.
- [ProFellow](https://www.profellow.com/fellowships/) - Free account-based database of professional, graduate, doctoral, postdoctoral, and fully funded fellowship programs.
- [ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/) - Free search of tax-exempt organizations, Form 990 filings, officers, finances, and foundation grant records.
- [SAM.gov Assistance Listings](https://sam.gov/assistance-listings) - Searchable descriptions of federal programs offering grants, loans, scholarships, insurance, and other assistance.
- [Simpler.Grants.gov Search](https://simpler.grants.gov/search) - Modern public search interface for active Grants.gov opportunities, with filters for agency, eligibility, status, and deadlines.
- [UCLA Graduate Funding Search](https://grad.ucla.edu/funding/) - Free search covering hundreds of graduate scholarships, dissertation grants, fellowships, and postdoctoral awards.
