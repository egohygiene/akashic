# Massachusetts

## Start here: statewide finders

- [Mass.gov](https://www.mass.gov/) - Official Commonwealth portal for benefits, licenses, taxes, health, housing, transportation, work, and state agencies. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Massachusetts Behavioral Health Help Line Resource Directory](https://www.masshelpline.com/bhhldirectory/) - Search frequently updated mental health, substance use, and social-support services by location and need. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Massachusetts City and Town Websites](https://www.mass.gov/lists/massachusetts-city-and-town-websites) - Open the official website for any of the Commonwealth's 351 municipalities. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Massachusetts Legal Resource Finder](https://masslrf.org/) - Answer a few questions to find free or low-cost legal programs, court services, and self-help material. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Massachusetts State Organization Directory](https://www.mass.gov/collections/massachusetts-state-organization-directory) - Search state agencies, statewide officials, courts, and legislative bodies by topic or keyword. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Mass 211](https://www.mass211.org/) - Confidential, 24/7 connection to health and human services, benefits, food, housing, support groups, volunteering, and other local help. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [MassOptions](https://massoptions.org/en/) - Find trusted local supports for older adults, people with disabilities, and caregivers through a state-run navigator. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Massachusetts Library Directory](https://mblc.state.ma.us/directories/libraries/index.php) - Search public, academic, school, special, and institutional libraries across the Commonwealth. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [RideMatch](https://massridematch.org/) - Search public, private, community, and accessible transportation options by trip and rider needs. <!-- atlas-role: index -->

## Benefits, food, and housing

- [Apply for State-Funded Public Housing](https://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-for-state-funded-public-housing) - Learn eligibility rules and use CHAMP to apply to multiple local housing-authority waitlists with one application.
- [DTA Connect](https://dtaconnect.eohhs.mass.gov/) - Apply for or manage Massachusetts food and cash assistance, upload documents, and check case information; an account is needed for case management.
- [Find Food Assistance](https://www.mass.gov/decision-tree/find-food-assistance) - Follow a state decision guide to SNAP, WIC, school meals, food banks, and immediate food support. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Find Free or Low-Cost Tax Help](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/find-a-tax-professional-to-help-with-your-taxes) - Locate VITA, low-income taxpayer clinics, senior tax assistance, and other qualified tax support.
- [Home Energy Assistance](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/home-energy-assistance) - Learn about income-qualified heating help and find the local agency or online application for the current program year.
- [Housing Navigator Massachusetts](https://www.housingnavigatorma.org/) - Search verified affordable rental properties, lotteries, open waitlists, and available units across Massachusetts. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [How to Start Looking for Affordable Housing](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/how-to-start-looking-for-affordable-housing-in-massachusetts) - A practical state guide connecting renters to Housing Navigator, CHAMP, local authorities, and application help. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [MassHealth Application Help](https://www.mass.gov/how-to/find-help-with-your-masshealth-insurance-application) - Find free enrollment assistance by phone, appointment, or trained community assister.

## Health, safety, and family support

- [Child Care Resource and Referral Agencies](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/child-care-resource-and-referral-agencies-ccrrs) - Find the regional agency that helps families locate child care and apply for financial assistance. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Community Behavioral Health Center Crisis Care](https://www.mass.gov/cbhc-crisis-care) - Find nearby 24/7 community crisis services and mobile crisis support; no insurance is required for crisis care. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Domestic Violence Services](https://www.mass.gov/domestic-violence-services) - Search local survivor services and reach SafeLink, the statewide 24/7 domestic-violence hotline; call 911 for immediate danger. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Find a Licensed Child Care Program](https://www.mass.gov/guides/find-a-licensed-family-group-or-school-age-child-care-program) - Use the Department of Early Education and Care's geographic search and learn how to check licensing history. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Find Your Local Veterans Service Officer](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/find-your-local-veterans-service-officer-vso) - Search by city, town, or ZIP code for local help with veterans' benefits, referrals, and applications. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Massachusetts Substance Use Helpline](https://helplinema.org/) - Free, confidential, 24/7 connection to licensed harm-reduction, treatment, recovery, and problem-gambling services. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [MassRealLives Program Finder](https://www.mass.gov/massreallivesorg-find-a-program) - Search hundreds of work, day, home, and support programs for people served by the Department of Developmental Services. <!-- atlas-role: index -->

## Work, learning, and getting around

- [Commonwealth Catalog](https://mblc.state.ma.us/resource-sharing/commonwealth-catalog.php) - Search and request materials from participating library networks for delivery to a local library; a participating library account is required to request. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [MassHire Career Centers](https://www.mass.gov/orgs/masshire-department-of-career-services) - Find statewide career centers offering job-search, resume, interview, training, and unemployment-related support. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [MassHire JobQuest](https://www.mass.gov/find-your-next-job-with-masshire-jobquest) - Search Massachusetts jobs, build a jobseeker profile, and register for workshops and hiring events; an account is needed for personalized tools.
- [Massachusetts Libraries Statewide Databases](https://mblc.state.ma.us/resource-sharing/database-licensing.php) - Access research and reference databases licensed for Massachusetts residents, with availability varying by library and database.
- [ORI Community Partners Directory](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/ori-community-partners-directory) - Find refugee and immigrant service providers by community, population served, language, and program. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Public Transportation in Massachusetts](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/public-transportation-in-massachusetts) - Find MBTA and regional transit-authority coverage, accessibility information, and reduced-fare resources. <!-- atlas-role: index -->

## Government, legal, and civic life

- [Emergency Preparedness Volunteering](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/get-involved-with-your-communitys-emergency-preparedness) - Find Community Emergency Response Teams, Medical Reserve Corps units, and other ways to help communities prepare and recover. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [File a Consumer Complaint](https://www.mass.gov/how-to/file-a-consumer-complaint) - Submit a complaint to the Massachusetts Attorney General's Consumer Advocacy and Response Division or contact its hotline.
- [Find Local Election Offices and Dropboxes](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/elections/voter-resources/find-my-local-election-office.htm) - Locate municipal election contacts and ballot drop boxes for registration, voting by mail, and poll-worker questions. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Legal Assistance for Court Users](https://www.mass.gov/legal-assistance) - Find court service centers, volunteer lawyers, limited assistance representation, law libraries, and self-help guidance. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Massachusetts Legislature](https://malegislature.gov/) - Read bills and laws, follow hearings and calendars, and find state legislators.
- [Massachusetts Municipal Data Hub](https://www.mma.org/all-directory-data/) - Compare all Massachusetts communities across government, demographics, municipal finance, elections, and representation. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [MA Responds](https://www.mass.gov/public-health-volunteer-opportunities-requesting-volunteers) - Register for medical or non-medical public-health and emergency-response volunteer deployments; screening is required before deployment.
