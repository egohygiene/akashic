# Massachusetts

## Government and public services

- [Mass.gov](https://www.mass.gov/) - Official Commonwealth portal for benefits, licenses, taxes, health, housing, transportation, work, and state agencies.
- [Mass 211](https://www.mass211.org/) - Confidential, 24/7 connection to health and human services, benefits, food, housing, support groups, volunteering, and other local help.
- [Massachusetts Legislature](https://malegislature.gov/) - Bills, laws, hearings, legislative calendars, and tools for finding and contacting state lawmakers.

## Maps and public data

- [MassGIS](https://www.mass.gov/orgs/massgis-bureau-of-geographic-information) - The Commonwealth's official bureau for statewide geographic data, mapping services, and GIS coordination.
- [Massachusetts Data Hub](https://data.mass.gov/) - Search public datasets and dashboards published by Commonwealth agencies.
