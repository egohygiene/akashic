# California

## Start here: statewide finders

- [CA.gov](https://www.ca.gov/) - Official state portal for services, agencies, benefits, licenses, taxes, health, housing, work, and everyday government tasks. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [California 211](https://211ca.org/) - Search local food, housing, health, utility, transportation, legal, and other community services by need and location; phone coverage can vary by county. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [California Community Action Service Finder](https://calcapa.org/find-help/) - Find the community-action, energy-assistance, Native American, or migrant and seasonal farmworker agency serving a California county. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [California County Social Services Offices](https://www.cdss.ca.gov/county-offices) - Find county contacts for CalFresh, CalWORKs, Medi-Cal, General Assistance, foster care, and other locally administered programs. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [California Department Directory](https://www.ca.gov/departments/) - Search and browse California departments, boards, commissions, and their public services. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [California Department of Aging County Service Finder](https://www.aging.ca.gov/Find_Services_in_My_County/) - Choose a county to reach the local Area Agency on Aging and programs for older adults, adults with disabilities, and caregivers. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [California Regional Centers](https://www.dds.ca.gov/rc/listings/) - Find the nonprofit regional center coordinating developmental-disability services for a particular county or community. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [California Roster](https://www.sos.ca.gov/administration/california-roster) - Use the Secretary of State's current directory for state agencies, courts, legislators, county officials, and incorporated cities and towns. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Get Help from CA.gov](https://www.ca.gov/support/) - Start with a plain-language state guide to food, cash aid, shelter, health coverage, mental health, utilities, immigration, and nearby services. <!-- atlas-role: index -->

## Benefits, food, and housing

- [BenefitsCal](https://benefitscal.com/) - Apply for or manage county-administered benefits such as CalFresh, CalWORKs, Medi-Cal, and General Assistance; available programs depend on household and county.
- [CalHFA Homebuyer Programs](https://www.calhfa.ca.gov/homebuyer/index.htm) - Explore state-backed down-payment and mortgage-assistance programs, approved lenders, and required education; CalHFA is not a direct lender and eligibility varies.
- [California Association of Food Banks Find Food](https://www.cafoodbanks.org/find-food/) - Find a member food bank by county, connect with immediate food help, or follow the path to CalFresh. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [California Housing and Homelessness Agency](https://housing.ca.gov/) - Start with the state's housing, homelessness, civil-rights, and housing-finance agencies and their programs. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [California Unclaimed Property Search](https://claimit.ca.gov/app/claim-search) - Search and file a claim for property held by the State Controller; the official search and claim process is free.
- [California WIC Office Finder](https://myfamily.wic.ca.gov/Home/ContactUs) - Find a nearby WIC office and contact California's nutrition and breastfeeding-support program for pregnant people and families with young children. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [CalKIDS](https://calkids.org/) - Check whether a child or eligible public-school student has a state-funded scholarship account, learn what identification is needed, and claim it for qualified education expenses.
- [Covered California](https://www.coveredca.com/) - Compare marketplace health plans, check financial help or Medi-Cal eligibility, enroll, and find free certified enrollment assistance.
- [Free California Tax Help](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/help/free-tax-help/) - Use Franchise Tax Board guidance to locate current VITA or TCE help for eligible taxpayers; sites and seasonal availability vary.
- [HUD California Resources](https://www.hud.gov/states/california) - Navigate housing counselors, public-housing agencies, affordable-rental resources, homelessness help, utility aid, and homeownership support across California. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Medi-Cal Application Options](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/medi-cal/apply/) - Compare the official online, phone, mail, and county-office routes for applying for free or low-cost California health coverage.
- [Utility CARE and FERA Discounts](https://www.cpuc.ca.gov/care/) - Check income-qualified electric and gas bill discounts and follow the California Public Utilities Commission's links to participating utilities.
- [Utility and Home Energy Assistance](https://www.csd.ca.gov/pages/liheapprogram.aspx) - Learn about LIHEAP bill help and weatherization, then contact the local agency that determines eligibility and current funding availability. <!-- atlas-role: index -->

## Health, safety, and family support

- [California 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/988-suicide-and-crisis-lifeline/) - Call, text, or chat 988 for free crisis support from California centers; call 911 for immediate physical danger or a medical emergency.
- [California Caregiver Resource Centers](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/services/mental-health-services-division-default/adult-caregivers-resource-centers/) - Find the regional center serving a California county for caregiver information, education, planning, counseling, and support. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [California Independent Living Services](https://dor.ca.gov/Home/IndependentLiving) - Find a local Independent Living Center supporting people with disabilities through peer support, skills, advocacy, and community-living services. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [California Poison Control](https://calpoison.org/emergency) - Get free expert poison-exposure guidance at 1-800-222-1222 around the clock; call 911 for life-threatening symptoms.
- [California Substance Use Disorder County Access Lines](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/individuals/substance-use-disorder-county-access-lines/) - Find the county line or provider directory used to request assessment and treatment; call 911 for a medical or psychiatric emergency. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [CalVet County Veterans Service Offices](https://www.calvet.ca.gov/VetServices/Pages/CVSO-Locations.aspx) - Find local, accredited help with veterans' benefits, claims, records, and referrals by county. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Child Care Resource and Referral Directory](https://rrnetwork.org/about/r-r-directory) - Search by ZIP code or county for a state-funded local agency that helps families find child care and understand available support. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Find Behavioral Health Resources](https://www.ca.gov/departments/217/services/36/) - Navigate county crisis contacts, Medi-Cal mental-health and substance-use care, treatment directories, and statewide support. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Find Domestic Violence Help](https://cpedv.org/find-help/) - Use a county map to find local hotlines and survivor-service organizations; the statewide coalition provides referrals rather than direct shelter or legal services. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Health Consumer Alliance](https://healthconsumer.org/) - Get free statewide help applying for or keeping health coverage, challenging denials, resolving plan problems, and understanding medical bills.
- [Licensed Child Care Search](https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/child-care-licensing/licensed-child-care-search) - Search California's licensed child-care programs and learn how to review public licensing history before choosing care. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Maternal Health Resources](https://www.ca.gov/support/maternal-health-resources.html) - Navigate state-reviewed pregnancy, postpartum, mental-health, nutrition, coverage, leave, and family-support resources. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Safe at Home Enrolling Agencies](https://www.sos.ca.gov/registries/safe-home/where-find-an-enrolling-agency) - Find county enrollment help for California's address-confidentiality program for eligible survivors and other protected applicants. <!-- atlas-role: index -->

## Work, learning, and getting around

- [America's Job Center of California](https://edd.ca.gov/ajcc) - Find a local one-stop center for no-cost job search, resume, training, workshop, and employment-support services. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [CalCareers](https://calcareers.ca.gov/) - Search California state jobs, compare classifications and exams, create alerts, and apply through an account.
- [California Community Colleges](https://www.cccco.edu/) - Find a public community college, explore career and transfer paths, compare student support, and learn how to apply and pay for college. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [California Immigrant Guide](https://immigrantguide.ca.gov/) - Navigate statewide information on benefits, jobs, education, citizenship, immigration services, legal help, and disaster relief. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [California Service Corps](https://www.californiavolunteers.ca.gov/california-service-corps/) - Compare paid community-service programs, local placements, eligibility, living allowances, education awards, and application paths. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [California Student Aid Commission](https://csac.ca.gov/) - Explore Cal Grants, the California Dream Act application, scholarships, aid workshops, and other state student-aid programs. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [CalJOBS](https://edd.ca.gov/en/jobs_and_training/Caljobs/) - Use California's official job-search and training system for resumes, openings, employer research, and career services; personalized tools require an account.
- [Caltrans Travel](https://dot.ca.gov/travel) - Reach live road conditions, closures, cameras, QuickMap, Amtrak California, 511 regions, and other statewide traveler tools. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [CCCApply](https://www.cccapply.org/) - Apply through the statewide California Community Colleges admission center, including standard, noncredit, international, and Promise Grant applications.
- [QuickMap](https://quickmap.dot.ca.gov/) - View current California highway traffic, incidents, closures, cameras, chain controls, and other Caltrans travel layers.
- [Regional 511 Services](https://dot.ca.gov/programs/traffic-operations/traveler-information/511) - Find the regional web and phone systems offering real-time traffic, transit, trip-planning, and rideshare information around California. <!-- atlas-role: index -->

## Government, legal, and civic life

- [California Courts Self-Help Guide](https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/) - Read plain-language court guidance, forms, and next steps for family, housing, debt, probate, small claims, and other common legal matters. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [California Legislative Information](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/) - Search current and historical bills, California codes, legislative publications, votes, and bill status.
- [CalGOLD Permit Assistance](https://calgold.ca.gov/) - Identify local, state, and federal permits for a business by city and business type; the tool links to issuing agencies but does not issue permits. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [DCA License Search](https://search.dca.ca.gov/) - Check licenses and public disciplinary information for professionals and businesses regulated by California's Department of Consumer Affairs. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Find Your California Court](https://courts.ca.gov/find-my-court) - Search by city or ZIP code for nearby county court locations, contacts, websites, and self-help services. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [LawHelpCA](https://www.lawhelpca.org/) - Find local free or low-cost legal aid and reliable self-help information by legal problem and location. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Register to Vote in California](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voter-registration/) - Review eligibility, register or preregister, request a paper form, and learn about same-day registration and confidential registration.
- [State Bar Free Legal Help](https://www.calbar.ca.gov/public/legal-resources/free-legal-help) - Find State Bar-funded legal aid, LawHelpCA referrals, disaster legal help, and guidance for locating trustworthy assistance. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [California Polling Place Finder](https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/polling-place) - Enter an address to find polling information, ballot details, early-voting links, and county election contacts. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [California Consumer Protection Hub](https://www.oag.ca.gov/consumers) - Use Attorney General guidance to identify the right complaint agency, report business misconduct, and review scam and consumer-rights information. <!-- atlas-role: index -->

- [California Open Data](https://data.ca.gov/) - Search datasets and dashboards published by California state agencies across health, transportation, government, water, fire, and natural resources. <!-- atlas-role: index -->

- [Calisphere](https://calisphere.org/) - Search digitized photographs, documents, artworks, maps, recordings, and other primary sources from California libraries, archives, and museums. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
