- [Town Calendar](https://www.wilmingtonma.gov/calendar) - Find public meetings, hearings, municipal events, and town schedule updates. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Town Clerk](https://www.wilmingtonma.gov/town-clerk) - Access voting, vital records, licenses, town-meeting records, census, and other clerk services.
- [Town Departments](https://www.wilmingtonma.gov/departments) - Find Wilmington boards, offices, public safety, public works, health, planning, and other departments. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Trash and Recycling](https://www.wilmingtonma.gov/trash-recycling) - Check collection guidance, recycling rules, bulk pickup, yard waste, and Department of Public Works contacts.

- [Wilmington Recreation](https://www.wilmingtonma.gov/recreation) - Find town recreation programs, registration information, commission meetings, and seasonal updates.

- [Wilmington Community Fund](https://www.wilmingtoncommunityfund.org/) - Request local food-pantry or emergency utility assistance, volunteer, or donate to support Wilmington residents.
- [Wilmington Elderly Services](https://www.wilmingtonma.gov/elderly-services) - Find Senior Center programs, meals, transportation, benefits assistance, events, and contact information. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
- [Wilmington Housing Authority](https://www.wilmingtonha.org/) - Review local public-housing properties, applications, notices, and Housing Authority contacts.
- [Wilmington Veterans Services](https://www.wilmingtonma.gov/veterans) - Get local help with Chapter 115 assistance, VA benefits, records, healthcare, housing referrals, and other veterans' services. <!-- atlas-role: index -->
