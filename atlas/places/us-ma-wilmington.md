# Wilmington, Massachusetts

## Town government

- [Town of Wilmington](https://www.wilmingtonma.gov/) - Official municipal home for town news, services, notices, meetings, departments, and resident information.
- [Town Departments](https://www.wilmingtonma.gov/departments) - Direct directory for Wilmington boards, offices, public safety, public works, health, planning, and other departments.
- [Town Calendar](https://www.wilmingtonma.gov/calendar) - Public meetings, hearings, municipal events, and town schedule updates.
- [Trash and Recycling](https://www.wilmingtonma.gov/trash-recycling) - Current collection guidance, schedules, recycling rules, bulk pickup, yard waste, and Department of Public Works contacts.
- [Wilmington Recreation](https://www.wilmingtonma.gov/recreation) - Town recreation programs, registration information, commission meetings, and seasonal updates.

## Learning and community

- [Wilmington Memorial Library](https://wilmlibrary.org/) - Local library catalog, events, research tools, digital collections, technology access, and community services.
- [Wilmington Public Schools](https://www.wpsk12.com/) - District announcements, school directories, calendars, family resources, and public school information.
