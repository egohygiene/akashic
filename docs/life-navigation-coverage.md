## Disposition and scope

All **18 content releases** and **seven adopted follow-ups** are closed as completed and represented by merged work. The seven baseline releases are also present. The thirteen original domains have useful, bounded first editions; no new collection or resource addition is needed for this audit. Recommend closing #152 and #40 when this acceptance PR merges and its checks pass.

This is a coverage, source-ownership, structural, and representative reader-journey audit. It does not renew external-source verification dates, certify provider availability, confer human review, or claim exhaustive worldwide coverage. U.S. discovery and Massachusetts-specific workflows retain their stated limits. Public reading, an application, an observed HTTP response, eligibility, and actual service delivery remain separate.
