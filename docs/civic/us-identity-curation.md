## Official evidence and limits

- [USA.gov document gateway](https://www.usa.gov/request-documents), [birth certificates](https://www.usa.gov/birth-certificate), [marriage records](https://www.usa.gov/marriage-certificate), [death certificates](https://www.usa.gov/death-certificate), and [divorce records](https://www.usa.gov/divorce-decree): distinguish copies from corrections, marriage licenses from certificates, and divorce decrees from certificates. The issuing jurisdiction, requester eligibility, and receiving agency determine the useful document.
- [CDC directory](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/w2w/index.htm) and [request guidelines](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/w2w/guidelines.htm): use where the event occurred, not automatically the current residence. No national fee, delivery estimate, or universal alternative-ID rule is invented.
- Department of State [application gateway](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/apply.html), [adult application](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/apply/adults.html), [renewal gateway](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/renew-replace.html), [online renewal](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/renew-replace/online.html), [mail renewal](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/renew-replace/mail.html), [loss reporting](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/renew-replace/report-passport-lost-stolen.html), and [changes/corrections](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/renew-replace/change-correct-passport.html): separate first application, renewal, loss report, replacement, and correction. Link the current eligibility checklists rather than reproducing all combinations. An online renewal cancels the old passport; reporting a lost valid passport invalidates it without replacing it. A completed passport does not need an address-only replacement; a pending application's address has its own contact route.
- State Department [fees](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/apply/help/fees.html), [processing](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/apply/help/processing-time.html), [contact](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/contact-support/us-passports.html), and [accessibility](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/apply/unique-needs/disability.html): fees vary by transaction and service. Processing estimates exclude mailing and are not travel guarantees. The mail-renewal page gives inconsistent two/four-week expectations for separately returned supporting documents; this guide promises neither and directs readers to track each return and contact the agency.
- SSA [replacement](https://www.ssa.gov/number-card/replace-card), [name change](https://www.ssa.gov/life-events/change-name), [evidence selector](https://www.ssa.gov/ssnumber/ss5doc.htm), and [contact](https://www.ssa.gov/agency/contact/): no card fee; physical cards are not always needed. The old `/personal-record/change-name` address redirects to the life-events URL. Evidence depends on age, status, and request; originals or issuer-certified copies differ from notarized photocopies. Delivery estimates begin after SSA completes the request. Some status-specific cases permit a name-record update without a corrected card; do not promise card issuance for everyone.
- [Vote.gov registration](https://vote.gov/register), [voting guide](https://vote.gov/guide-to-voting), USA.gov [status](https://www.usa.gov/confirm-voter-registration), [updates](https://www.usa.gov/change-voter-registration), and [election-office directory](https://www.usa.gov/state-election-office), [registration overview](https://www.usa.gov/register-to-vote), and EAC [mail-form instructions](https://www.eac.gov/voters/national-mail-voter-registration-form): neutral state/territory discovery only. The pages disagree on the state exceptions to the national mail form (Vote.gov includes Wisconsin; USA.gov's update page does not). Do not publish a fixed exception list or claim universal acceptance. Follow the current responsible election office for forms, deadlines, eligibility, accessibility, and any fees for related documents. Registration confirmation, voter cards, and voting identification are different things.

USA.gov, CDC, and SSA public pages were also retrieved by direct HTTP GET. State Department and Vote.gov direct automated requests returned 403, but their public content was readable through web retrieval. This is source-content verification, not a claim that every automated client receives HTTP 200. No account login, form submission, transaction, payment, or personal record was used. Source pages can change after this check.

## Scope and review

`us` labels the discovery chapter, not an eligibility finding or uniform national process. State, territorial, local, consular, adult/child, and status-specific exceptions stay visible. Phone, mail, in-person, language, and disability alternatives lead back to the responsible office. A prerequisite means check whether evidence is needed, not order every linked document.

All ten cards carry adjacent sources, agency/jurisdiction, channels, record/document distinctions, evidence, fee/timing sources, follow-up, and review metadata. The six-stage checklist records only blank private follow-through prompts. Browser reminders stay in memory and reset; no identifying inputs, account connections, uploads, persistent storage, or agency submissions are introduced.
