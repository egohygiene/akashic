## Conflicts, exclusions, and review gates

- Current Mass.gov and consolidated address-notice statutes say electronic reporting. Danvers still reproduces older certified-mail notices to FRB plus original/current police departments. The guide exposes this conflict and routes clarification; it does not prescribe both as a new statewide rule, calculate compliance, or promise immunity for late reporting.
- The Firearms Services gateway retains a resident-alien-permit description that conflicts with newer Guidance #5. Its general description is used only for agency navigation; the chapter does not repeat that eligibility/type claim.
- Some Mass.gov links still target legacy MIRCS login hosts despite Unified Gun Portal labels. Link the maintained Mass.gov task page instead of hard-coding an authentication endpoint.
- A printed expiration, application receipt, unchanged-address card, transaction record, and agency validity decision are separate. Do not describe the application-status service as a definitive validity lookup. Timely-renewal statutory conditions and adverse notices require qualified review.
- The general-looking `/how-to/check-a-professional-license` page currently describes landscape architects; it was excluded as a universal route. Older ePLACE/wallet-delivery descriptions do not supersede current eLIPSE instructions for migrated boards.
- No firearms usage/handling, transaction execution, inventories, storage-location examples, personalized eligibility, statewide replacement guarantee, professional reciprocity, nationwide rule matrix, automated applications, or private case management is added. Exhaustive municipal/profession coverage remains deferred.

**Before promoting legal truth review:** a qualified human should check the firearms statements against then-current consolidated law, EOPSS guidance, relevant court orders, channel conflicts, resident/nonresident transitions, late reporting, renewal conditions and card-replacement limitations. A maintainer must record an actual review date only after that review; an agent passing tests cannot supply it. Every card visibly retains pending human review, and the page states pending legal review.
