# Massachusetts and federal licenses and permits

Find the responsible authority → distinguish the transaction → verify the actual result. This is public civic navigation, not legal advice, a status checker, or an application service. It does not determine anyone's eligibility, rights, liability, or permission to possess, carry, transfer, transport, or conduct a regulated activity.

**Bounded first edition. Sources checked: 2026-10-17. Human and legal review: pending. Review due: 2026-10-27, or earlier for a changed law, court order, form, fee, portal, or conflicting instruction.** Source verification is an agent's public-source check, not agency confirmation of a person's status. Legal effective dates and unresolved points are recorded in the [curation record](./ma-licenses-permits-curation.md). The firearms FAQ announces registration/serialization changes beginning **2026-10-28**; that is a review trigger, not a transaction guide here. [Current DCJIS announcements](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/firearms-license-and-transaction-frequently-asked-questions)

**Privacy:** Give identifying information only to the agency through its verified channel. Never enter or publish names, home addresses, license numbers, firearms inventories, storage locations, forms, or case histories here. Keep receipts and correspondence privately. Optional progress marks reset on reload and do not establish agency approval or legal status.

## Keep five questions separate

| Question | What must be checked |
| --- | --- |
| Agency record status | Did the correct agency receive and apply the requested change? A receipt proves a filing checkpoint, not that every record agrees. |
| Physical card printing | Was a replacement actually requested/issued? An address update need not print a new card. |
| License validity | What do the governing law, current agency record, and any actual notice say? An old-address card, printed expiration, or receipt alone does not settle the answer. |
| Firearm transaction records | Acquisition, disposal, registration, and serialization reporting have their own rules; they are not the license-address transaction. Use [current DCJIS guidance](https://www.mass.gov/firearms-services). |
| Jurisdictional authority | Which state's and federal rules concern the particular residence, activity, place, and date? A Massachusetts license does not determine New Hampshire legality. |

## Jurisdiction boundaries

These are questions for the relevant agency or a qualified attorney, not universal permission rules. [Massachusetts Firearms Services](https://www.mass.gov/firearms-services), [New Hampshire State Police Permits and Licensing](https://www.nhsp.dos.nh.gov/our-services/justice-information-bureau/permits-and-licensing/pistol-and-revolver-licensing), and [ATF firearms questions](https://www.atf.gov/firearms/questions-and-answers) provide separate starting points.

| Situation | Verify separately before relying on a credential |
| --- | --- |
| Interstate residence or a move out of Massachusetts | Ask FRB/current municipal authority about the Massachusetts record and resident/nonresident route; ask the other state's authority about its requirements. Federal residence for transactions is a separate question under [27 CFR 478.11](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/chapter-II/subchapter-B/part-478/section-478.11). |
| Possession or storage in another state | Ask about that state's possession, prohibited-person, location, firearm-type, and storage requirements. An LTC/FID record inquiry or a Massachusetts storage rule does not answer them. |
| Sale, gift, inheritance, or another transfer | Check both states and federal transfer/residency rules; ask ATF or a qualified attorney whether a licensed dealer or another authorized route is required. Do not assume private transfers share one nationwide process. [ATF questions](https://www.atf.gov/firearms/questions-and-answers) |
| Transport through or between states | Check origin, destination, transit jurisdictions, and federal law separately. [18 USC 926A](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t18/s926A/) has conditions; it is not a blanket carry or possession authorization. This guide does not evaluate whether they are met. |

New Hampshire's [RSA 159:6](https://gc.nh.gov/rsa/html/XII/159/159-6.htm) displays both current provisions and a version effective **2027-01-01**. Verify the version for the relevant date; this chapter makes no New Hampshire carry-eligibility conclusion. Court orders and later amendments can change the analysis. If a deadline is overdue or records conflict, seek the responsible agency and qualified legal counsel promptly; no promise of immunity or automatic cure is made.
