# U.S. identity and civic paperwork

Find the responsible office → choose the correct transaction → keep a useful confirmation trail. A record update, an application receipt, and a newly issued document are different checkpoints.

**U.S. first edition. Sources checked: 2026-09-27. Human review: pending. Review due: 2026-10-27, or sooner after a fee, form, channel, eligibility, or source conflict changes.** These are discovery routes, not a nationwide eligibility or deadline determination. Vital records follow the issuing jurisdiction; election rules follow the responsible state or territory. Federal agencies have their own age, citizenship/status, evidence, and channel requirements. Confirm the current official page before acting.

**Privacy:** Submit information yourself through the agency's verified channel. Do not enter or publish names, dates of birth, addresses, SSNs, passport numbers, account details, or document images here. Optional progress marks are memory-only reminders and reset on reload. Keep confirmations privately; marking Done does not establish approval, a valid credential, or voter registration.

## Use the cards

Read the first check before ordering anything. A prerequisite link means check whether that evidence is needed; it does not require everyone to buy a birth or marriage certificate. Fees and timing are linked to their current official source. Where no common amount or deadline exists, ask the actual office.

For passports, [State Department support](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/contact-support/us-passports.html) routes urgent travel and overseas cases; [accessibility guidance](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/apply/unique-needs/disability.html) explains how to request accommodations. [SSA contact options](https://www.ssa.gov/agency/contact/) include telephone, local offices, interpreter services, and disability support. For vital records and elections, call the office found through the official directory to confirm an accessible offline route.

For driver/state ID and moving tasks, use the [Massachusetts RMV chapter](./ma-rmv.md), also available as the [RMV portal](https://akashic.egohygiene.io/civic.html). Existing [passport travel resources](../../lists/travel-and-mobility/README.md), [Vote.gov and civic participation](../../lists/awesome-abundance/culture-travel-and-participation/README.md#volunteering-and-civic-participation), and [election offices and legal help](../../lists/legal-help-and-law/README.md#voting-government-records-and-public-accountability) retain their canonical homes. After a death, see [certificates and survivor benefits](../../lists/public-services-and-support/README.md#after-a-death-certificates-and-survivor-benefits). [Benefits](../../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#start-here), [taxes](../../lists/public-services-and-support/README.md#debt-finance-and-taxes), and [court/legal routes](../../lists/legal-help-and-law/README.md) have separate requirements. Jury duty, professional licensing, public-record requests, and immigration applications are outside this chapter.

## Replace birth certificate

| Check | Action |
| --- | --- |
| First check | Ask the receiving agency whether a certified copy is needed; identify the birthplace, not just where you live. |
| Record change | Ordering a copy does not correct the underlying record. Ask the issuing office about an error before ordering. [Official instructions](https://www.usa.gov/birth-certificate) |
| New document | Request the certified version your recipient accepts. U.S. citizens born abroad may need a consular birth record instead. [Official instructions](https://www.usa.gov/birth-certificate) |
| Where and how | Find the issuing office; online, mail, and in-person options vary. Call about alternatives if online access fails. [CDC directory](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/w2w/index.htm) |
| Bring | Confirm requester eligibility, identity proof, and event details. If all ID is lost, ask about that office's alternative verification. [Official instructions](https://www.usa.gov/birth-certificate) |
| Cost | No national price: verify copy, search, and delivery charges with the office before paying. [Request guidelines](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/w2w/guidelines.htm) |
| Timing | No national turnaround: ask the office about processing, delivery, and any urgent option. [Issuing-office directory](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/w2w/index.htm) |
| Interim proof | An order receipt is not a certified birth record; ask the receiving agency what it accepts while waiting. [Official instructions](https://www.usa.gov/birth-certificate) |
| Done when | The requested certified copy arrives, its details are checked, and any returned originals are accounted for. |
| If blocked | Contact the issuing office about missing, restricted, or inaccurate records and accessible request methods. [Find the office](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/w2w/index.htm) |

## Order family vital records

| Check | Action |
| --- | --- |
| First check | Ask which document the recipient needs and who is allowed to request it. Choose the jurisdiction where the event occurred. |
| Record change | A copy request does not amend the event record. A marriage license authorizes marriage; the certificate records it. [Official instructions](https://www.usa.gov/marriage-certificate) |
| New document | A divorce decree contains the court's orders; a divorce certificate confirms the event. [Official instructions](https://www.usa.gov/divorce-decree) [Death-certificate copies](https://www.usa.gov/death-certificate) |
| Where and how | Use the event's vital-record office for certificates; contact the court clerk for a divorce decree. Verify available channels. [Official instructions](https://www.usa.gov/divorce-decree) [Directory](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/w2w/index.htm) |
| Bring | Check identity, relationship/requester eligibility, event details, and purpose; restrictions differ. [Death-record requirements](https://www.usa.gov/death-certificate) |
| Cost | Copy/search/delivery fees vary; confirm the current fee and payment method with the issuing office. [Request guidelines](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/w2w/guidelines.htm) |
| Timing | Ask that office for processing and delivery estimates; no nationwide deadline or expedited service is assumed. [Directory](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/w2w/index.htm) |
| Interim proof | Check whether the recipient needs a certified copy, an ordinary copy, or the decree itself; a receipt is not the record. [Official instructions](https://www.usa.gov/divorce-decree) |
| Done when | The correct document arrives and is accepted for its intended purpose. Track each copy and any originals privately. |
| If blocked | Missing or restricted certificate: contact the issuing office; wrong decree: contact the court clerk. Foreign events may need a consular/foreign route. [Official instructions](https://www.usa.gov/divorce-decree) [Marriage abroad](https://www.usa.gov/marriage-certificate) |

| Done when | Loss/nonreceipt is handled through the correct route, the replacement arrives, and returned evidence is accounted for. |
| If blocked | For nonreceipt, urgent travel, or being abroad, use the relevant official support route promptly. [Official instructions](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/contact-support/us-passports.html) [Nonreceipt instructions](https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/renew-replace/report-passport-lost-stolen.html) |

| --- | --- |
| First check | Choose the responsible election jurisdiction and check before its deadline. This guide does not determine your eligibility. |

| New document | A registration card and voting ID are different. A voter-registration card is not itself required to vote. [Official instructions](https://vote.gov/guide-to-voting) |

| Bring | Provide only the details requested by the official lookup or office, through its private channel. Check name, address, status, and party where used. [Official instructions](https://www.usa.gov/confirm-voter-registration) |
| Cost | The public discovery pages are free to read. Ask the office about any separate document or mailing costs; do not buy a third-party status report. [Official instructions](https://www.usa.gov/state-election-office) |
| Timing | Check early enough to address errors before the applicable registration deadline; no national deadline applies to every voter. [Official instructions](https://www.usa.gov/confirm-voter-registration) |

## Update voter registration

| Check | Action |
| --- | --- |
| First check | Check status first, then the jurisdiction's eligibility, deadline, and accepted method. Moving to another state generally means registering there. |
| Record change | Requests a registration/name/address/party update as permitted by the jurisdiction; it does not update other agencies. [Official instructions](https://www.usa.gov/change-voter-registration) |
| New document | Registration confirmation is separate from a voter card or voting-ID requirement. Confirm the actual record after processing. [Official instructions](https://vote.gov/register) |
| Where and how | Select your state/territory for supported online, mail, or in-person routes; phone can help identify the right process. Military/overseas voters have a separate route. [Official instructions](https://vote.gov/register) [Office help](https://www.usa.gov/state-election-office) |
| Bring | Evidence and forms vary. Ask about accepted alternatives if you lack the usual ID; do not assume one national form works everywhere. [Official instructions](https://www.usa.gov/register-to-vote) [State instructions](https://vote.gov/register) |
| Cost | Official form downloads are free; an accepted mailed form needs postage. Ask the office about separate costs for any supporting documents. [Mail-form instructions](https://www.eac.gov/voters/national-mail-voter-registration-form) |
| Timing | Verify the deadline and whether it concerns receipt, postmark, or an online cutoff. Processing is not instant; recheck status before the deadline. [Official instructions](https://www.usa.gov/change-voter-registration) [Check status](https://www.usa.gov/confirm-voter-registration) |
| Interim proof | A submission or mailing receipt is not confirmation of registration. Contact the office if confirmation or the expected record update is missing. [Official instructions](https://www.usa.gov/confirm-voter-registration) |
| Done when | The election office confirms the intended registration/update; save its confirmation privately and check where/how to vote. |
| If blocked | Use the election office for a rejected request, deadline question, access need, or correction. Check old-state cancellation separately if relevant. [Official instructions](https://www.usa.gov/state-election-office) [Move/name guidance](https://www.usa.gov/change-voter-registration) |
