# If you're broke: choose one next step

**Need food or somewhere safe tonight?** Call **211**, or use the existing [211, emergency food, and shelter routes](../lists/public-services-and-support/README.md#benefits-and-emergency-assistance). Say your ZIP code and what cannot wait. For food, try the [community food finders](../lists/awesome-abundance/community-sharing-and-material-resources/README.md#food-and-household-essentials); ask for a prepared meal if you cannot safely cook or store groceries. Confirm the actual location, current intake, access, and transport before travelling when possible. A referral does not reserve food, a bed, or money. For immediate physical danger or a medical emergency, contact emergency services when safe.

You do not need to complete this guide. Pick the need with the earliest consequence, make one contact, and write down the next step. Food today and an application for longer-term help can happen in parallel.

**United States first edition, with a Massachusetts fork. Sources checked October 17, 2026; human review pending.** Programs differ by state, tribal or territorial administration, locality, household, funding, and case facts. Recheck eligibility, intake, costs, and deadlines when you use a route. ## Seven needs, one first contact

| What cannot wait? | Start here | Ask before relying on it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Food | [Food access to meals](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#food-access-to-meals): pantries and community meals now; SNAP, WIC, school, or older-adult programs where applicable. | “Who is serving my area today? Groceries or a prepared meal? What are the pickup, delivery, allergy, and storage limits?” Local intake varies; a benefits application is not food for today. |
| Safe housing | [Emergency shelter](../lists/public-services-and-support/README.md#benefits-and-emergency-assistance) or [housing and rent help](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#housing-and-rent). | “Do you serve my address and household? Is intake open? What can help before tonight or this notice's deadline?” Shelter, rental assistance, housing searches, and legal defense are different routes. |
| Utilities | Call the verified number on the bill; use [energy assistance and shutoff help](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#utilities-and-communications). | “What is due, when could service stop, and what hardship or assistance route applies? Does any protection apply, and can you confirm it in writing?” A repayment plan is not a grant; applying does not automatically stop disconnection. |
| Health and prescriptions | [Coverage, health centers, and medical-bill help](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#health-coverage-and-medical-bills); [prescription assistance](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#prescriptions). | “What care or medicine can I access, what will I owe, and who can help apply?” Health centers may charge on a sliding scale; hospital financial assistance differs from financing. Ask the pharmacist or prescriber about an access problem before supplies run out. |
| Cash and income | [Benefits screening](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#benefits-screening), [income assistance](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#income-and-employment), and [public career support](../lists/work-and-learning/README.md#career-research-and-public-support). | “Which program fits my household and work history? What proof, ongoing reporting, and decision steps apply?” Unemployment, family cash assistance, SSI, and SSDI have separate rules. An application, interview, job offer, or possible refund is not money received. |
| Legal deadlines | [Find qualified legal help](../lists/legal-help-and-law/README.md#start-here); keep the actual notice and envelope. | “What is the earliest deadline, and can someone advise or represent me before it?” Intake, complaints, negotiations, and aid requests generally do not pause other deadlines. Court procedural help and a lawyer's representation are different services. |
| Transport, phone, and internet | [Local ride routes](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#transportation), [Lifeline](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#utilities-and-communications), and [library access](../lists/awesome-abundance/libraries-and-local-access/README.md#find-libraries-and-materials). | “Can I reach the appointment or complete this application? What fare, booking, eligibility, device, or remaining service charge applies?” A discount is not necessarily free service. Ask about accessible rides, a phone application, or a person who can help. |

For a small paid engagement using skills you already have, use the [remote paid-work navigator](remote-paid-work.md). It separates application and screening effort from assigned work and payment received; keep using assistance routes while potential income is uncertain.

Outside Massachusetts, follow the state or local administrator from these collections. A national directory does not process your case or prove that a program is available where you live.

## Today, this week, and later

These are planning prompts, not program deadlines. A notice, hearing, medicine supply, or unsafe situation can require action sooner.

| When | A manageable action | What to keep privately |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Today | Address food, safe shelter, urgent care, and the earliest notice. Choose one route above; call if the online form is inaccessible. Tell the worker the real deadline and ask what can happen before it. | The notice, provider's name, contact date, answer, next action, and any actual deadline. |
| This week | Submit the applications that fit, complete interviews or requested proofs, and follow up through the right office. Ask existing providers or creditors about hardship terms. Confirm transport and a safe way to receive calls or mail. | Application receipt, missing items, written terms, who decides, and the follow-up date. |
| Later, once essentials are steadier | Check renewals, income-change reporting, affordable coverage, tax help, and a realistic work or training plan. Revisit the budget when an award or payment actually arrives. | Renewal and reporting dates, the first usable payment or service date, and any balance still owed. |

**A short script:** “I live in [town or ZIP]. I need help with [one need] before [date or event]. Do you serve this area and situation? Is the help free, discounted, or repayable? What is the next step, what information is essential, and what alternatives are accepted if I cannot provide it? Can someone help by phone or in person? If you cannot help, who should I contact next?”

Ask about language assistance, disability accommodations, a safe callback method, and help completing forms. A library worker, benefits assister, caseworker, school office, or trusted helper may help you reach the correct program; that person does not automatically become an authorized representative or legal adviser. Do not give a helper your password or one-time security code.

| --- | --- | --- |

Household, income, assets where applicable, and other program rules matter; unemployment ending alone does not establish eligibility. For a cash application by phone, contact your local DTA office. Ask about required proofs and accepted alternatives. |
| An existing SNAP application | Use the [SNAP interview, proofs, and DTA Connect follow-through checklist](massachusetts-snap-follow-through.md). Locate the existing case and notices before automatically applying again. | An upload receipt is not a decision. Confirm interviews, outstanding proofs, submission, reporting, and recertification through DTA; ask about expedited screening when appropriate. Use food-today routes while waiting. |
| Rent, housing loss, or a housing-cost emergency | Use [RAFT and housing assistance](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#housing-and-rent); contact the regional administering agency serving your address. | Coverage, treatment, or medicines | Use the [Massachusetts coverage transition guide](massachusetts-health-coverage-transition.md) and [MassHealth application help](../atlas/places/us-ma.md#benefits-food-and-housing). | Work, training, or unemployment | Contact [MassHire](../lists/work-and-learning/README.md#career-research-and-public-support) for free career services and a person to help. | Getting there and staying connected | Use [RideMatch, MassOptions, and local finders](../atlas/places/us-ma.md#start-here-statewide-finders), [Massachusetts public transportation](../atlas/places/us-ma.md#work-learning-and-getting-around), and your library. Ask MassHealth or the plan about covered medical transportation if relevant. | Confirm service area, fare or reduced-fare eligibility, accessibility, advance booking, and trip purpose. Library computers, Wi-Fi, printing, hotspots, and device loans differ by branch; ask what is available and what costs remain. |

For tax preparation, use [VITA/TCE and Massachusetts tax-help routes](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#taxes-and-legal-aid) and the [Atlas tax-help entry](../atlas/places/us-ma.md#benefits-food-and-housing). Eligible basic preparation can be free, but appointment availability and return scope vary. An IRS dispute or tax hardship needs a different route, such as a Low Income Taxpayer Clinic or the Taxpayer Advocate Service; tax preparation does not itself resolve collection or appeal issues.

## Legal help: choose the kind of help

**A notice or hearing comes first.** Keep every page and envelope, record the earliest possible deadline, and tell legal intake about it immediately. Ask the issuing court or agency about its official procedure and qualified legal help about your options. Do not wait for a callback, aid decision, complaint outcome, or settlement discussion to protect a deadline. This guide does not calculate one for you.

**Across the United States:** use the existing [civil legal-aid finders and intake guide](../lists/legal-help-and-law/README.md#start-here) to locate help for your state and issue. Ask the actual provider about phone or in-person intake. The Massachusetts routes below are a local fork; their service areas and rules do not apply nationwide.

| What you need | Existing route | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal information | The [Legal Help guide](../lists/legal-help-and-law/README.md#start-here), or [Mass Legal Help](../lists/awesome-abundance/public-programs-and-services/README.md#massachusetts) for Massachusetts. | Public explanations help you understand a problem and find a next contact. Reading a page or using an information site's contact form does not obtain individual advice or representation. |
| Civil legal advice or possible representation | [Massachusetts Legal Resource Finder](../atlas/places/us-ma.md#start-here-statewide-finders). For practical information, use [Mass Legal Help](../lists/awesome-abundance/public-programs-and-services/README.md#massachusetts). | Legal aid screens geography, subject, household and financial rules, conflicts, priorities, and capacity. Free civil help is not available for every case; submitting intake is not acceptance. |
| A person or an alternative to the finder | [MLAC's regional and subject-specific directory](https://mlac.org/help/) links the legal-aid organizations serving Massachusetts. Use the provider's current intake contact. | Greater Boston, MetroWest, Northeast, central/western, and southeast/Cape/Islands providers have different service areas and subjects. Town, age, issue, and program exceptions can matter more than a county label. MLAC itself does not give advice or representation. |
| Forms and court procedure | [Legal Assistance for Court Users](../atlas/places/us-ma.md#government-legal-and-civic-life), including Court Service Centers, law libraries, and any applicable Lawyer for the Day program. | Court information and form help do not establish representation or supply legal strategy. Confirm the particular case types, service route, accessibility, and any clinic eligibility. |
| Brief, reduced-fee, or limited assistance | Use the alternatives linked by [MassLegalHelp's Find a Lawyer guide](https://www.masslegalhelp.org/find-lawyer); the [Massachusetts Bar referral service](https://www.masslawhelp.com/) offers a phone route. | The bar referral itself is free, but referred private lawyers charge and may charge for the first consultation. Reduced fees depend on eligibility and subject. Online volunteer advice and clinics have their own rules and capacity. |
| Criminal defense or another covered appointed-counsel matter | Ask the court about appointed counsel and the [Committee for Public Counsel Services](https://www.publiccounsel.net/). | This is separate from ordinary civil legal aid. Appointment depends on the proceeding and eligibility; it is not a general free lawyer for every legal problem. |

Before intake, have a short factual timeline, the other parties' names for conflict screening, court or agency notices, hearing dates, and the requested household income or asset information available. Ask what is required and how to send it securely; do not delay the first urgent contact while trying to assemble a perfect packet.

Ask: “Can you provide information, brief advice, document review, limited representation, or the whole case? Has someone accepted responsibility, what exactly will they do, and what must I still file, serve, attend, or pay?” Get the agreed scope, fees, expenses, and responsibilities in writing. A lawyer helping with one task does not automatically handle the rest of the case.

If declined, ask whether the reason is geography, income, subject, conflict, capacity, or missing information, and ask for a suitable referral. Check another applicable program, a clinic or brief-advice route, conditional reduced-fee help, or court procedural assistance. Ask about a provider's review process if appropriate. A decline from one program does not establish that every route is closed, and a referral does not protect the deadline or guarantee acceptance.

## What is free, and what still costs money?

Free information, an application, a referral, an approved award, and money actually received are different things. Ask about remaining charges before agreeing to a service.

Ask: “What will I owe now or later? Could interest, fees, repayment, or recovery of an overpayment apply? What changes must I report, and what happens at renewal?” Get the provider's actual terms in writing rather than assuming every assistance program has the same conditions. Zero income alone does not establish eligibility.

- Assistance can be food, restricted benefits, coverage, a payment to a provider, or a service. Keep its purpose and restrictions separate from cash.
- Discounts, sliding scales, and conditional reduced fees can leave a balance. Check actual prices, appointments, transport, documents, and renewal.
- A hardship plan or deferral changes payment terms. Ask about interest, fees, collections, credit reporting, the later balance, and written approval. Use the existing [debt-hardship guide](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#debt-and-payment-hardship) or [credit-card hardship workflow](credit-card-hardship.md).
- Use [library and local access](../lists/awesome-abundance/libraries-and-local-access/README.md), [community supplies](../lists/awesome-abundance/community-sharing-and-material-resources/README.md), and [digital access help](digital-access.md) to reduce practical barriers. Confirm local availability; avoid paying for a course, device, or subscription merely because an aid or job lead suggests it.

Use an agency's own application or independently verified provider. Guaranteed grants, paid access to government applications, gift-card or cryptocurrency demands, and pressure to disclose credentials are warning signs. Ask a trusted navigator to help verify a confusing message.

## Keep pending help out of today's money

These are private planning labels, not official agency status codes:

| Label | What to record | Count as available today? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Received / available | The money or benefit actually arrived and is usable, or a provider payment actually posted. Record restrictions and what expense it covers. | Only what is available for the particular expense. SNAP is not unrestricted cash; do not count a landlord payment again as cash income. |
| Pending | Applied, interview or proofs outstanding, awaiting a decision, or approved for a future payment not yet received. Keep the precise stage, evidence, conditions, and expected date. | No. An approval can support a separate forecast; check that the payment or service actually arrives. |
| Possible | A lead, preliminary screen, potential job, or program not yet verified. | No. Record one next contact rather than an estimated award. |
| Not eligible / declined | The provider's decision and reason, date, and any correction or review instructions. Distinguish ineligibility from capacity or a closed intake. | No. Track an appeal or a new referral separately as pending or possible. |

## Private checklist

Copy this into a private note or print it. **No account or upload is required to use this worksheet.** It is a blank planning aid, not an application. Do not put a completed copy, real notices, case identifiers, financial records, or medical information in public issues or repositories. On a shared computer, follow the [digital-access privacy steps](digital-access.md).

TODAY'S MONEY

When the next action is done, pause and reassess the most urgent remaining need. The broader [Financial Relief and Assistance collection](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#start-here) keeps the canonical resources and detailed workflows together.
