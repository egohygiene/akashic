# Emergency navigation: ownership and source notes

Issue [#150](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/150) adds a United States first-edition [before / during / after guide](emergency-preparedness-and-recovery.md), [printable essentials checklist](emergency-essentials-checklist.md), and a focused [Public Services section](../lists/public-services-and-support/README.md#emergency-preparedness-and-disaster-recovery). Sources were checked **2026-09-23**. This is general orientation, not a current incident report or a verification of a local shelter, road, organization, or household's eligibility. Recheck every event-specific fact against the responsible current authority.

## Access, freshness, and exclusions

- Six new Public Services records have explicit stable IDs, United States scope, access and account fields, monthly review cadence, emergency sensitivity, and privacy/financial sensitivity where applicable. Free information does not guarantee free equipment, repairs, insurance, or aid. The moved Ready.gov gateway retains its derived ID explicitly; there are no changed URLs requiring aliases.
- Public Services rises from **115 to 121** resources; the overall catalog from **5,361 to 5,367**. Cross-linked lists keep their counts. Confirm these parsed counts with the validator and generated catalog before release.
- The checklist is source-controlled blank Markdown with semantic headings and checkboxes; a reader can print it and fill a private copy. No personal contact information, address, medical record, photograph, insurer claim, or event-specific status is collected or published.
- Excluded: a live alert/forecast map, rescue or clinical directions, fixed evacuation routes, unverified shelter inventory, local provider directory, national duplicate application catalog, automatic outreach, unsupported disaster eligibility or grant promise, and blanket endorsement of mutual aid. Use current local instructions during an event and current program notices during recovery.
- Validate the affected lists with the pinned Awesome Lint wrapper, the collection validator, Node tests, site build and generated-site checks, link/anchor checks, ID preservation, and payload budgets. Generated `dist/` remains uncommitted.
