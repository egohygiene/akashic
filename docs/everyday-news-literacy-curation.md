Issue [#147](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/147), under [#40](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/40), extends the collection created by completed [#59](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/59). ## Public source verification

Sources were opened/read on **2026-10-17**. This is agent source verification, not human truth review; no `reviewed` dates are added. No accounts, extension installations, uploaded images, paid subscriptions, outreach or submissions were tested. The synthetic library example is wholly invented, contains no real person/library/claim or live evidence URL, and demonstrates a bounded conclusion rather than an accusation.

## Exclusions, counts and validation

No general news aggregator, political outlet recommendation list, partisan ranking, universal trust score, paywall bypass, detector leaderboard, surveillance workflow, private-case data, or duplicate library/tool directory. Lack of disclosure/search results is recorded as uncertainty; authorship and content truth remain separate questions. A short check may end in withholding sharing or asking a librarian/expert for help.

Two new records raise Claims from **53 to 55** and the whole catalog from **5,378 to 5,380**. All 36 collections, 483 topic paths and 147 Atlas resources remain. No resource moves between collections; the one URL migration preserves its ID and prior alias. Human review remains pending.
