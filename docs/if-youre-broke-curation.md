# If you're broke: source and overlap audit

Issue [#194](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/194) adds a short route through existing help for people with no or low income. [Financial Relief and Assistance](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md) remains the canonical home for assistance navigation, with its short first-minute entry linking the fuller [If you're broke guide](if-youre-broke.md). Both are canonical Markdown; the fuller guide connects existing resources rather than duplicating their catalog records. This is a United States first edition with an explicitly separate Massachusetts lane; neither a U.S. label nor residence establishes eligibility or available funding.

The navigator should let a reader choose one next action without completing a benefits inventory. Immediate food, a place to stay, essential care, and a notice deadline come before a longer application list. Information, referrals, direct assistance, discounts, and payment arrangements remain distinct. An application, referral, appeal request, or message does not automatically reserve help, create representation, pause a deadline, or provide spendable cash.

## One catalog addition and stable identity

**Massachusetts RAFT is the one distinct resource gap.** Existing housing, CHAMP, affordable-rental, Community Action, and DTA routes do not replace the statewide emergency housing-payment application. Its canonical card belongs in Financial Relief's [Housing and Rent](../lists/financial-relief-and-assistance/README.md#housing-and-rent), with permanent ID **`massachusetts-raft-housing-assistance`** and canonical URL `https://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-for-raft-emergency-help-for-housing-costs`.

Use Massachusetts geography, free application information, eligibility-based assistance, high volatility, monthly review, and financial/legal/privacy sensitivity. `programChecked` and `linkChecked` record **2026-10-17**; canonical direct retrieval was blocked, so `linkStatus` is **`unknown`**, not `ok`. No human `reviewed` date is inferred. A canonical official destination can be retained while readable administrators corroborate its purpose and accessible local route.

With one addition, Financial Relief changes **58 → 59** and the total **5,395 → 5,396**. The 37 collection identities remain unchanged. No existing resource is moved, renamed, or assigned a new ID; there are no URL migrations or aliases to invent. Other canonical counts remain Public Services 132, Legal Help 349, Housing 24, Work 195, and Abundance 884.

The audit also found an existing TAFDC primary citation in the unemployment-exhaustion guide. EAEDC was already named through DTA navigation. Reuse those routes rather than add a second generic cash-assistance directory. General transportation already routes to transit, 211, Eldercare, and Medicaid; Massachusetts transport examples can link supporting official evidence without duplicating RideMatch or the existing state transport gateway.

## Evidence method and date limits

Public primary sources were checked **2026-10-17**. “Read” below means readable public content was inspected; “indexed” means the official source's search-index text was available while direct retrieval failed; “blocked” or “no extracted content” does not establish a dead link. Source publication, review, and update dates are shown separately when visible. The check date does not refresh untouched resource metadata or establish human review.

No household details, accounts, applications, phone calls, appointments, local search results, agency submissions, document uploads, or provider case workflows were exercised. Public reading verifies limited routing claims, not local capacity, funds, appointment availability, complete accessibility, or an individual's eligibility. Exact income limits, annual thresholds, benefit amounts, processing-time promises, and changing office hours are intentionally omitted.
