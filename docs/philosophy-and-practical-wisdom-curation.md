# Philosophy and practical wisdom: curation decisions

Issue [#157](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/157) adds an English-language first edition for philosophical inquiry in ordinary life, alongside primary texts and scholarship. These canonical-home and overlap decisions were recorded before implementing the collection.

## Canonical home and migration map

A standalone [Philosophy and Practical Wisdom](../lists/philosophy-and-practical-wisdom/README.md) collection is justified by its coherent work: evaluating arguments, clarifying values, comparing philosophical positions, and reading texts in context. It is distinct from empirical source verification, religious practice, clinical care, and general research tooling. Focus on a small set of references, courses, editions, and accessible introductions rather than another general reading directory.

## Source and editorial policy

Prefer expert-edited references, university/open textbooks and courseware, scholarly editions, named translators/editors, and accountable public scholarship. Read the linked content and its rights/access pages. Separate primary text, adaptation, historical interpretation, contemporary commentary, and empirical evidence. A university host does not make every claim settled; philosophical positions and classifications are contested.

Tradition names describe diverse, internally contested fields, not interchangeable practices or a single communal worldview. Include scholarly routes beyond a Western survey, name the scope and mediation of translations, and retain community-specific context. Do not present a brief guide as representative of every tradition or living philosopher.

Exclude quotation compilations, undisclosed commercial coaching, promises that a school of thought will fix someone's life, duplicated library portals, copied primary texts, clinical recommendations, and another broad research catalog. Paid books and memberships may be optional downstream paths, but distinguish them from the linked free reading/course materials. Record item-specific reuse limits; free access alone is not an open license.

Primary source checks are dated separately from human `reviewed`. Human review remains pending. Recheck moved destinations, access terms, course availability, and revised translations sooner than the scheduled review.
