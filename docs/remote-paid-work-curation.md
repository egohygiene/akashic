# Remote paid work: source and overlap audit

Issue [#195](https://github.com/egohygiene/akashic/issues/195) adds a bounded route for technically experienced readers seeking paid contribution during an income disruption. [Work and Learning](../lists/work-and-learning/README.md) owns its compact portal guide and the new Remote Paid Work and Expert Services section; the fuller [remote paid-work guide](remote-paid-work.md) owns the decision path, comparison, anti-spec-work filter, starter services, and blank private effort/payout tracker. Public information about an application is not an available assignment, accepted contract, booked session, or spendable income.

## Canonical ownership and overlap

The pre-change collection has **5,396 resources across 37 top-level collections**; Work and Learning has **195**, and Public Services has **132**. Title, domain, canonical URL variants, organizations, and existing guide searches found seven distinct gaps. Keep the existing research panels in their current home and link them from the work guide.

## Evidence method and freshness

Public primary pages were inspected by the research team on **2026-10-17**. Publisher dates below remain distinct from this agent check. **Human truth review is pending; monthly review is next due 2026-11-17.** No account creation, sign-in, application, assessment, interview, screening answers, external message, identity upload, contract acceptance, booking, actual work, or payout was tested. A public apply button only verifies that entry point was visible.

The 16 evaluation fields are explicit for each selected resource. “Unknown” means the inspected public evidence did not establish the fact; it does not mean a restriction or cost is absent. Classification records the provider's stated arrangement, not a legal determination. Public rate advertising is not comparable realized net earnings; the reader guide does not freeze transient numeric rates. Payout processing, funds becoming available, and bank/provider arrival are separate events.

Controlled metadata describes worker entry: `eligibility-based` does not promise admission, and verified free or freemium profile options do not waive service, proposal, payment-provider or withdrawal fees. Broad `global` service scope does not establish eligibility in every country; unresolved country lists remain explicit below. No new compensation or payout schema is introduced; the existing fields and descriptions link to these dated evaluations.
